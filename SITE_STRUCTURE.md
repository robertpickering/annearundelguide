## Key Verified Facts

### Anne Arundel County
- **Population:** 587,901 (2020 Census)
- **Area:** 608 square miles
- **Founded:** 1637 (as Patuxent Patent), 1695 (as Anne Arundel County)
- **County Seat:** Annapolis
- **County Executive:** Steuart Pittman

### Annapolis
- **Population:** ~41,000 (city proper)
- **Founded:** 1649
- **Notable:** U.S. Naval Academy, Maryland State House
- **Known as:** America's Sailing Capital
- **Address:** Maryland State House, 100 State Circle, Annapolis, MD 21401

### Severna Park
- **Founded:** 1920s as planned community
- **Schools:** Severna Park High School ( nationally recognized)
- **Notable:** B&A Trail, Severna Park Community Center

### Crofton
- **Founded:** 1960s as master-planned community
- **Developer:** William Greenberg
- **Known as:** "The Community by the Bay"
- **Amenities:** Multiple lakes, recreation centers

### Pasadena
- **Founded:** 1741
- **Known for:** Crab Island Beach, Maryland Point Park
- **Waterfront:** Severn River access

### Glen Burnie
- **Founded:** 1700s
- **Known for:** Mall at Columbia, commercial hub
- **Transportation:** Major highway access (I-95, MD-295)

### Odenton
- **Founded:** Late 1800s as railroad stop
- **Known for:** Joint Base Annapolis-Lewis proximity
- **Location:** Southern Anne Arundel County

### Severn
- **Founded:** 1600s
- **Known for:** Waterfront communities, Severn River
- **Proximity:** Near BWI Airport
