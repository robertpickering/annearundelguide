# STEP 1 RESEARCH: Annapolis City Page

## VERIFIED FACTS

### History & Founding
**Source:** Annapolis Official Website (annapolis.gov/588/History-of-Annapolis)
- King William's School founded in 1696
- Third oldest college in United States (after Harvard and William & Mary)
- Became St. John's College in 1784
- Maryland designated capital in 1695
- First capitol of United States (1783-1784)

**Source:** Maryland State Archives (msa.maryland.gov)
- Historical chronology of Anne Arundel County
- King William's School established in Annapolis
- St. John's College merged in 1784

### Population Data
**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau
- Population (2020 Census): 40,812
- Increase of 6.3% since 2010
- Moderately sized city with modest growth

**Source:** Annapolis Official Website (annapolis.gov/875/Demographics)
- Diverse racial/ethnic population
- High median household income
- Well-educated community

### Key Landmarks & Attractions
- **U.S. Naval Academy** - America's premier military academy
- **Maryland State House** - Historic capitol building, oldest in continuous use
- **Historic Downtown** - Maryland Avenue shopping district
- **Waterfront & Harbor** - Maritime heritage, sailing culture
- **St. John's College** - Historic institution founded 1696

### Location & Geography
- Situated on Chesapeake Bay
- Mouth of Severn River
- 25 miles south of Baltimore
- 30 miles east of Washington DC
- Part of Baltimore-Washington metropolitan area

### Schools & Education
- Part of Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS)
- Elementary, middle, and high schools serve city residents
- Multiple private school options
- Proximity to higher education institutions
