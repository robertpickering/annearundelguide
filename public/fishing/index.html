Franklin Point State Park

Address: 22000 Point Lookout Road, Plattsburg, MD 20677 Overview Franklin Point State Park is a hidden gem nestled on the Western Shore of the Chesapeake Bay, offering visitors a peaceful retreat with stunning water views and excellent recreational opportunities that feel both special and genuinely accessible. This smaller park provides a more intimate setting for enjoying Maryland’s natural beauty while avoiding the crowds that often diminish the experience at larger, more popular destinations throughout the region. You will discover that this park represents something increasingly rare in developed Maryland because it preserves large areas of intact natural landscape that feel authentically wild and untamed despite being easily accessible from major population centers. The peninsula location and diverse ecosystems make it an ideal destination for outdoor enthusiasts seeking authentic Maryland experiences that connect them to the Chesapeake Bay’s natural heritage. ...

Love Point State Park

Address: 2701 Love Point Road, Stevensville, MD 21666 Overview Love Point State Park is a picturesque waterfront park that offers visitors beautiful views of the Chesapeake Bay and excellent recreational opportunities in a setting that feels both intimate and authentically Maryland. This serene destination provides a perfect setting for fishing, boating, picnicking, and enjoying nature’s beauty along Maryland’s scenic coastline while avoiding the crowds that often diminish the experience at larger, more famous destinations. You will discover that this park punches above its weight despite its smaller size because it combines quality facilities, natural beauty, and recreational variety in ways that satisfy visitors of all ages and interests. The compact setting makes it easy to explore thoroughly in a single visit while still offering enough depth to justify multiple trips as the seasons change and new features become apparent. ...

Sandy Point State Park

Address: 1100 E College Parkway, Annapolis, MD 21409 Overview Sandy Point State Park is a 786-acre recreational paradise stretching along the northwestern shore of the Chesapeake Bay. This beloved destination offers visitors an incredible array of activities while providing stunning views of the iconic Chesapeake Bay Bridge that connects the region. You will find yourself drawn to this special place where Maryland’s natural beauty combines with accessible recreational opportunities for people of all ages. The park serves as a gateway to Chesapeake Bay experiences that many locals consider essential to understanding why this region remains so beloved by visitors year after year. ...
