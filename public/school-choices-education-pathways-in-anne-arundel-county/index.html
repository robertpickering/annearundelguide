School Choices & Education Pathways in Anne Arundel County

Understanding Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS)

District Overview

Anne Arundel County Public Schools serves approximately 55,000+ students across 80+ schools. As one of Maryland’s largest and highest-performing districts, AACPS offers diverse educational options for students from kindergarten through 12th grade.

Key District Facts:

  • Superintendent: Dr. Mark T. Bedell (began term July 1, 2022)
  • Total Students: ~55,000+
  • Total Schools: 80+ (19 high schools, 13 middle schools, 40+ elementary schools, specialty schools)
  • Teacher-Student Ratio: Approximately 1:16
  • Website: www.aacps.org
  • Phone: 410-222-4500
  • Address: 1 County Grounds, Annapolis, MD 21401

District Performance

AACPS consistently ranks among Maryland’s top school districts:

Overall Strengths:

  • High graduation rate (~92%)
  • Strong college readiness programs
  • Extensive special education services
  • Diverse course offerings
  • Robust extracurricular activities

State Performance Metrics:

  • Maryland State Department of Education ratings available online
  • Multiple schools receiving state and national recognition
  • Strong STEM program development
  • Advanced placement and honors course availability

School Performance Metrics

Understanding School Ratings

When evaluating schools, consider these key metrics:

Test Scores & Academic Performance:

  • Maryland Comprehensive Assessment (MCA) scores
  • State standardized testing results
  • National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP)
  • College readiness indicators

Graduation Rates:

  • AACPS average: ~92%
  • Individual school rates vary
  • 4-year vs. 5-6 year graduation rates
  • Cohort tracking available online

College Acceptance Rates:

  • College matriculation reports published annually
  • NCAA Division I, II, III institutions
  • State colleges and universities
  • Private and out-of-state options

Student-Teacher Ratios:

  • Varies by school and grade level
  • Elementary: Typically 1:14-1:16
  • Middle school: 1:15-1:17
  • High school: 1:16-1:18
  • AP/honors classes often smaller

Homeschooling Resources

Maryland Homeschool Requirements:

Legal Requirements:

  1. File Notice of Intent to homeschool with county superintendent
  2. Provide letter of credentials (parent qualifications)
  3. Maintain attendance records
  4. File annual evaluation results

Homeschool Evaluation:

  • Standardized test scores
  • Portfolio review
  • Teacher evaluation
  • Psychoeducational evaluation

Resources for Anne Arundel Homeschoolers:

Anne Arundel County Public Schools - Homeschool Office:

  • Website: www.aacps.org/homeschool
  • Phone: 410-222-4500

Homeschool Organizations:

  • Co-op opportunities

Homeschool Supplies:

School Selection Process

Step 1: Research Your Zone

Public School Assignment:

  • Elementary, middle, and high schools assigned by address
  • Use AACPS school finder tool
  • Verify boundaries before purchasing/renting

Resources:

  • www.aacps.org/schools/findyourschool
  • Contact school district directly
  • Check with real estate agent familiar with school zones

Step 2: Evaluate School Performance

Research Metrics:

  • Test scores and trends
  • Graduation rates
  • College acceptance
  • Teacher qualifications
  • Class sizes
  • Extracurricular offerings

Visit Schools:

  • Attend open houses
  • Schedule tours
  • Meet with principals
  • Talk to current parents
  • Observe classes if possible

Step 3: Consider Your Child’s Needs

Questions to Ask:

  • Does the school match my child’s learning style?
  • Are there appropriate challenge/support levels?
  • How does the school handle behavioral issues?
  • What extracurricular options exist?
  • How does the school communicate with parents?
  • What support services are available?

Step 4: Visit and Compare

During School Visits:

  • Meet with teachers
  • Tour facilities
  • Review curriculum materials
  • Ask about parent involvement
  • Understand school culture
  • Check technology resources

Enrollment Process

Public School Enrollment

Required Documentation:

  1. Proof of Residency:

    • Lease agreement or deed
    • Utility bills (last 30 days)
    • Maryland driver’s license or ID

  2. Child Documentation:

    • Birth certificate
    • Social Security card
    • Immunization records
    • Previous school records
    • Report cards and transcripts
    • IEP or 504 plan (if applicable)

  3. Parent/Guardian:

    • Photo ID
    • Custody documents (if applicable)

Enrollment Steps:

  1. Contact assigned school or district office
  2. Complete enrollment forms
  3. Submit documentation
  4. Schedule health screening (if required)
  5. Attend orientation
  6. Purchase uniforms (if required)
  7. Obtain books/materials

Timeline:

  • Register before school starts if possible
  • Spring/Summer for fall enrollment
  • Rolling enrollment throughout year for transfers

Private School Enrollment

Varies by school, but typically:

  • Application form and fee
  • Entrance exam (some schools)
  • Interview with parent/student
  • Transcripts and records
  • Reference letters
  • Tuition agreement
  • Health records

Application Timelines:

  • Fall enrollment: Apply 6-12 months in advance
  • Rolling admissions (some schools)
  • Waiting lists common for popular schools

Homeschool Registration

Process:

  1. File Notice of Intent with county superintendent
  2. Submit letter of credentials
  3. Maintain required records
  4. Submit annual evaluation

Deadline: Must file before beginning homeschool instruction

Transportation & Busing

School Bus Service

Eligibility:

  • Available for students walking more than specified distance
  • Elementary students: Typically 1.5+ miles
  • Middle/High school: Typically 2+ miles
  • Special education students: As determined by IEP

Service Hours:

  • Morning: 6:30-7:30 AM pickup windows
  • Afternoon: 2:30-4:30 PM drop-off windows
  • Extended care available (some schools)

Contact: AACPS Transportation Division

  • Phone: 410-222-4500
  • Website: www.aacps.org/transportation

Parent Carpooling

Organizing Carpool:

Final Thoughts

Choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you’ll make in Anne Arundel County. Take your time, research thoroughly, visit schools, and trust your instincts.

Remember:

  • There’s no “perfect” school - find the best fit for your child
  • Your involvement makes a huge difference in your child’s success
  • AACPS offers diverse options for different learning styles
  • School choice is an ongoing conversation as your child grows

Good luck with your school search!
