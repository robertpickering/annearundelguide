Anne Arundel County Transit Routes
#

Public transit in Anne Arundel County centers on the MTA bus system and MARC train. Routes connect the county to Baltimore and DC, plus local circulation within communities.

MTA Commuter Bus Routes
#

The Maryland Transit Administration runs commuter buses from Anne Arundel to Baltimore and Washington DC. These are your main options for car-free commuting.

Route 4 - Glen Burnie to DC
#

Stops in Anne Arundel:

  • Glen Burnie MVA (1001 Crain Highway)
  • Glen Burnie Shopping Center
  • Ritchie Highway locations
  • Other stops along Crain Highway

DC Stops:

  • Union Station area
  • L’Enfant Plaza
  • Downtown DC stops

Schedule:

  • Morning: 5:30 AM - 9:00 AM (peak service)
  • Evening: 4:00 PM - 7:30 PM (peak return)
  • Weekend: Limited or no service

Cost:

  • Single ride: $2.00 cash, $1.50 with CharmCard
  • Monthly pass: Around $75-95 for unlimited

Website: Check current schedules at mtamaryland.com

Route 7 - Annapolis to Baltimore
#

Stops in Anne Arundel:

  • Annapolis MVA (200 Calvert Street)
  • Westgate Center
  • Route 50 corridor
  • Severna Park area

Baltimore Stops:

  • Timonium area
  • Downtown Baltimore
  • Other route-specific stops

Schedule:

  • Weekdays: Multiple trips both directions
  • Peak times: Every 30-60 minutes
  • Evenings and weekends: Reduced service

Cost:

  • Single ride: $2.00 cash, $1.50 with CharmCard
  • Monthly pass: $75 for unlimited selected routes

Route 12 - Severna Park to Baltimore
#

Stops in Anne Arundel:

  • Severna Park MVA (750 Ritchie Highway)
  • Ritchie Highway corridor
  • Severna Park village

Baltimore Stops:

  • Timonium
  • Downtown routes
  • Specific stops along route

Schedule:

  • Weekday peak hours primarily
  • Limited evening service
  • Weekend service varies by season

Cost: Same as other MTA commuter routes

Route 22 - Crofton to DC
#

Stops in Anne Arundel:

  • Crofton Center area
  • Defense Highway corridor
  • Southern Anne Arundel locations

DC Stops:

  • Union Station
  • Downtown stops
  • L’Enfant Plaza area

Schedule:

  • Morning rush: 5:45 AM - 9:00 AM
  • Evening rush: 4:15 PM - 8:00 PM
  • Limited midday and weekend service

Cost: Standard MTA commuter rates

Route 5 - Annapolis to BWI
#

Stops:

  • Annapolis to BWI Airport
  • Connects transit to airport

Use Case: Good for catching flights without driving

Route Information
#

CharmCard:

  • Reloadable transit card
  • Discounted fares with card vs cash
  • Available at MTA offices, vending machines
  • Works on all MTA services

Mobile Payment:

  • Some routes accept mobile payment
  • Check MTA website for current options

Real-Time Tracking:

  • MTA has app for route tracking
  • Check current arrivals before heading to stop

MTA Local Bus Routes
#

MTA also operates local routes within Anne Arundel County. These connect neighborhoods and shopping areas.

Local Route Examples
#

Route 100 series: Annapolis local circulation Route 200 series: Glen Burnie area Route 300 series: Severna Park/Odenton Route 400 series: Crofton area

Schedule:

  • Generally Monday-Saturday service
  • Reduced Sunday service
  • Evenings end by 7-8 PM typically

Cost:

  • $2.00 cash, $1.50 with CharmCard
  • Free transfers within 2 hours

Note: Local routes change frequently. Check current schedules on MTA website.

MARC Train
#

The MARC Penn Line runs from BWI Rail Station to Baltimore and DC.

BWI Rail Station
#

Location: BWI Airport Marriott Drive, adjacent to airport

Stops:

  • BWI Rail Station (Anne Arundel County)
  • Baltimore Union Station
  • Washington Union Station

Schedule Details
#

To Washington DC:

  • Morning peak: ~6:00-9:00 AM
  • Evening peak: ~4:00-7:00 PM
  • Travel time: 45-50 minutes
  • No weekend service (Amtrak serves this route)

To Baltimore:

  • More frequent throughout day
  • Travel time: 20-25 minutes
  • Some weekend service available

Tickets
#

Monthly Passes:

  • BWI to DC: Around $150-180
  • BWI to Baltimore: Around $100-120
  • Zone pricing applies

Single Ride:

  • DC: $5.75-12.00
  • Baltimore: $3.50-6.00
  • Purchase at station or online

Reserve Seats:

  • Available for additional fee
  • Guarantees seat on busy trains
  • Book ahead online

Ferry Services
#

St. Leonard Ferry
#

Route: St. Leonard to Solomons Island

Cost: Free

Schedule:

  • Runs 24/7
  • Frequency varies by time of day
  • Wait times 5-30 minutes typically

Use Case: Connects southern Anne Arundel to Calvert County and Eastern Shore via ferry

Website: Maryland Transportation Authority ferry info

Paratransit Services
#

MTA Access Link
#

Eligibility:

  • Must have disability preventing use of regular bus
  • Application and certification required
  • Door-to-door or curb-to-curb service

Service Area:

  • Within 3/4 mile of MTA bus routes
  • County-wide coverage

Reservation:

  • Call ahead required
  • Same-day service sometimes available
  • Advance booking recommended

Cost: Same as regular bus fare

Contact: MTA Access Link office for application

Tips for Transit Users
#

Peak vs Off-Peak:

  • Commuter buses fill up during rush hour
  • Arrive 10-15 minutes early
  • Consider flexible schedules if possible

CharmCard Benefits:

  • Cheaper than cash fares
  • Easy to reload
  • Works across all MTA services

Weekend Service:

  • Limited compared to weekdays
  • Plan ahead for trips
  • Some routes don’t run Sundays

Cold Weather:

  • Bus stops may not be heated
  • Have warm clothes ready
  • Check for weather delays

Luggage:

  • Commuter buses have space for bags
  • Check baggage policies for specific routes

Service Changes & Alerts
#

Schedule Changes:

  • Routes adjust seasonally
  • Holiday schedules posted in advance
  • Summer vs winter schedules differ

Service Alerts:

  • MTA website posts delays
  • Text alerts available for subscribers
  • Check before trips during weather events
