AACPS Enrollment Guide

AACPS Enrollment Guide

Enrolling your child in Anne Arundel County Public Schools requires specific documentation and following the right process. This guide walks through what you need and when to do it.

Determining Your School Zone

School Assignment

Public schools are assigned by address. Your specific street address determines which elementary, middle, and high school your child attends.

Find Your School:

  • AACPS School Finder tool on website
  • Enter your exact address
  • Shows assigned elementary, middle, and high school
  • Verify before moving or buying property

Website: www.aacps.org/schools/findyourschool

Important: School boundaries can change. Always verify current assignment for your address.

Required Documentation

Proof of Residency

You must prove you live in Anne Arundel County. Acceptable documents include:

Primary Documents (one required):

  • Current lease agreement
  • Deed or mortgage statement
  • Property tax bill
  • Purchase contract with closing date

Secondary Documents (supporting):

  • Utility bills in parent/guardian name (last 30 days)
  • Maryland driver’s license or ID with county address
  • Mail with county address dated within 30 days
  • Bank statements with county address

Child Documentation

Required for all students:

  • Original birth certificate
  • Social Security card (if child has one)
  • Immunization records
  • Previous school records (if transferring)
  • Report cards and transcripts

Immunization Requirements:

  • Maryland state-mandated vaccines
  • Valid health certificate from physician
  • Records must be current and on official form
  • Homeschool students need different documentation

If Records Missing:

  • Contact previous school district
  • Request records be sent directly to AACPS
  • School can request on your behalf
  • Enroll conditionally while waiting

Parent/Guardian Documentation

Required:

  • Valid photo ID (driver’s license, passport, etc.)
  • Custody documents if applicable
  • Contact information (phone, email, address)

Special Circumstances:

  • Guardianship papers if not biological parent
  • Court documents for custody arrangements
  • Foster care documentation if applicable

Enrollment Process

Step 1: Contact Your School

Before Enrolling:

  • Call your assigned school
  • Ask about enrollment appointments
  • Confirm required documents
  • Ask about orientation sessions

What They’ll Tell You:

  • Specific document requirements
  • Appointment availability
  • Health screening needs
  • First day information

Step 2: Complete Paperwork

Enrollment Forms:

  • Student registration form
  • Emergency contact information
  • Health information form
  • Transportation request (if needed)
  • Meal program application (free/reduced lunch)

Where to Get Forms:

  • School website download
  • In-person at school office
  • District enrollment office

Step 3: Submit Documentation

Bring Original Documents:

  • Copies won’t be accepted
  • Originals will be verified and returned
  • Keep copies for your records

Enrollment Office Review:

  • Staff verify all documents
  • Note any missing items
  • Provide checklist of remaining requirements

Step 4: Health Screening

Health Requirements:

  • Maryland state health form required
  • Completed by licensed physician
  • Must be current (within one year)
  • Includes vision and hearing screening

Schedule Health Exam:

  • Pediatrician or family doctor
  • Bring school health form to appointment
  • Complete before enrollment if possible
  • Some schools offer on-site screenings

Step 5: Schedule First Day

Orientation:

  • Some schools offer new student orientation
  • Check with your specific school
  • Helps students and parents adjust

First Day Info:

  • Arrival time
  • What to bring
  • Schedule overview
  • Bus information (if applicable)

Enrollment Timeline

Summer Enrollment (July-August)

Best Time:

  • Enroll in June or July for September start
  • Easier to get appointments
  • More flexibility with documentation
  • Child has time to adjust

Year-Round Enrollment

Ongoing Process:

  • AACPS accepts enrollments throughout year
  • Transfers at any time
  • Fill classes as capacity allows
  • May not get specific school if full

After Moving to County

Timeline:

  • Enroll within 2 weeks of moving
  • School district expects timely enrollment
  • Don’t wait until school starts
  • Can start process before actually moving

Grade-Level Specifics

Kindergarten

Age Requirements:

  • Must turn 5 by September 1
  • Proof of age required (birth certificate)
  • Some schools offer pre-K options

Kindergarten Readiness:

  • Schools may assess readiness
  • Not required but helpful
  • Talk to school about expectations

Middle School (Grades 6-8)

Transition Support:

  • Many schools offer transition programs
  • Meet teachers before school starts
  • Learn campus layout
  • Understand schedule changes

High School (Grades 9-12)

Credit Transfer:

  • Previous school transcripts reviewed
  • Credits may transfer or not
  • May need placement tests
  • Guidance counselor assigns courses

Graduation Requirements:

  • Maryland state requirements
  • AACPS additional requirements
  • Guidance counselor reviews plan

Special Programs Enrollment

Magnet Programs

Application Required:

  • Separate from regular enrollment
  • Applications due typically February-March
  • Interviews or assessments may be required
  • Acceptance based on program capacity

Popular Magnets:

  • STEM programs
  • IB programs
  • Arts programs
  • Language immersion

Special Education

Initial Evaluation:

  • Request evaluation in writing
  • School must respond within timeline
  • Evaluation determines services
  • IEP developed if eligible

Timeline:

  • Request evaluation
  • 60 days for evaluation (state requirement)
  • IEP meeting if eligible
  • Services begin immediately

English Language Learners

Assessment:

  • Home language survey
  • English proficiency test
  • Level determined
  • Program placement

Support Services:

  • ESL instruction
  • Bilingual staff available
  • Family communication support

Transfer Requests

Inside County Transfers

Inter-district Transfers:

  • Request change to different school
  • Must have space available
  • Transportation usually not provided
  • Approval not guaranteed

Process:

  • Submit transfer request
  • Wait for decision
  • If approved, follow enrollment process
  • May need to re-enroll

Out of County Transfers

Moving to AACPS:

  • Same enrollment process
  • Provide out-of-state or out-of-county records
  • May need transcript evaluation
  • Placement based on grade and space

Private to Public Transfers

Enrolling from Private School:

  • Same as out-of-county transfer
  • Provide private school records
  • Placement based on grade level
  • May need testing

Homeschool to Public School

Returning to Public School:

  • Follow standard transfer process
  • Provide homeschool records
  • Placement based on age and prior work
  • May need assessment

International Students

Non-U.S. Citizens:

  • Same residency requirements
  • Additional documentation may be needed
  • Visa documentation if applicable
  • English proficiency assessment

Documentation:

  • Valid visa or immigration documents
  • Passport
  • I-20 if F-1 student
  • English proficiency test

Transportation & Bus Service

Bus Eligibility

Eligibility:

  • Elementary students: Free bus if eligible route
  • Middle school: Usually free
  • High school: May have restrictions
  • Depends on distance from school

Requesting Bus Service:

  • Indicate on enrollment form
  • Provide pickup address
  • Route determined based on location
  • Route may change each year

Walking Zone

Some students walk:

  • Live within walking distance
  • May not qualify for bus
  • Safe walking routes developed
  • Parent may need to walk with young children

Fees & Costs

Public School Fees

Generally Free:

  • Tuition-free for residents
  • No enrollment fees
  • Most materials provided

Possible Costs:

  • Extracurricular activities
  • Athletic fees for some sports
  • AP exam fees (some financial aid)
  • Technology fees for certain programs
  • Field trip costs

Free & Reduced Lunch

Apply for Program:

  • Income-based eligibility
  • Application available online or at school
  • Annual renewal required
  • Confidential process

Benefits:

  • Free or reduced-price meals
  • May qualify for other assistance
  • Application determines eligibility

Common Questions

Can I enroll if I don’t have all documents?

Answer: Schools will work with families. You may enroll conditionally while gathering documents. Contact school immediately if missing items.

What if my child is behind grade level?

Answer: Schools assess incoming students. Placement may differ from previous grade. Focus is on student success.

Can I choose my child’s school?

Answer: Public schools assign by residence. You can request transfers but they’re not guaranteed. Private schools have different enrollment.

How do I handle custody issues?

Answer: Provide custody documentation. Schools need to know who has enrollment authority. Court orders help clarify.

What if I’m renting?

Answer: Lease agreement works as proof of residence. Some schools may ask for additional verification. Contact school if unsure.

Tips for Smooth Enrollment

  1. Start early - Don’t wait until last week
  2. Make copies - Keep copies of all documents
  3. Call ahead - Confirm requirements before visiting
  4. Be organized - Have everything ready at appointment
  5. Ask questions - Don’t hesitate to clarify requirements
  6. Follow up - Ensure all requirements are met
  7. Visit school - Meet staff before first day
