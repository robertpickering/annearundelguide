Healthcare & Mental Health Services

Comprehensive health care and mental health support for veterans and service members.

<div class="content-grid">
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🏥 VA Health Care</h3>
    <p><strong>VA Maryland Health Care System:</strong> Available for Maryland returning veterans needing assistance receiving health care or navigating the various services available through the VA.</p>
    <p><strong>Annapolis Vet Center:</strong> Located in Annapolis, provides counseling and support services.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Eligibility determination assistance</li>
      <li>Health care enrollment support</li>
      <li>Counseling services</li>
      <li>Small free parking lot available</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🧠 Mental Health Services</h3>
    <p><strong>Anne Arundel County Mental Health Agency, Inc.:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Crisis response systems and resources</li>
      <li>Immediate help and prevention services</li>
      <li>Recovery and resiliency programs</li>
      <li>Health and wellbeing support</li>
      <li><strong>Helpline:</strong> 410-222-3500 (Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM-4:30 PM ET)</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Free Treatment for Iraq & Afghanistan Veterans:</strong> Anne Arundel County veterans returning from Iraq & Afghanistan and their families will receive free mental health and substance use treatment (confidential services).</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🤝 Network of Care</h3>
    <p><strong>County Network of Care for Veterans:</strong> Features local provider, program, and facility listings in a searchable database.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Links to statewide and national organizations</li>
      <li>Related to military benefits and support</li>
      <li>For service members, veterans, and family members</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Additional Support:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Suicide Prevention and Support</li>
      <li>SMVF (Service Members, Veterans and Their Families) Suicide Prevention Coalition</li>
      <li>Anne Arundel County has the second highest Veteran population in the State of Maryland</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

Education Benefits & Scholarships

Educational opportunities and financial support for veterans and military families.

<div class="content-grid">
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🎓 Maryland State Scholarships</h3>
    <p><strong>Conroy-Cryor Memorial Scholarship:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Up to $13,689/year (2025-2026)</li>
      <li>For veterans and qualifying dependents</li>
      <li>Available at University System of Maryland schools</li>
      <li><strong>Application Deadline:</strong> March 1</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>VAIC Scholarship:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Up to 50% of tuition, fees, and room and board</li>
      <li>For Afghanistan and Iraq veterans and their families</li>
      <li>Administered through Maryland Higher Education Commission</li>
      <li><strong>Application Deadline:</strong> March 1</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>📚 Anne Arundel Community College</h3>
    <p><strong>Military & Veterans Benefits:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>VA Education Benefits assistance</li>
      <li>Entitlement explanations</li>
      <li>Enrollment verification support</li>
      <li>Military/Veteran Resource Center (MVRC) on Arnold campus</li>
      <li>Residency policies for tuition purposes for military personnel and spouses/dependents</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🎯 University of Maryland College Park</h3>
    <p>UMCP offers information of interest to Veterans about:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>How to register for classes</li>
      <li>Campus life resources</li>
      <li>Campus employment opportunities</li>
      <li>How military call-ups affect UMCP students</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
