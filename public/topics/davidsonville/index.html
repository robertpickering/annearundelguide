Davidsonville, Maryland
#

Historic Waterfront Chesapeake Bay Community

Davidsonville is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. One of Maryland’s oldest settlements, Davidsonville offers historic charm, waterfront properties, and a unique blend of colonial heritage and modern waterfront living on the scenic Chesapeake Bay.

About Davidsonville
#

Community Overview
#

Davidsonville offers:

  • Chesapeake Bay waterfront access
  • Historic colonial village (one of Maryland’s oldest)
  • 15 minutes to Annapolis
  • 45-55 minutes to Baltimore
  • 60-70 minutes to Washington, D.C.
  • 30 minutes to BWI Airport
  • Small, tight-knit community

The village is known for its historic charm, waterfront properties, and unique colonial character.

Google Maps: View Davidsonville Location

Historic Significance
#

Davidsonville was founded in 1705 and is one of Maryland’s oldest settlements:

  • Colonial Era: Established as a tobacco shipping port
  • Historic Buildings: Several structures date to the 18th century
  • Old Davidsonville: Historic village center with colonial architecture
  • Waterfront Heritage: Maritime history integral to community identity

The village maintains its historic character with strict preservation efforts.

Waterfront Village
#

Davidsonville’s defining features:

  • Waterfront homes with private docks and bay access
  • Historic village center with colonial charm
  • Small-town feel with strong community bonds
  • Limited development preserving historic character

The water and history define Davidsonville’s unique appeal.
