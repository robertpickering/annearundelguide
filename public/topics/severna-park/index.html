Severna Park, Maryland
#

Maryland’s Top-Rated Schools & Family Community

Severna Park is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Famous for having some of Maryland’s highest-performing public schools, Severna Park is one of the most sought-after communities in the state for families seeking excellent education in a friendly, suburban environment.

About Severna Park
#

Community Overview
#

Severna Park offers:

  • Top-rated schools (consistently ranked among Maryland’s best)
  • 15 minutes to Annapolis
  • 35-45 minutes to Baltimore
  • 45-60 minutes to Washington, D.C.
  • 20 minutes to BWI Airport
  • Water access to Chesapeake Bay (nearby Sandy Point)

The community is known for its small-town charm, excellent schools, and strong sense of community.

Google Maps: View Severna Park Location

History
#

Severna Park’s history dates to the late 19th century when the B&O Railroad extended service to the area. The name “Severna” comes from the nearby Severn River. The community developed as a residential area with a strong emphasis on education and family life. The Severna Park School system gained national recognition in the 1980s-1990s for exceptional academic performance.

School District Fame
#

Severna Park is synonymous with educational excellence:

  • Severna Park High School consistently ranks in Maryland’s top 10%
  • Severna Park Middle School receives high academic ratings
  • Elementary schools in the zone are highly sought-after
  • The school system attracts families from across Maryland

This reputation drives housing demand and property values.

School Zoning
#

Critical Consideration: Severna Park housing prices are strongly tied to school zoning:

Severna Park Elementary Zone:

  • Highly sought-after
  • Properties often command premium prices
  • Some areas zoned to other elementary schools

Severna Park Middle School Zone:

  • Consistently high-performing
  • Strong feeder to Severna Park High

Severna Park High School Zone:

  • Top-ranked in Maryland
  • Includes portions of Severna Park CDP
  • May include some neighboring areas

Verify Zoning: Always confirm school zoning for specific properties with AACPS
