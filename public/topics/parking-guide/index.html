Anne Arundel County Parking Guide

Anne Arundel County Parking Guide

Parking in Anne Arundel County varies significantly depending on where you are. Downtown Annapolis has strict rules and paid parking. Suburban areas like Crofton and Odenton mostly offer free parking at businesses and homes.

Annapolis Parking

Downtown Annapolis

Downtown Annapolis has the most restrictive parking in the county. This is historic district with limited space and high demand.

Metered Street Parking:

  • Cost: $1.50-2.00 per hour
  • Time limits: 2-4 hours typically
  • Payment: Coin meters and mobile app (ParkMobile)
  • Hours: Monday-Saturday 9 AM - 6 PM
  • Enforcement: 7 AM - 7 PM weekdays, 9 AM - 5 PM weekends

Public Parking Garages:

  • West Street Garage: $2/hour, $12/day max
  • Chase Street Garage: $2/hour, $12/day max
  • State Circle Garage: $2/hour, $15/day max
  • City Dock Garage: $2/hour, $12/day max

Free Parking Options in Annapolis:

  • East County area (east of West Street)
  • Residential areas outside downtown core
  • Some edge locations with 4+ hour limits

Residential Parking Permits:

  • Required in designated zones
  • $25-40 annually depending on zone
  • Must be Annapolis resident
  • Apply through Annapolis Parking Office

Annapolis Parking Office

Address: 100 George Street, Annapolis, MD 21401
Phone: 410-267-7800
Hours: Monday-Friday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM
Website: www.annapolismd.gov/parking

Glen Burnie Parking

Glen Burnie is more suburban with ample free parking at most locations.

Mall at White Flint:

  • Free parking in all lots
  • 24-hour access
  • Large selection of spaces

Glen Burnie Municipal Center:

  • Free parking
  • Open 7 AM - 5 PM weekdays
  • Near MVA and county offices

Restaurants & Shopping:

  • Most offer free customer parking
  • Time limits rare except in busy areas
  • Wegmans, Giant, and other major retailers have large lots

Severna Park Parking

Severna Park maintains a small-town feel with mostly free parking.

Severna Park Park & Ride:

  • Free parking
  • For commuter bus users primarily
  • Ritchie Highway location

Downtown Severna Park:

  • Free parking on side streets
  • Some time-restricted spaces near businesses
  • Generally easy to find spots

Retail Areas:

  • Free parking at all shopping centers
  • Crooked橡树 Mall nearby with free parking
  • No restrictions except during special events

Crofton & Odenton Parking

These southern county areas are fully suburban with abundant free parking.

Crofton Center:

  • Free parking throughout
  • Large lots at major retailers
  • No time restrictions

Odenton:

  • Free parking at shopping areas
  • Village Place has ample spaces
  • No metered parking in most areas

County Recreation Centers:

  • Free parking for programs
  • Some have hourly limits during events

BWI Airport Parking

BWI has multiple parking options with different price points. See BWI Airport Guide for complete details.

Quick reminder:

  • Economy: $8-10/day
  • Daily: $15-18/day
  • Valet: $25-30/day

County Facility Parking

Anne Arundel County Government Buildings:

  • Free parking at all locations
  • County Grounds (Annapolis): Large lot, free
  • Glen Burnie facilities: Free parking
  • Recreation centers: Free

Anne Arundel County Public Library:

  • Free parking at all 9 branches
  • Usually adequate except during popular programs
  • Central Library (Annapolis) can fill on weekends

Courts & Justice Center:

  • Free parking
  • Can fill early on court days
  • Arrive 30+ minutes early for court appearances

Special Event Parking

Annapolis Events

SmartFuel Sailing Week:

  • Heavy parking demand
  • Use park-and-shuttle when available
  • Downtown meters enforce 2-hour limits strictly

Blues Festival:

  • Parking opens early for events
  • Recommend using garages rather than street
  • Some streets close to vehicles

Fall Festival:

  • Special parking arrangements
  • Free shuttle from overflow lots
  • Check county website for updates

County-Wide Events

County Fair:

  • Parking fees apply ($10-20)
  • Shuttle service from remote lots
  • Arrive early for best spots

Community Festivals:

  • Usually free parking
  • Some require permits for vendor booths

Parking Tips

Residential Areas:

  • Watch for tow-away zones
  • Some neighborhoods require permits 24/7
  • Snow emergency parking rules apply in winter

Work Parking:

  • Most county employers provide free parking
  • Some downtown Annapolis employers charge
  • Ask about parking before accepting job

Visitor Parking:

  • Many businesses have visitor spaces
  • Don’t park in marked employee areas
  • Ask host if unsure

Time Limits:

  • Observe posted limits carefully
  • Some meters have enforcement cameras
  • Mobile payment apps track your time

Paid Parking Apps

ParkMobile:

  • Works in Annapolis and some county areas
  • Accepts credit cards
  • Extend time remotely if allowed

PayByPhone:

  • Used in some county locations
  • Similar functionality to ParkMobile

Enforcement & Tickets

Parking Enforcement:

  • Annapolis: City parking enforcement
  • County areas: County code enforcement
  • Hours vary by location

Ticket Costs:

  • Annapolis meter violations: $25-50
  • Time limit violations: $20-40
  • No parking zones: $50-100
  • Disabled spot violations: $500+

Paying Tickets:

  • Online: www.aacounty.org/parking-tickets
  • Mail: Address on ticket
  • In person: County cashier offices

Disabled Parking

County-Wide Rules:

  • Valid disabled placard or plates required
  • Free parking in metered spaces
  • Some locations have designated accessible spots

Annapolis Specific:

  • Disabled parking available in all garages
  • Free in metered zones with placard
  • Apply for county disabled permit through city

Tips to Avoid Problems

  1. Always read posted signs carefully
  2. Note time limits and enforcement hours
  3. Use garages in busy areas during events
  4. Keep parking tickets receipt if paying by app
  5. When in doubt, ask where to park
  6. Consider park-and-ride if commuting to Annapolis
