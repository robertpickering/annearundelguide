Odenton, Maryland

Family-Friendly Community Near Fort Meade

Odenton is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Known for its proximity to Fort Meade and Joint Base Andrews, excellent schools, and convenient MARC train access, Odenton is a popular choice for military families, government employees, and commuters to Washington, D.C.

About Odenton

Community Overview

Odenton is centrally located in Anne Arundel County, offering:

  • 20-25 minutes to Fort Meade
  • 15-20 minutes to Joint Base Andrews
  • 45-50 minutes to Washington, D.C. (MARC train)
  • 30-40 minutes to Baltimore

The community features a mix of residential neighborhoods, shopping centers, and recreational facilities, making it ideal for families and professionals.

Google Maps: View Odenton Location

History

Odenton developed primarily in the mid-20th century, with significant growth following the expansion of Fort Meade. The name “Odenton” is derived from the combination of “Oden” (from Oden Street, a local road) and “ton” (town).

Military & Government Connections

Proximity to Major Installations:

  • Fort Meade: 5 miles north - NSA headquarters, Army installations
  • Joint Base Andrews: 12 miles south - Air Force base, naval air facility
  • BWI Airport: 15 miles west - Major transportation hub

This proximity makes Odenton particularly attractive to military families and federal employees.

Transportation

MARC Train (Major Feature)

Odenton’s BWI Rail Station is a key transportation asset:

BWI Rail Station:

  • Address: 7001 BWI Rail Road, Baltimore, MD 21240
  • Distance from Odenton: ~5 miles (10-15 minute drive)
  • MARC Penn Line: Direct service to Washington, D.C.
  • Travel Time: 45-50 minutes to Union Station DC
  • Parking: $8/day (fills early on weekdays, arrive before 7 AM)
  • Schedule: Weekday commuter service (no weekend service)

MARC Schedule: marc.mta.info
