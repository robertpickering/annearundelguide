BWI Airport Guide - Getting There & Using the Rail Station

BWI Airport Guide

Baltimore-Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport sits right in Anne Arundel County, which makes it your local airport. That convenience means you’re usually 10-20 minutes from terminals depending on where you live in the county.

Location & Access

Address: 700 Ground Circle, Baltimore, MD 21240

BWI sits between Annapolis and Glen Burnie, south of MD-3 and east of I-95. The airport owns the land, so while the mailing address says Baltimore, you’re physically in Anne Arundel.

Driving to BWI

From Different Parts of the County

From Annapolis: Take MD-3 North to I-95 North, exit at BWI Airport. About 25-35 minutes depending on traffic.

From Glen Burnie: Direct access via MD-295 South. Most drivers know this route well - about 15-20 minutes.

From Severna Park: Take MD-174 West to MD-295 South. Around 20 minutes typically.

From Crofton: Take MD-175 North to MD-295 South. Roughly 25-30 minutes.

Routes

Most Common: MD-295 (Baltimore-Washington Parkway) is the main artery to the airport. Follow signs for BWI Airport.

From Baltimore: I-95 South to MD-295 South, follow airport signs.

From DC: I-95 South to MD-295 South. About 45-60 minutes depending on DC traffic.

Parking at BWI

BWI offers several parking options with different price points:

Rover Lot (Economy):

  • $8/day first day, $6/day subsequent days
  • Free shuttle every 10-15 minutes
  • About 10-15 minute drive to terminals
  • Best for trips longer than 3 days

Daily Parking (Galleries):

  • $15-18/day
  • Covered and uncovered spaces
  • Walking distance to terminals (5-10 minutes)
  • Good for 1-3 day trips

Valet Parking:

  • $25-30/day
  • Drop off right at curbside
  • Pick up at curbside when returning
  • Most convenient, most expensive

Oversized Vehicle Parking:

  • Same rates as economy or daily
  • Specific lots for RVs, large vehicles
  • Shuttle service available

Airport Website

Official BWI website has real-time parking availability: bwia.com

BWI MARC Train Station

The MARC Penn Line runs from BWI Rail Station to Union Station in DC. This is useful if you work in DC and want to avoid driving.

BWI Rail Station Details

Location: BWI Airport Marriott Drive, adjacent to airport

Hours:

  • Monday-Friday: 4:30 AM - 11:30 PM
  • Saturday-Sunday: Limited service

Parking at Rail Station:

  • Free parking available
  • Fill up quickly on weekday mornings
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before train

Train Schedule

To Washington Union Station:

  • Morning: ~6:00 AM - 9:00 AM (multiple trains)
  • Evening: ~4:00 PM - 7:00 PM (multiple trains)
  • Travel time: About 45-50 minutes
  • Weekend service: Limited, check schedule

To Baltimore Union Station:

  • Multiple trains throughout day
  • Travel time: About 20-25 minutes
  • More flexible than DC service

Ticketing

Monthly Pass:

  • Zone 2 (BWI to DC): Around $150-180/month
  • Zone 1 (BWI to Baltimore): Around $100-120/month
  • Purchase online or at station machines

Single Ride:

  • DC: $5.75-12.00 depending on time
  • Baltimore: $3.50-6.00
  • Cash or credit at machines

MARC Website

Official MARC info and schedules: marylandtransit.com/marc

Rideshare & Taxis

Uber & Lyft

Both services operate at BWI. Designated pickup areas are on the ground floor after baggage claim.

Typical costs within county:

  • Annapolis: $40-70
  • Glen Burnie: $25-40
  • Severna Park: $35-50
  • Crofton: $40-55

Peak pricing: Expect higher fares during holidays, rush hours, and bad weather.

Taxis

Yellow cab companies serve BWI. Fares are metered.

Base fare: Around $3-5 plus mileage

To Annapolis: $60-90 typical

To DC: $120-180 depending on destination

Public Transit Options

MTA Bus Routes

Route 4: Connects Glen Burnie to BWI and continues to DC

Route 7: Some service to BWI from Annapolis area

Cost: $2.00 cash, $1.50 with CharmCard

BWI Express

Shuttle services run to various locations. Check current schedules as routes change.

Terminal Information

BWI has one main terminal building with four levels:

Level 1: Baggage claim Level 2: Check-in counters Level 3: Departures, security, shops, restaurants Level 4: Airline gates

The layout is straightforward. Check your airline for which level to use.

Airlines at BWI

Major carriers include:

  • Southwest (largest presence)
  • American Airlines
  • Delta
  • United
  • JetBlue
  • Frontier
  • Spirit
  • Alaska Airlines

Each airline has specific check-in areas. Check your ticket or the BWI website for your airline’s terminal location.

Tips for BWI Travel

Weekday mornings: Arrive at least 2 hours early. Airport gets busy with commuters using MARC.

Weekends: Less busy than weekdays, but still arrive 1.5 hours early.

Holiday travel: Add extra time. BWI gets crowded during Thanksgiving, Christmas, and summer.

Parking reservations: Some lots allow online reservations, especially during peak travel times.

MARC tickets: Buy ahead online to avoid line at station.

Rideshare surge: During bad weather or peak times, prices jump. Factor this into your cost calculation.
