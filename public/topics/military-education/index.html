Military Education & Training Opportunities
#

Military families in Anne Arundel County have unique education options. From military education programs to civilian school support to GI Bill benefits, this guide covers educational resources for service members and families.

Military Education Programs
#

Department of Defense Education Activity (DoDEA)
#

DoDEA Schools:

  • Schools on military installations
  • For eligible military dependents
  • Curriculum matches national standards
  • Free tuition for eligible students

DoDEA High School (Fort Meade):

  • Addresses Fort Meade area
  • Grades 9-12
  • College preparatory
  • AP courses available
  • Extracurricular activities

DoDEA Middle School:

  • Middle school grades
  • Same campus or nearby
  • Smaller class sizes
  • Supportive environment

Eligibility for DoDEA
#

Who Qualifies:

  • Active duty dependents
  • Some National Guard
  • Some Reserve members
  • Certain other military categories

Verification:

  • DEERS registration required
  • Sponsorship verification
  • ID card required for enrollment

Child Development Centers
#

Childcare on Base:

  • Infant through preschool
  • Before/after school care

Enrollment:

  • Priority for active duty

  • Apply through base childcare

GI Bill Benefits
#

Post-9/11 GI Bill
#

Benefits Include:

  • Tuition and fees covered
  • Housing allowance
  • Book stipend
  • 36 months of benefits

Using in Maryland:

  • Covers public school tuition fully
  • Housing allowance varies by location
  • Can use at private schools (with limits)
  • Transfer to dependents possible

Montgomery GI Bill
#

Benefits:

  • Monthly payment
  • 36 months available
  • More flexibility in use
  • Less generous than Post-9/11

Yellow Ribbon Program
#

For Private Schools:

  • Extra funding for out-of-state/private
  • School and VA share costs
  • Covers full tuition at participating schools
  • Application required

Tips for Military Families
#

  1. Understand DEERS - Keep registration current for all benefits
  2. Use DoDEA if eligible - Consider military schools for stability
  3. Plan for GI Bill - Know your benefits before college age
  4. Document everything - Keep military training records
  5. Connect with liaison - School liaison officers help a lot
  6. Understand credit transfer - Military training can count for college
  7. Use community college - Good starting point for many
  8. Know deployment rights - Students have protections during deployment
  9. Take advantage of resources - Many free services available
  10. Plan for transitions - Moves are easier with preparation
