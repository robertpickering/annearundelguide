Anne Arundel County Settling In Guide - First 30 Days

Anne Arundel County Settling In Guide
#

Your First 30 Days Checklist
#

Moving to Anne Arundel County? This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know for your first 30 days in the area, from utility setup to school enrollment, healthcare navigation, and local resources.

Week 1: Essential Setups (Days 1-7)
#

Utilities & Services
#

Immediate Actions (Day 1-2)
#

1. Verify Utility Accounts

  • Electric: Potomac Edison (1-800-225-5777) or AEP Maryland
    • Account setup: potomacedison.com
    • Business hours: 7 AM - 7 PM EST
  • Gas: Potomac Edison (if needed)
  • Water: Anne Arundel County Water Department
    • Service area varies by location
    • aacounty.org/water
  • Sewer: County water department handles sewage
  • Trash/Recycling: County-sanctioned haulers
    • Roto rooter: Major hauler (410-263-3700)
    • Other approved haulers: Check county website
    • Schedule: Weekly collection by neighborhood

Google Maps: AACounty Water Department

2. Internet & Cable Setup

  • Providers:
    • Comcast Xfinity: (800) 266-5278
    • Verizon Fios: (800) 935-3000
    • Frontier Communications: (800) 917-8287
  • Action: Schedule installation 3-5 days before move-in
  • Cost: $50-$100/month for basic packages

3. Phone Service

  • Mobile: Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, Sprint all have good coverage
  • Landline: Optional, if needed for business
  • Emergency: 911 coverage (always available)

Housing & Property
#

4. Property Orientation (Day 1-2)

  • Learn Location:
    • Emergency exits
    • Main water shut-off valve location
    • Electrical panel location
    • Circuit breakers and their functions
  • HVAC System:
    • Learn thermostat operation
    • Schedule filter change/replacement
    • Note maintenance schedule
  • Appliances:
    • Read manuals for major appliances
    • Learn operation of oven, dishwasher, washing machine

5. Security Setup (Day 2-3)

  • Change Locks: If you have the keys, change exterior door locks
  • Alarm System:
    • ADT: (800) 442-2741
    • SimpliSafe: DIY option (simplisafe.com)
    • Frontpoint: (877) 516-1700
  • Exterior Lights: Ensure all exterior lights work
  • Windows: Check all window locks and operation

Healthcare Setup
#

6. Find Healthcare Providers (Days 2-4)

  • Primary Care Physician:
    • Calvert Anne Arundel Physical: Multiple locations
    • CVPH: Community Health Partnerships
    • Annapolis Internal Medicine: (410) 268-5858
  • Dentist: Find local dentist near your neighborhood
  • Pharmacy: Choose convenient pharmacy for prescriptions
    • CVS: Multiple locations
    • Walgreens: Multiple locations
    • Giant Pharmacy: Shopping centers

7. Insurance & Medical Records

  • Update Insurance: Provide new address to health insurance
  • Transfer Records: Request medical records transfer
  • Schedule Check-up: Book first appointment within first month

Schools & Education
#

8. School Enrollment (If Applicable)

  • Contact AACPS: (410) 222-8000
  • Required Documents:
    • Proof of residence (lease/deed)
    • Birth certificate
    • Immunization records
    • Previous school records
    • Medical records
    • Photo ID of parent/guardian
  • Timeline: Complete within first 2 weeks

Resources:

  • AACPS Enrollment
  • Google Maps: AACounty Education Department

Local Orientation
#

9. Identify Essential Services

  • Nearest Hospital: Note emergency contact
    • Anne Arundel Medical Center: (410) 268-5000
    • Location: 2000 International Drive, Annapolis
  • Nearest Pharmacy: Note for prescriptions
  • Nearest Grocery Store: For immediate needs
  • Nearest Gas Station: For vehicle fueling
  • Nearest Police Station: Non-emergency contact: (410) 268-2222

10. Mail & Address Updates

  • USPS: Update address via USPS.com
  • Banking: Update address with all financial institutions
  • Credit Cards: Update billing addresses
  • Subscriptions: Update delivery addresses
  • Online Accounts: Update address with online retailers

Week 2: Community Integration (Days 8-14)
#

Neighborhood Exploration
#

11. Shop Local (Days 8-10)

  • Grocery Stores:
    • Giant Food: Multiple locations
    • Whole Foods: Annapolis area
    • Trader Joe’s: Nearby locations
    • Walmart: Gambrills area
    • Local Markets: Farmer’s markets (seasonal)
  • Local Restaurants: Explore neighborhood dining
  • Coffee Shops: Identify local cafes

12. Parks & Recreation

  • County Parks: aacounty.org/parks
  • Community Centers: Learn about programs
  • Recreation Departments: Register for activities
  • Beaches: Sandy Point State Park (5401 Ritchie Highway, Severn)
    • Activities: Swimming, camping, fishing
    • Hours: Seasonal (Memorial Day - Labor Day)

13. Community Resources

  • Public Library: Anne Arundel County Free Library
    • Locations: Annapolis, Arnold, Gambrills, Glen Burnie, Severna Park
    • Services: Books, internet access, programs, computer use
    • Website: aacounty.org/library
  • Fire Department: Learn about local station
  • Police: Attend community meetings if available

Administrative Tasks
#

14. Driver’s License & Vehicle Registration

  • MD Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA):
    • Address: Must update within 60 days
    • Required: Current MD license, proof of residency, Social Security number
    • Location: Find nearest MVA office
    • Website: mdmva.maryland.gov
    • Google Maps: Maryland MVA Annapolis

15. Voter Registration

  • Maryland State Board of Elections:
    • Website: votemaryland.org
    • Timeline: Can register anytime, deadline 30 days before election
    • Required: MD driver’s license or state ID

Work & Employment
#

16. Local Commute Assessment

  • Test Routes: Drive to work during rush hour
  • Public Transit: Test MTA routes if using
    • MTA Info: (410) 768-7777
    • Website: mtamaryland.com
  • Parking: Confirm work parking arrangements
  • Transportation Costs: Calculate commuting expenses

Financial Setup
#

17. Banking & Financial

  • Local Banks:
    • Anne Arundel County Credit Union: Multiple locations
    • SunTrust/Truist: Branch locations
    • Bank of America: Branch locations
  • Set Up Direct Deposit: Update with employer
  • Update Billing Addresses: All credit cards and accounts
  • Budget Planning: Estimate local cost of living

Week 3: Community Connection (Days 15-21)
#

Social Integration
#

18. Meet Neighbors

  • Introductions: Meet immediate neighbors
  • Community Groups: Join neighborhood social media groups
    • Facebook groups for each community
    • Nextdoor app for neighborhood updates
  • HOA: If applicable, attend next meeting

19. Religious/Spiritual Communities

  • Churches: Find local church if applicable
  • Synagogues: Jewish communities in Annapolis area
  • Mosques: Muslim communities in Anne Arundel
  • Temples: Buddhist and Hindu communities

20. Recreational Activities

  • Fitness Centers: Local gyms and fitness studios
  • Sports Leagues: Recreational sports leagues
  • Hobby Groups: Clubs and organizations matching interests
  • Volunteer: Local charities and volunteer opportunities

Education Deep Dive
#

21. School Community

  • PTA/PTO: Join parent organization
  • Teacher Meetings: Attend introductory meetings
  • School Calendar: Review academic calendar
  • Extracurricular: Register for after-school activities
  • Transportation: Arrange school bus or carpool

22. Childcare (If Applicable)

  • Daycare Centers: Research licensed facilities
  • Preschool Programs: AACPS early childhood programs
  • After-School Care: After-school programs for children
  • Baby Sitters: Local references from parents

Healthcare Integration
#

23. Establish Care

  • Pediatrician: If children (separate from adult care)
  • Specialists: Schedule any needed specialist appointments
  • Dental: Complete first dental cleaning/exam
  • Vision: Eye exam if needed
  • Mental Health: Identify mental health providers if needed

Home & Property
#

24. Property Maintenance

  • Lawn Care: Arrange service if needed
  • Pool Maintenance: If applicable
  • Landscaping: Garden or yard maintenance
  • Cleaning: Professional deep cleaning if needed
  • Repairs: Address any immediate repairs

Week 4: Full Integration (Days 22-30)
#

Community Engagement
#

25. Attend Community Events

  • County Events: Check community calendar
  • Neighborhood Meetings: Attend local HOA or community meetings
  • Town Halls: Attend county commissioner meetings
  • Festivals: Look for upcoming local festivals

26. Local Shopping Patterns

  • Favorite Stores: Establish regular shopping locations
  • Restaurants: Favorite dining spots
  • Services: Establish relationships with service providers
  • Support Local: Make intentional choices for local businesses

Transportation Optimization
#

27. Commute Optimization

  • Best Routes: Identify fastest routes to destinations
  • Park-and-Ride: If using, note locations
  • Bike Routes: Check for bike paths if cycling
  • Walking Routes: Test walking to local destinations

28. Transportation Accounts

  • ParkPay: If using paid parking
  • Toll Accounts: E-ZPass for Maryland tolls
  • Transit Cards: CharmCard for Baltimore transit if needed

Documentation & Records
#

29. Organize Important Documents

  • Create Filing System: Physical and digital organization
  • Important Documents: Store safely
    • Birth certificates
    • Marriage certificates
    • Social Security cards
    • Passports
    • Vehicle titles
    • Home documents (deed, mortgage, insurance)
  • Emergency Contacts: Create contact list

30. Review & Adjust

  • Budget Check: Review first month expenses
  • Settling In Review: What works, what needs adjustment
  • Goal Setting: Set goals for next 3 months
  • Community Feedback: Provide feedback on county services if needed

Cost of Living Quick Reference
#

Tax Rates:

  • State Income Tax: 2% - 6.25% (graduated)
  • Property Tax: ~0.95% (among lowest in Maryland)
  • Sales Tax: 6.45% (state 6% + county 0.45%)

Average Costs (2025):

  • Median Home Price: $500,000 - $900,000 (varies by area)
  • Rental (1BR): $1,400 - $2,000/month
  • Rental (2BR): $1,700 - $2,500/month
  • Utilities: $150 - $250/month
  • Groceries: 8% above national average
  • Gas: Varies with state prices

Detailed Budget Guide: Cost of Living in Anne Arundel County

Seasonal Considerations
#

Summer (June-August)
#

  • Beaches: Peak season at Sandy Point and area beaches
  • Events: Numerous outdoor festivals and events
  • Traffic: Heavy tourist traffic on weekends
  • Heat: Humid summers, plan outdoor activities for morning/evening

Fall (September-November)
#

  • Weather: Pleasant temperatures, ideal for outdoor activities
  • Events: Harvest festivals, county fair
  • School: Start of school year, community activities resume
  • Foliage: Beautiful fall colors in parks

Winter (December-February)
#

  • Weather: Cold, occasional snow/ice
  • Heating: Ensure HVAC system is functioning
  • Roads: Prepare for winter driving conditions
  • Indoor Activities: Library programs, indoor recreation

Spring (March-May)
#

  • Weather: Mild, variable
  • Gardening: Start spring gardening projects
  • Events: Spring festivals and outdoor activities
  • Maintenance: Prepare outdoor spaces for summer
