Housing Savings
#

Ways to Save on Housing:

  • Rent First: Build credit before buying
  • Down Payment Assistance: County programs available
  • First-Time Buyer Programs: Lower rates, assistance
  • House Hacking: Rent rooms, reduce mortgage
  • Refinance: Lower interest rates when available

Transportation Savings
#

Ways to Save on Transportation:

  • Public Transit: Use MTA bus instead of driving
  • Carpool: Split gas costs
  • Bike: Zero cost for short trips
  • Combine Trips: Reduce driving
  • Fuel Efficiency: Maintain car, drive efficiently

Grocery Savings
#

Ways to Save on Groceries:

  • Meal Planning: Plan weekly meals
  • Coupons: Use digital and paper coupons
  • Store Brands: Save 20-40% on store brands
  • Sales: Buy when on sale
  • Loyalty Cards: Store rewards programs

Frequently Asked Questions
#

Common Cost of Living Questions
#

Q: Is Annapolis expensive? A: Yes, Annapolis has the highest housing costs in the county, with median home prices around $750,000.

Q: What’s the cheapest area in Anne Arundel County? A: Glen Burnie and Gambrills tend to have the most affordable housing options.

Q: Is rent or buy better in Anne Arundel? A: Depends on your timeline. Buying builds equity; renting is more flexible. Calculate based on how long you’ll stay.

Q: What are the property taxes like? A: Maryland has relatively low property taxes at 0.95%, among the lowest in the state.

Q: How much should I budget for groceries? A: $300-500/month for singles, $800-1,200/month for families of 4.

Q: Are there property tax exemptions? A: Yes, Maryland offers homestead exemptions and other property tax relief programs.
