Living in Anne Arundel County

Everything you need to know about cost of living, schools, neighborhoods, and transportation.

Cost of Living

Median Household Income: $106,367 (2020-2024)

Mean Travel Time to Work: 31.4 minutes

Education: 40.1% have Bachelor's degree or higher

Anne Arundel County offers a higher cost of living compared to the national average, driven primarily by housing costs. The median home value reflects the county's desirable location and amenities.

Schools & Education

County Schools: Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS)

District Size: Over 78,000 students

High Schools: 18 high schools serving grades 9-12

AACPS operates numerous elementary, middle, and high schools throughout the county, with several schools consistently earning national recognition for academic excellence.

Commuting & Transportation

Major Highways: I-95, MD-295, US-50

Public Transit: MTA Maryland bus service, MARC train access

Airport: BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport (15-25 minutes from most areas)

County offers multiple commuting options with convenient access to Baltimore, Washington DC, and the Baltimore-Washington corridor.
