Anne Arundel County Transportation & Commuting Guide
#

Complete Guide to Getting Around Anne Arundel County
#

Anne Arundel County offers multiple transportation options for residents, from major highways for driving to public transit services. This comprehensive guide covers all transportation options, commuting routes, parking, and travel tips for the county.

Highway & Road Network
#

Major Highways
#

Interstate 95 (I-95):

  • Route: Runs north-south through eastern Anne Arundel County
  • Connections: Baltimore to south (Delaware, Virginia)
  • County Access: Linthicum, Annapolis Road exits
  • Traffic: Heavy during rush hour (6-9 AM, 4-7 PM)
  • Toll: Some sections are toll (Delaware)

Baltimore-Washington Parkway (MD-295):

  • Route: Main north-south corridor through county
  • Connections: DC to Baltimore
  • County Access: Multiple exits throughout South County
  • Traffic: Very heavy during rush hour
  • Free: No toll on MD-295

US-50 (Ritchie Highway):

  • Route: East-west corridor across county
  • Connections: Annapolis to Baltimore suburbs
  • County Access: Throughout South and Central County
  • Traffic: Heavy during rush hour
  • Scenic: Beautiful bay views

Maryland Route 174 (Annapolis Road):

  • Route: Connects Annapolis to DC area
  • County Access: Central Anne Arundel
  • Traffic: Moderate to heavy
  • Connections: To MD-295, I-95

Maryland Route 100 (Riva Road):

  • Route: Connects Annapolis to Severna Park
  • County Access: Southern Annapolis area
  • Traffic: Moderate

Local Roads
#

Major Arterials:

  • Severn Station Road: Major north-south arterial
  • Crain Highway: East-west corridor in South County
  • Golf Road: Connects to Severna Park
  • Ritchie Highway (US-50): Main east-west route
  • Maryland Avenue: Annapolis connection

Residential Streets:

  • Neighborhood Streets: Vary by community
  • HOA Roads: Some private roads in planned communities
  • County Roads: Maintained by Anne Arundel County

Driving Considerations
#

Traffic Patterns:

  • Rush Hour: 6:30-9:00 AM (morning), 4:00-7:00 PM (evening)
  • Weekends: Lighter traffic, tourist areas busy
  • Construction: Frequent road work on major routes
  • Events: Major events cause traffic delays

Common Congestion Points:

  • MD-295: Near Annapolis exits
  • US-50: Crossing Severn River
  • I-95: BWI area, Baltimore connection
  • Severn Station Road: Heavy during rush hour
  • Annapolis Road: Connecting areas

Public Transit
#

MTA Maryland Bus Services
#

MTA Maryland Overview:

  • Service: Bus transit throughout Maryland
  • Anne Arundel Coverage: Extensive county coverage
  • Routes: Multiple routes connecting communities
  • Fare: $2.00 cash, $1.50 CharmCard

Major Routes:

Route 3:

  • Route: Annapolis to Baltimore
  • Stops: Annapolis, Glen Burnie, Towson
  • Frequency: Every 30-60 minutes
  • Hours: 5 AM - 11 PM (varies by direction)
  • Use: Commute to Baltimore

Route 28:

  • Route: Crofton to Annapolis
  • Stops: Crofton, Severna Park, Annapolis
  • Frequency: Every 30-45 minutes
  • Hours: 6 AM - 10 PM
  • Use: South County to Annapolis

Route 55:

  • Route: Linthicum to Baltimore
  • Stops: Linthicum, Glen Burnie, Towson
  • Frequency: Every 30-60 minutes
  • Hours: 5:30 AM - 10:30 PM
  • Use: BWI area to Baltimore

Route 95:

  • Route: BWI to Annapolis
  • Stops: BWI, Severn, Annapolis
  • Frequency: Every 45-60 minutes
  • Hours: 6 AM - 9 PM
  • Use: Airport area to Annapolis

Route 97:

  • Route: Crofton to Baltimore
  • Stops: Crofton, Glen Burnie, Towson
  • Frequency: Every 60 minutes
  • Hours: 6 AM - 8 PM
  • Use: South County to Baltimore

MTA Resources:

  • Website: mtamaryland.com
  • Phone: (410) 768-7777
  • Schedule: Route schedules and maps online
  • Fare Info: CharmCard and cash payments

CharmCard
#

CharmCard Overview:

  • What: Smart card for Maryland transit
  • Acceptance: MTA buses, Light Rail, MARC, Metro
  • Discount: $1.50 fare vs. $2.00 cash
  • Purchase: MTA offices, vending machines, online
  • Reload: Online, machines, retail locations

CharmCard Benefits:

  • Fare Cap: Free rides after 20 rides per month
  • Transfer: Free transfers within 2 hours
  • Balance: Pre-loaded with fare amount
  • Personalized: Lost card protection

CharmCard Purchase:

  • MTA Offices: In-person purchase
  • Vending Machines: Stations and bus terminals
  • Retail: Select retail locations
  • Online: MTA Maryland website
  • Cost: $5 card fee, load fare amount

  • South County Express: Crofton to DC area

Commuter Bus Benefits:

Rail Services
#

MARC Train
#

MARC Overview:

  • Service: Regional rail commuter train
  • Lines: Penn Line, Camden Line
  • Station: BWI Rail Station (Anne Arundel)

BWI Rail Station:

  • Address: 7001 Friendship Road, Baltimore, MD 21240
  • Parking: 1,200+ spaces (free)
  • Access: From BWI Airport via shuttle
  • Trains: Penn Line (DC/Baltimore)
  • Hours: 5 AM - 11 PM (varies by day)

MARC Routes:

  • Penn Line: BWI to Baltimore Union Station, Washington DC
  • Frequency: 15-20 trains per day
  • Travel Time: 30-40 min to Baltimore, 60-75 min to DC
  • Fare: $6.75 - $13.25 (one-way, peak/off-peak)

MARC Ticket Purchase:

  • MARC Ticket App: Purchase on smartphone
  • Station Vending Machines: At BWI Rail Station
  • Online: MARC website
  • Monthly Pass: Unlimited rides for $150-200

MARC Resources:

  • Website: marctrain.com
  • Phone: (800) 325-6224
  • Schedule: Schedules and maps online

Amtrak
#

BWI Amtrak Station:

  • Address: 7001 Friendship Road, Baltimore, MD 21240

  • Services: Long-distance and regional trains
  • Routes: Northeast Corridor to DC, New York, Boston
  • Fare: Varies by destination and advance purchase

Amtrak Benefits:

  • Comfort: Spacious seating, amenities
  • WiFi: Free onboard internet
  • Power: Outlets at seats
  • Storage: Luggage racks

Amtrak Resources:

  • Website: amtrak.com
  • Phone: (800) 872-2849
  • Apps: Mobile app for tickets

Light Rail

Baltimore Light Rail:

  • Fare: $2.25 (same as MTA bus)

Auto Insurance
#

Maryland Auto Insurance:

  • Requirements: Liability coverage minimums

    • $60,000 bodily injury per accident

  • Cost: $1,200-2,000/year (varies by factors)

Insurance Options:

Frequently Asked Questions
#

Common Transportation Questions
#

Q: What’s the best way to get to Baltimore? A: MARC train is fastest (40 min), or drive via I-95 (35-50 min). Bus is cheaper but slower (45-60 min).

Q: Can I bike to Annapolis from Crofton? A: Yes, there are bike routes, but it’s 15-20 miles with some busy roads. Consider MTA bus or car for long distances.

Q: Is BWI Rail Station convenient? A: Yes, free parking, connected to airport. Good for DC commuters.

Q: What’s the cheapest way to get around? A: MTA bus with CharmCard ($1.50/ride). Car is cheaper for multiple stops or groups.

Q: Are there bike lanes in Annapolis? A: Yes, some bike lanes and shared lanes in Annapolis and Severna Park.
