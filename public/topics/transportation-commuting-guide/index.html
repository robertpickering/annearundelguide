Transportation & Commuting Guide

Complete Transportation Options for Anne Arundel County

Navigate Anne Arundel County with ease using our comprehensive transportation guide. Learn about free county bus service, MTA connections to BWI Airport and beyond, ferry services, commuter options, and everything you need to know about getting around Maryland's premier coastal county.

County Bus Service BWI Airport Access

⚠️ St. Leonard's Ferry Status Update

IMPORTANT: The St. Leonard's Ferry has been closed since 2020 and remains unavailable for transportation. This ferry previously served as the only transportation link between Maryland and Virginia on that section of the Potomac River.

For more information about Maryland ferry services, including the closed St. Leonard's Ferry status and alternative crossing options, visit the MDOT Maryland Official Website.

Anne Arundel County Transit System

All Anne Arundel County public transit is FREE! The Office of Transportation operates 9 bus routes to help residents get around the county efficiently.

BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport Transportation

Multiple transportation options available for getting to and from BWI Airport from anywhere in Anne Arundel County.

<div class="transportation-grid">
  <div class="transportation-card">
    <h3>🚌 MTA Bus Services</h3>
    <p>The Maryland Department of Transportation Maryland Transit Administration (MDOT MTA) offers bus service on the <strong>#75 bus</strong> from BWI Marshall Airport connecting to:</p>
    <ul>
      <li> Parkway Center</li>
      <li> Arundel Mills Mall</li>
      <li> Airport 100 Park</li>
      <li> Patapsco Light Rail Stop</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Schedule:</strong> Regular service throughout the day. Check MTA schedules for current times.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="transportation-card">
    <h3>🚌 MTA Commuter Bus 201</h3>
    <p>The MTA Commuter Bus 201 operates between Gaithersburg Park and Ride lot (located at I-270 and MD 124 in Montgomery County) and BWI Marshall Airport.</p>
    <p><strong>Key Stops:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li> Shady Grove Metro</li>
      <li> Georgia Avenue Park & Ride</li>
      <li> Burtonsville</li>
      <li> BWI Airport (MARC/Amtrak Station)</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Note:</strong> MARC monthly and weekly passes are accepted on trips to and from BWI.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="transportation-card">
    <h3>🚌 Anne Arundel County BWI Express</h3>
    <p>The Anne Arundel County Office of Transportation offers a <strong>FREE</strong> shuttle between BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport and Annapolis Mall. The shuttle also makes intermediate stops at Crownsville and Millersville Park & Ride.</p>
    <p><strong>Service Hours:</strong> Open daily from 6 am to 10 pm (except major holidays).</p>
    <p><strong>Route:</strong> Annapolis Mall → Crownsville → Millersville Park & Ride → BWI Southwest Terminal → BWI International Terminal</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="transportation-card">
    <h3>🚆 MTA Light Rail & MARC Train</h3>
    <p><strong>Light RailLink:</strong> BWI Airport / Glen Burnie - Hunt Valley route provides direct rail service.</p>
    <p><strong>MARC Train:</strong> Penn Line service available at BWI Marshall Airport station for commuting to Baltimore and Washington, DC.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Commuter Options & Programs

Multiple commuter programs and transportation alternatives for getting to work and reducing traffic congestion.

<div class="commuter-grid">
  <div class="commuter-card">
    <h3>🚗 Commuter Choice Maryland</h3>
    <p>Commuter Choice Maryland offers programs to help residents join carpools and vanpools, find transit routes that are right for them, and access Guaranteed Ride Home programs.</p>
    <ul>
      <li> Carpool and vanpool matching</li>
      <li> Guaranteed Ride Home program</li>
      <li> Transit route planning</li>
      <li> Commuter benefits</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="commuter-card">
    <h3>🚌 Employer Shuttle Programs</h3>
    <p>Multiple employer-sponsored shuttle programs serve the BWI Business District and Fort Meade area:</p>
    <ul>
      <li> MeadeRide (Fort Meade employees)</li>
      <li> MillsRide (Arundel Mills area)</li>
      <li> Wegmans shuttle</li>
      <li> NSA's LINK shuttle between BWI Business District and Fort Meade/NSA</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="commuter-card">
    <h3>🚂 MARC Train Service</h3>
    <p><strong>Penn Line:</strong> Connects BWI Marshall Airport station to Washington Union Station and Baltimore Penn Station.</p>
    <p><strong>Amtrak:</strong> Additional intercity rail service available at BWI station.</p>
    <p><strong>Monthly Passes:</strong> Accepted on MTA Commuter Bus 201 for trips to and from BWI.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Major Highways & Roadways

Anne Arundel County benefits from excellent highway access connecting to major regional markets.

<div class="highways-grid">
  <div class="highway-card">
    <h3>🛣️ Interstate 95</h3>
    <p>I-95 runs through northern Anne Arundel County, providing access to:</p>
    <ul>
      <li> Baltimore (north)</li>
      <li> Washington, DC (south)</li>
      <li> Major Midwest markets within overnight drive</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="highway-card">
    <h3>🛣️ MD Route 170</h3>
    <p>Connects I-95 to Annapolis and southern Anne Arundel County communities.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="highway-card">
    <h3>🛣️ MD Route 2</h3>
    <p>Coastal route connecting Annapolis to Southern Anne Arundel County and Calvert County.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="highway-card">
    <h3>🛣️ MD Route 100</h3>
    <p>Connects Glen Burnie to Annapolis and the U.S. Naval Academy.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="highway-card">
    <h3>🌉 Chesapeake Bay Bridge</h3>
    <p>MD Route 50/301 links Maryland's Eastern Shore to the Baltimore-Washington metropolitan area. The Chesapeake Bay Bridge is a critical transportation corridor for the region.</p>
  </div>
