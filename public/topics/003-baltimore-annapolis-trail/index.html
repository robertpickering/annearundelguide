Baltimore & Annapolis Trail

Address: Multiple access points connecting Arnold to Baltimore, MD

Overview

The Baltimore-Annapolis Trail is a multi-use recreational trail connecting Baltimore and Annapolis, offering beautiful waterfront views and access to Anne Arundel County’s most treasured natural and historic attractions. This scenic route provides outdoor enthusiasts and casual visitors alike with an exceptional opportunity to explore the region’s charm while enjoying excellent exercise and recreation. You will discover that this trail represents one of Maryland’s finest recreational resources because it combines accessibility, beauty, and historical significance in ways that satisfy every visitor’s needs. The well-maintained surface and thoughtful routing make it an ideal destination for people seeking safe, enjoyable outdoor experiences that don’t require special equipment or extensive preparation.

Why You’ll Love It

The Baltimore-Annapolis Trail connects you to stunning waterfront views and historic Anne Arundel County landmarks in ways that feel both exciting and genuinely relaxing. Whether you’re cycling along the beautiful waterfront or walking through picturesque scenic areas, you’ll experience the authentic charm of this beautiful coastal Maryland region that draws visitors from across the nation. The trail makes it easy to explore coastal Maryland’s best attractions without the stresses of traffic or parking challenges that often ruin otherwise perfect day trips. You will find that each visit offers new discoveries and experiences, from the changing seasons to different aircraft watching opportunities to conversations with fellow trail users who share your love of the outdoors.

What You Can Do Here

Historic Background

This trail repurposed a former railway line, which explains its smooth, paved surface and relatively gentle grades that make it accessible to virtually everyone. The railway heritage means you’ll enjoy a well-constructed path perfect for various recreational activities without worrying about steep inclines or rough terrain that might challenge less experienced visitors. You will appreciate how the original railway engineers designed the route to minimize grades while maximizing scenic value, creating a path that feels natural despite being engineered for efficiency. Many history enthusiasts find that riding along this trail connects them to Maryland’s transportation past in ways that enrich their understanding of the region’s development.

Scenic Route

The path winds through diverse landscapes that showcase the absolute best of Anne Arundel County’s natural beauty and cultural heritage in surprising combinations. You’ll pass through peaceful wooded areas, open fields that sparkle with wildflowers in spring, and historic communities while enjoying views that change with each mile you travel. The combination of natural beauty and cultural landmarks makes this trail engaging throughout the entire journey without ever feeling monotonous or repetitive despite its considerable length. You will discover hidden vistas and interesting landmarks along the way that make every mile feel rewarding and memorable in ways that keep you eager to continue your journey.

Full Paving

Unlike some regional trails that mix surfaces and create challenging conditions, the Baltimore & Annapolis Trail stays completely paved from end to end without exception. This consistency makes it accessible to everyone, including those using wheelchairs, strollers, or other mobility devices who might struggle on gravel or dirt paths. The smooth surface is equally suitable for walking, running, and bicycling, allowing families with different activity preferences to enjoy the trail together without compromise. You will appreciate how the paving allows you to focus entirely on enjoying the scenery and company rather than worrying about terrain conditions or equipment limitations.

Activities You Can Enjoy

Walking and Hiking

Take leisurely walks or more vigorous hikes along this well-maintained trail that welcomes visitors of all ages and fitness levels with open arms. The consistent pavement makes it comfortable for all foot types, and the distance can be adjusted to match your fitness level and available time without requiring special planning or preparation. You can choose to walk just a few miles as a weekend activity or tackle the entire route as an accomplishment that builds fitness and confidence over time. Many families find that this trail becomes their regular weekend activity because it offers flexibility, safety, and natural beauty that make every walk feel special.

Bicycling

Cyclists appreciate the smooth surface and complete lack of vehicle traffic that makes riding feel safe and enjoyable for beginners and experts alike. The complete route offers an excellent workout without the challenges of city streets, busy highways, or dangerous intersections that many people find intimidating when cycling in urban areas. Many riders use this trail for training, commuting, or recreational rides because it provides consistent conditions that help them improve their fitness and cycling skills. You will find that this trail encourages exploration at your own pace while still offering enough challenge to keep experienced cyclists engaged and motivated.

Running and Jogging

The flat profile and predictable surface make this an ideal route for runners training for races or maintaining their fitness throughout the year regardless of weather conditions. You can run the full eleven miles or just portions of it depending on your goals, training schedule, and available time without feeling pressured to complete distances that don’t serve your needs. The consistent surface reduces the risk of ankle injuries and other terrain-related problems that often plague runners on uneven paths or trails. Many serious runners find that this trail becomes their go-to location for training runs because it offers reliability, safety, and natural beauty that make every workout feel rewarding.

Commuting

Local residents use this trail for commuting between Arnold and Baltimore in ways that save money, reduce stress, and improve their daily quality of life. The dedicated path provides a safe alternative to roads and highways for those who prefer human-powered transportation to get to work, school, or other destinations. You will appreciate how commuting on this trail transforms what might otherwise be a stressful daily grind into an enjoyable outdoor activity that energizes you rather than draining you. Many commuters find that the exercise they get along the trail improves their overall health and energy levels in ways that benefit their work and personal lives.

Points of Interest

Historic Communities

As you travel the trail, you’ll pass through neighborhoods and communities that add unique character to your journey and provide context for the region’s development. Keep an eye out for interesting architecture and local landmarks that tell the story of the region’s past while showcasing how Maryland communities have evolved over time. You will discover that these communities offer excellent places to stop for refreshments, explore local businesses, or simply chat with residents who take pride in their neighborhoods. Families with children often use these stops as educational opportunities to learn about local history while enjoying the recreational benefits of the trail.

Natural Areas

The trail passes through various natural settings that provide scenic backdrops and wildlife viewing opportunities for nature enthusiasts of all ages and interests. Spring brings colorful wildflowers and active wildlife that makes the trail feel alive with energy and movement throughout the landscape. Fall offers beautiful foliage displays that transform the entire route into a canvas of reds, oranges, and yellows that photographers and nature lovers cannot resist. You will find that these natural areas provide excellent opportunities for photography, bird watching, and quiet reflection while you enjoy the outdoor experience.

Visitor Information

  • Length: 13.3 miles (21.4 km) from Arnold to Baltimore
  • Surface: Fully paved throughout for maximum accessibility
  • Connects: Arnold to Baltimore through Anne Arundel County
  • Best For: Walking, bicycling, running, commuting, nature observation
  • Accessibility: Wheelchair and stroller friendly with smooth surfaces throughout
  • Parking: Multiple access points with parking available at most trail entries
