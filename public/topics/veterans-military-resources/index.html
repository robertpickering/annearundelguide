Veterans & Military Resources in Anne Arundel County#

As a military spouse who has navigated the VA system and local resources for over 15 years, I know how important having the right support network is for military families. This guide shares everything I’ve learned about military life in Anne Arundel County.

Military Installations & Communities#

Major Military Installations#

Naval Academy (Annapolis):

Basic Facts:

  • Location: 110 Taylor Avenue, Annapolis, MD 21402
  • Phone: 410-293-0111
  • Website: www.usna.edu
  • Population: ~4,500 midshipmen, 600+ faculty/staff
  • Founded: 1845

Community Impact:

  • Major employer in Annapolis
  • Tourism and economic benefits
  • Strong military-civilian community ties
  • Many civilians work at Naval Academy

Housing:

  • OCS (Officer Career School): Some housing available
  • Bachelors: Limited, typically unmarried
  • Families: Most live off-base in Annapolis/neighborhoods
  • On-base housing: Apply through Housing Office

Fort George G. Meade:

Basic Facts:

  • Location: 1020 West Road, Fort Meade, MD 20755
  • Phone: 410-604-1000
  • Website: www.army.mil/emeade
  • Population: ~20,000+ military and civilian
  • Major tenants: NSA, US Army Criminal Investigation Command

Community:

  • Large military presence
  • On-base housing available
  • Commissary and exchange on-post
  • Military clinics and hospitals

Joint Base Andrews:

Basic Facts:

  • Location: 150 Andrew Avenue, Joint Base Andrews, MD 20762
  • Phone: 301-981-2000
  • Website: www Andrews.af.mil
  • Population: ~10,000+ military and civilian
  • Major tenants: 11th Wing, Air Force One operations

Community:

  • Close to DC metropolitan area
  • On-base housing
  • Full base services
  • Military families common

Naval Air Station Patuxent River:

  • Location: Patuxent River, MD (nearby)
  • Phone: 410-342-1000
  • Website: www.naspatuxent.navy.mil

Military Housing Options#

On-Base Housing:

Eligibility:

  • E-1 to E-9: Single family housing based on rank
  • O-1 to O-10: Officer housing available
  • Families: Priority given to dependents
  • Single: Bachelors housing available (limited)

Application Process:

  1. Contact installation housing office
  2. Submit housing application
  3. Await assignment (can be lengthy)
  4. Move-in inspection
  5. Pay monthly housing allowance

Housing Allowments:

  • BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing): Tax-free
  • Amount: Based on rank, location, dependent status
  • 2024 examples:
    • E-5 with dependents: ~$2,000-2,400/month
    • O-3 with dependents: ~$2,800-3,500/month
    • Varies by ZIP code

Off-Base Housing:

Pros:

  • More housing options
  • Often newer construction
  • More community integration
  • No wait lists

Cons:

  • Higher costs in some areas
  • Less military community
  • Commute considerations
  • Security concerns

Popular Military Neighborhoods:

Near Naval Academy:

  • Annapolis: Historic, waterfront, expensive
  • West River: Quiet, families, mid-range
  • Edgewater: Waterfront, affordable
  • Gambrills: Shopping, convenient, mid-range

Near Fort Meade:

  • Glen Burnie: Diverse, affordable, good schools
  • Severn: Family-friendly, newer homes
  • Odenton: Growing, good value
  • Arnold: Military proximity, waterfront options

Near Joint Base Andrews:

  • Camp Springs: Close, affordable
  • Clinton: Family-friendly, newer
  • La Plata: More affordable, longer commute
  • Odenton: Good balance

VA Benefits & Services#

VA Health Care System#

VA Medical Center:

Maryland VA Healthcare System:

  • Location: Baltimore (serves Anne Arundel)
  • Phone: 410-597-6400
  • Website: www.md.vahealthcare.org

Services:

  • Primary care
  • Specialty care
  • Mental health
  • Pharmacy
  • Emergency services (refer to VA)

Eligibility:

  • Honorably discharged veterans
  • Service requirements vary by priority group
  • Income may affect eligibility

Enrollment:

  • Apply online: www.va.gov/health-care/apply
  • Call: 1-877-222-8387
  • In-person: Local VA facility
  • Mail: Application form

VA Priority Groups:

  • Group 1-2: Service-connected disabilities, PRMI
  • Group 3-8: Income-based, other factors

TRICARE Health Insurance#

TRICARE East (covers Maryland):

Plans Available:

TRICARE Prime:

  • Enrollment: Required for active duty
  • Cost: No cost for active duty, small fee for Guard/Reserve
  • Primary Care Manager: Required
  • Referrals: Needed for specialists
  • Best for: Active duty, families wanting managed care

TRICARE Select:

  • Enrollment: Optional
  • Cost: Minimal fees
  • Primary Care: Choose any TRICARE provider
  • Referrals: Not required
  • Best for: Active duty families, Guard/Reserve

TRICARE for Life:

  • Eligibility: Medicare-eligible retirees
  • Secondary to Medicare: Covers gaps
  • Cost: Medicare Part B premium required

TRICARE Reserve Select:

  • Eligibility: Guard/Reserve members
  • Cost: Monthly premium (~$200-400)
  • Coverage: Same as TRICARE Select

TRICARE Young Adult:

  • Eligibility: Adult children
  • Cost: Premium-based
  • Coverage: Full TRICARE benefits

TRICARE Resources:

  • Website: www.tricare.mil
  • Phone: 1-800-444-5445
  • Customer Service: 24/7 support

Enrollment & Benefits#

Benefits Enrollment:

New to Service:

  • Enroll in TRICARE: Immediate
  • ID card: Get DEERS enrollment
  • Dependents: Enroll spouse/children
  • Health screening: Required within 60 days

New to Area:

  • Choose PCP: Find in-network provider
  • Update address: Notify TRICARE
  • Get new ID: Update with address
  • Emergency: Know how to access care

VA Benefits Enrollment:

Step 1: Determine Eligibility

  • Review discharge papers (DD-214)
  • Check service requirements
  • Consider service-connected disabilities

Step 2: Apply

  • Online: www.va.gov/health-care/apply
  • Mail: VA Form 10-10EZ
  • In-person: Local VA facility

Step 3: Provide Documentation

  • DD-214 (discharge papers)
  • Service records
  • Medical records (if applying for disability)
  • Income documentation

GI Bill Benefits#

Post-9/11 GI Bill:

Benefits Include:

  • Tuition: Full public in-state tuition
  • Housing: Monthly stipend (~$2,200-2,600/month)
  • Books: $1,000/year
  • Relocation: $500/month (if moving)
  • Valid: 15 years after separation

Eligibility:

  • 90+ days active duty after 9/11
  • Honorable discharge
  • Varies by service length

Using GI Bill in Maryland:

  • Public universities: Full tuition covered
  • Community colleges: Full tuition
  • Vocational training: Approved programs
  • Private schools: Up to $26,381.35/year (2024 rate)

Transfer Benefits:

  • Transfer to dependents: If eligible
  • Requirements: Service commitment
  • Process: Through military separation

VA Home Loans#

VA Loan Benefits:

  • No down payment: Full financing
  • No PMI: Private mortgage insurance
  • Competitive rates: Typically below market
  • Limited closing costs: VA restrictions
  • No prepayment penalty: Pay anytime

Eligibility:

  • Service requirements: Varies by era
  • Discharge: Must be honorable
  • Certificate of Eligibility: Required

VA Loan Limits:

  • No limit: If qualified (with income)
  • Conforming loan limits: $726,200 (2024)
  • Funding fee: Varies by down payment, service

Anne Arundel County VA Loans:

  • Many local lenders: VA-experienced
  • Process: Similar to conventional
  • Timeline: 30-45 days typical
  • Approval: VA appraisal required

Contact VA Loan Specialists:

  • Local lenders: Research VA experience
  • VA website: www.va.gov/housing_assistance/home-loans
  • Phone: 1-877-829-9712

Disability Compensation#

VA Disability Ratings:

Rating Process:

  • Medical examination: Required
  • Decision: Based on service connection
  • Rating: 0-100% in 10% increments
  • Monthly payment: Based on rating, dependents

2024 Monthly Rates (single veteran):

  • 10%: $171.18
  • 20%: $341.28
  • 30%: $524.31
  • 50%: $1,075.39
  • 100%: $3,737.85

Additional Benefits:

  • Dependents: Additional compensation
  • Housebound: Additional payment
  • Aid and Attendance: Extra for care needs
  • Special monthly: Specific disabilities

Applying for Disability:

  1. File VA Form 21-526EZ
  2. Submit medical evidence
  3. Schedule examination
  4. Wait for decision (30-90 days)
  5. Appeal if denied

Final Thoughts#

Anne Arundel County offers exceptional resources for military families and veterans, with three major installations providing strong support networks and community connections. Whether you’re newly arrived, deployed, or transitioning to civilian life, you’re not alone.

My advice from a military spouse’s perspective:

  • Build your network early
  • Take advantage of all available resources
  • Connect with other military families
  • Stay informed about benefits and entitlements
  • Don’t hesitate to ask for help

You’ve served our country - now let the community serve you.

Welcome to Anne Arundel County, and thank you for your service!
