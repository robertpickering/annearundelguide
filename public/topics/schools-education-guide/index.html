Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS)

Complete Guide to Education in Anne Arundel County

Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS) serves over 50,000 students across the county. This comprehensive guide covers enrollment, school locations, programs, and everything families need to know about education in Anne Arundel County.

School System Overview

System Structure

AACPS operates a comprehensive system from pre-kindergarten through high school:

Educational Levels:

  • Pre-K: 3- and 4-year-old programs
  • Elementary: Kindergarten - 5th grade
  • Middle: 6th - 8th grade
  • High School: 9th - 12th grade
  • Alternative: Special education, adult education

School Zones:

  • Schools are zoned by residential address
  • Some students attend non-zoned schools (magnet, specialty)
  • Boundary changes announced annually

Contact Information:

  • Main Phone: (410) 222-8000
  • Address: 100 County Center, Annapolis, MD 21401
  • Hours: Monday - Friday, 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM

Google Maps: AACPS Administration

Enrollment & Registration

New Student Enrollment

Required Documents:

  1. Proof of Residence:
    • Current lease agreement
    • Deed or mortgage statement
    • Utility bill (within 30 days)
  2. Birth Certificate: Certified copy
  3. Immunization Records: State-mandated vaccines
  4. Previous School Records: Transcripts, report cards
  5. Medical Records: Health screening forms
  6. Photo ID: Parent/guardian identification
  7. Social Security Numbers: For all students

Enrollment Process:

  1. Online Pre-Registration: Start at aacounty.org/aacps/enrollment
  2. Schedule Appointment: Book enrollment appointment
  3. In-Person Registration: Bring all documents to school
  4. Placement Testing: May be required for certain grades
  5. Welcome: Attend orientation meeting

Timeline: Complete enrollment within first 2 weeks of residence

School Zoning

Determining Your School:

  • Zoning by Address: Each residential address has assigned schools
  • Online Zoning Tool: aacounty.org/school-zoning
  • Verify Zoning: Contact school directly for confirmation

Zoning Changes:

  • Annual Review: Boundaries reviewed yearly
  • Notification: Families notified of boundary changes
  • Grandfathering: Some students may remain in original school

How to Check Zoning:

  1. Visit aacounty.org/school-zoning
  2. Enter your address
  3. View assigned schools by grade level
  4. Print confirmation for records

Registration Timeline

For Starting School:

  • Fall Enrollment: February - May for next school year
  • Mid-Year Enrollment: Any time during school year
  • Summer Enrollment: April - August for fall start

For Transfers:

  • Spring Registration: February - March for next school year
  • Summer Processing: June - August
  • Fall Ready: First day of school

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Enrollment Questions

Q: What if I just moved to the county? A: Register at your zoned school with proof of residence. Enrollment can happen any time during the school year.

Q: How do I know which school my child attends? A: Use the school zoning tool on the AACPS website or call the school directly.

Q: Can my child transfer to a different school? A: Transfer requests are considered based on capacity and district policies. Contact your zoned school.

Q: What if my child is new to English? A: Enroll in the ELL program for English language support.

Q: How do I apply for free or reduced-price lunch? A: Complete the annual application on the AACPS website.

Common Academic Questions

Q: What if my child is struggling academically? A: Contact the school counselor for support services and intervention plans.

Q: How do I get my child into advanced classes? A: Gifted identification and teacher recommendations determine placement.

Q: What support is available for special needs students? A: Special education services are available through IEPs and other supports.

Q: How can I get involved in my child’s school? A: Join the PTA/PTO, attend meetings, and volunteer for events.
