Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS)

Anne Arundel County Public Schools is one of the largest and highest-performing school systems in Maryland, serving over 85,000 students across 126 schools.

Enrolling Your Child in AACPS

Step-by-step guide to enrolling your child in Anne Arundel County Public Schools.

<div class="content-grid">
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>📋 Enrollment Requirements</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Proof of residence in Anne Arundel County</li>
      <li>Child's birth certificate</li>
      <li>Immunization records</li>
      <li>Previous school records (if applicable)</li>
      <li>Parent/guardian identification</li>
      <li>Proof of custody (if applicable)</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>📍 School Assignment</h3>
    <p>School assignments are based on your residential address. Use the <strong>School Locator</strong> on the AACPS website to determine your assigned school.</p>
    <p><strong>Note:</strong> Some programs and schools may offer choice enrollment options. Check with the specific school for magnet and specialty program applications.</p>
  </div>
</div>
