Emergency Services

24/7 emergency care and urgent services available throughout Anne Arundel County.

<div class="content-grid">
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🚨 Emergency Medical Services</h3>
    <p>For medical emergencies, always call <strong>9-1-1</strong> immediately.</p>
    <p><strong>Emergency Room Services:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li>24-hour emergency departments at major hospitals</li>
      <li>Trusted emergency medical care</li>
      <li>Advanced trauma care capabilities</li>
      <li>Emergency physicians and specialists</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Note:</strong> Annapolis and Anne Arundel County officials have proposed a jointly funded peak-time medic unit to handle growing EMS demand, demonstrating ongoing commitment to emergency services improvement.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🏥 Urgent Care Options</h3>
    <p>For non-life-threatening conditions that require immediate attention:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Urgent care facilities throughout the county</li>
      <li>Walk-in services at medical centers</li>
      <li>After-hours care for sick or injured family members</li>
      <li>Minor injury and illness treatment</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>When to Use Emergency Room vs. Urgent Care:</strong></p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Emergency Room:</strong> Chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, head injury, stroke symptoms</li>
      <li><strong>Urgent Care:</strong> Minor injuries, fevers, infections, sprains, cuts requiring stitches</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

Regional Healthcare Networks

Healthcare services extend beyond county lines through regional health networks serving the broader Maryland area.

<div class="content-grid">
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🏢 Luminis Health System</h3>
    <p>Luminis Health is a nonprofit regional health system formed when Doctors Community Medical Center joined Anne Arundel Medical Center.</p>
    <p><strong>Service Area:</strong> 1.8 million people in Anne Arundel and Prince George's Counties, the Eastern Shore and beyond.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🏢 University of Maryland Medical System</h3>
    <p>The University of Maryland Medical System (UMMS) comprises more than 14 academic, community and specialty hospitals, as well as several outpatient centers.</p>
    <p><strong>UM Baltimore Washington Medical Center:</strong> Part of the UMMS network, providing comprehensive care to Anne Arundel County.</p>
  </div>
</div>
