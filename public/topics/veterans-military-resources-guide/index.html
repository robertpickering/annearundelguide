#

VA Benefits & Assistance
#

VA Health Care
#

VA Health Care System:

  • Eligibility: Based on service, income, disability
  • Enrollment: Annual or when circumstances change
  • Coverage: Comprehensive medical care
  • Cost: Varies by service-connected disability rating

Health Care Benefits:

  • Primary Care: Primary care physician
  • Specialty Care: Referrals to specialists
  • Mental Health: Counseling, therapy, psychiatric care
  • Pharmacy: Prescription medications
  • Dental: Limited dental coverage
  • Vision: Eye exams, glasses
  • Hospital Care: Inpatient and outpatient care
  • Emergency Care: Emergency services coverage

Enrollment Process:

  1. Online: Apply at va.gov/health-care
  2. Phone: Call VA enrollment at (877) 214-6311
  3. In-Person: Visit local VA facility
  4. Assistance: Veterans Service Office help available

Documents Required:

  • DD-214: Discharge papers
  • ID: Military ID, driver’s license
  • Income: Tax returns, pay stubs
  • Medical: Existing medical records

Disability Compensation
#

VA Disability Benefits:

  • Service-Connected: Compensation for service-connected disabilities
  • Rating: 0-100% disability rating
  • Monthly Payment: Varies by rating and dependents
  • Tax-Free: Benefits are tax-free

Disability Ratings:

  • 10-20%: $160-$350/month
  • 30-50%: $500-$1,000/month
  • 60-80%: $1,200-$2,000/month
  • 100%: $3,500+/month

Application Process:

  1. Submit Claim: Online at va.gov
  2. Evidence: Submit medical records, service records
  3. Examination: VA medical exam if needed
  4. Decision: VA determines rating
  5. Appeal: Appeal if disagree with rating

Disability Appeals:

  • Decision Review: Request decision review
  • Board Appeal: Appeal to Board of Veterans’ Appeals
  • Supplemental Claim: Submit new evidence
  • Assistance: Veterans Service Organizations help

Veterans Service Organizations:

  • DAV: Disabled American Veterans
  • VFW: Veterans of Foreign Wars
  • American Legion: American Legion
  • MABVAC: Maryland Association of Veterans Service Agencies

Education Benefits (GI Bill)
#

Post-9/11 GI Bill:

  • Eligibility: 90+ days active duty after 9/11
  • Benefits: Tuition, housing allowance, books
  • Duration: Up to 36 months
  • Transfer: Can transfer to dependents

GI Bill Benefits:

  • Tuition: Full in-state tuition at public universities
  • Housing: Monthly housing allowance ($2,000-2,500/month)
  • Books: $1,000/year for books
  • Yellow Ribbon: Additional funding for private schools

Using GI Bill:

  1. Apply: At va.gov/education
  2. Certification: School certifies program
  3. Payment: VA pays school directly
  4. Allowance: Housing allowance deposited to veteran

Education Resources:

  • Veterans Education: va.gov/education
  • Maryland State Approving Agency: maryland.gov/saa
  • School Certifying Officials: Contact your school

Home Loans (VA Loans)
#

VA Home Loan Benefits:

  • No Down Payment: Zero down payment option
  • No PMI: No private mortgage insurance
  • Competitive Rates: Below-market interest rates
  • Limited Fees: Lower closing costs
  • Assumable: Loan can be assumed by buyer

VA Loan Features:

  • Loan Limits: No limit for qualified borrowers
  • Credit Requirements: Flexible credit standards
  • Property Types: Single-family, condos, multi-family
  • Refinancing: VA IRRRL available

VA Loan Process:

  1. Pre-Approval: Get VA loan pre-approval
  2. Certificate of Eligibility: Obtain COE from VA
  3. House Hunting: Find property
  4. Application: Submit loan application
  5. Closing: Complete closing process

Resources:

  • VA Loans: va.gov/housingassistance
  • Local Lenders: Contact VA-approved lenders
  • ** counselors:** Free VA loan counseling available

Vocational Rehabilitation & Employment
#

VR&E Program:

  • Eligibility: Service-connected disability 10%+
  • Services: Career counseling, job training, employment support
  • Benefits: Training, education, job placement
  • Duration: Up to 48 months

VR&E Services:

  • Career Counseling: Vocational assessment
  • Job Training: Skills training programs
  • Education: College, technical school
  • Job Placement: Employment assistance
  • Self-Employment: Support for starting business
  • Independent Living: Services for severe disabilities

Application Process:

  1. Submit: File VA Form 28-1900
  2. Assessment: Vocational assessment
  3. Plan: Develop rehabilitation plan
  4. Implementation: Begin services
  5. Completion: Successful job placement

Contact: va.gov/vocational

Frequently Asked Questions
#

Common Veterans Questions
#

Q: How do I apply for VA benefits? A: Apply online at va.gov or visit your local VA facility. Anne Arundel County Veterans Office can assist.

Q: Can I get free healthcare as a veteran? A: Yes, if you meet eligibility requirements. Application required through VA health care system.

Q: What benefits do I get after service? A: Benefits depend on service, discharge, and circumstances. Contact Veterans Service Office for assistance.

Q: Is there help for homeless veterans? A: Yes, HUD-VASH housing vouchers and support services available. Contact Veterans Service Office.

Q: How do I find veteran-friendly employers? A: Use Veterans Job Network, Maryland Department of Labor, or contact Veterans Service Office.

Q: Can I use GI Bill for vocational training? A: Yes, GI Bill can be used for vocational training, technical schools, and certification programs.
