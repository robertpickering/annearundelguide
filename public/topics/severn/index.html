Severn, Maryland

BWI Airport & Severn River Access Community

Severn is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Located between Annapolis and Baltimore, Severn offers convenient access to BWI Airport, Severn River waterfront access, and a mix of residential neighborhoods with strong community amenities.

About Severn

Community Overview

Severn offers:

  • BWI Airport access (major economic driver)
  • Severn River waterfront
  • 15 minutes to Annapolis
  • 35-45 minutes to Baltimore
  • 45-55 minutes to Washington, D.C.
  • 15-20 minutes to BWI Airport
  • Waterfront properties with bay access

The community is known for its waterfront living, proximity to BWI, and family-friendly neighborhoods.

BWI Airport Influence

Severn’s proximity to BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport significantly impacts the area:

  • Employment: Airport jobs provide local employment
  • Convenience: Easy air travel access for residents
  • Economic: Hotels, businesses serving air travelers
  • Traffic: Airport access roads through Severn

The airport is a major economic and lifestyle factor for Severn.

Waterfront Community

Severn features waterfront properties along the Severn River:

  • Boating lifestyle with water activities

The Severn River waterfront is a defining community feature.
