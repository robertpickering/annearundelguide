BWI Trail

Address: Multiple access points throughout Glen Burnie to Odenton, MD

Overview

The BWI Trail is a 21.5-mile multi-use rail trail that runs from Glen Burnie to Odenton, offering a smooth, paved path perfect for walking, running, cycling, and countless other recreational activities. This exceptional trail provides a safe, car-free corridor through Anne Arundel County, connecting neighborhoods, parks, and transit stations in a way that makes outdoor recreation accessible to everyone. You will discover that this trail represents one of the region’s most valuable recreational resources because it combines convenience with natural beauty and unique attractions. The well-maintained surface and strategic routing make it an ideal destination for fitness enthusiasts, families, and casual visitors seeking a peaceful outdoor experience.

Why You’ll Love It

The BWI Trail connects you to the very best of Anne Arundel County’s outdoor recreation in ways that feel both exciting and genuinely relaxing. Whether you’re commuting to work on your bicycle, exploring charming neighborhoods at a leisurely pace, or enjoying a peaceful morning walk along the trail, you’ll discover the perfect way to experience this beautiful area. The paved surface and scenic route make it accessible for all ages and abilities, from toddlers learning to ride bikes to grandparents who enjoy gentle exercise. You will find that this trail offers something truly special for each visit, whether you come for the aircraft watching, fitness activities, or simply the opportunity to breathe fresh air while moving through nature.

What You Can Do Here

Aircraft Watching

Watch commercial airplanes land and take off from remarkably close range in a way that will leave you amazed by the sheer size and power of modern aviation. The trail provides excellent vantage points for aviation enthusiasts and photographers who want to capture spectacular shots of jets climbing into the sky or descending gracefully toward the runway below. Many visitors bring their best cameras to capture spectacular moments when massive aircraft fill the sky overhead while their families enjoy the unique experience together. You will find yourself repeatedly drawn back to this trail because each visit offers different aircraft, different angles, and different stories of people traveling across the world.

Shelter and Comfort

The trail features several shelters and benches strategically placed along the route to ensure you always have comfortable places to rest when you need a break. These thoughtfully positioned spots give you comfortable places to rest, enjoy refreshing refreshments, or simply watch the magnificent aircraft spectacle unfold above you while the wind rustles through nearby trees. The amenities make this trail accessible for visitors of all ages and mobility levels because you never have to push yourself beyond your comfort zone when nature calls for a pause. You will appreciate how these rest areas are positioned at scenic locations where you can take in the views while your legs recover from your journey.

Scenic Environment

Despite being near a busy airport, the trail passes through interesting landscapes that showcase the natural beauty surrounding this incredible transportation hub in surprising ways. You’ll enjoy stunning views of the airport operations while still feeling genuinely connected to nature through the trees, wildlife, and peaceful atmosphere that envelop the path. The contrast between human engineering marvels and natural landscapes creates a unique experience that you won’t find anywhere else in the region. You will discover that this environment provides a perfect backdrop for reflection and relaxation while still offering the excitement of watching aircraft perform their daily routines.

Activities You Can Enjoy

Walking and Jogging

The well-maintained asphalt surface makes this trail perfect for walking, jogging, or running regardless of your fitness level or exercise goals. The flat terrain suits all fitness levels, from casual strollers who want a gentle afternoon walk to serious runners training for marathons and other athletic competitions. You can choose your own pace and distance, making this an ideal location for people who want flexible exercise options without the pressure of busy streets or traffic. Many visitors find that this trail becomes their regular exercise routine because it offers consistency, safety, and natural beauty that make every workout feel rewarding.

Bicycling

Cyclists love the BWI Trail for its smooth pavement and complete lack of vehicle traffic that makes riding feel safe and enjoyable for everyone. The complete loop around the airport offers a substantial ride without dealing with cars, buses, or the stresses of urban cycling that many people find intimidating. Many riders enjoy coming back multiple times to capture different aircraft, enjoy different weather conditions, or simply enjoy the peaceful atmosphere that makes each ride feel special. You will appreciate how the trail’s design encourages leisurely exploration while still providing enough challenge for experienced cyclists who want a good workout.

Nature Observation

The surrounding area provides abundant opportunities to spot local wildlife and plants that thrive in this unique environment near the airport. Bird watchers often visit specifically to see both airport birds like herons and egrets and migratory species passing through the region during seasonal migrations. You will discover that the trail offers excellent opportunities for photographing wildlife while maintaining a safe distance that doesn’t disturb the animals. Families with children often bring field guides and educational materials to turn nature observation into a fun learning experience that everyone can enjoy together.

Visitor Information

  • Length: 21.5 miles total loop (can be broken into shorter segments)
  • Surface: Smooth asphalt throughout suitable for all wheeled activities
  • Best For: Walking, jogging, bicycling, aircraft watching, nature observation
  • Amenities: Shelters and benches along the route for resting and picnicking
  • Parking: Multiple access points throughout the trail with parking available at most entry points
  • Hours: Open dawn to dusk daily for public enjoyment and recreation
