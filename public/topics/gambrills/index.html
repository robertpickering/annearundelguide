Gambrills, Maryland - Complete Local Guide

Gambrills, Maryland

Commercial Hub of South Anne Arundel County

Gambrills is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Known primarily as a major commercial and retail center, Gambrills serves as a hub for shopping, dining, and business services with excellent highway access and convenient location near Annapolis and Baltimore.

Quick Facts

Time Zone EST (UTC-5)

About Gambrills

Community Overview

Gambrills offers:

  • Major commercial and retail center
  • Direct I-95 access
  • 15 minutes to Annapolis
  • 35-45 minutes to Baltimore
  • 50-60 minutes to Washington, D.C.
  • 20-25 minutes to BWI Airport
  • Hotel corridor with numerous accommodations

The area is primarily commercial and residential mix, serving as a commercial hub for the region.

Google Maps: View Gambrills Location

Commercial Development

Gambrills developed as a commercial corridor along MD-174 (Annapolis Road) and I-95:

  • Retail Shopping: Extensive shopping centers and plazas
  • Restaurants: Multiple dining options
  • Hotels: Several major hotel chains
  • Business Services: Professional offices, retail services
  • Residential: Some apartment complexes and townhomes

The commercial development defines Gambrills’ character.

Demographics

Population Statistics

According to U.S. Census Bureau data:

  • 2020 Population: ~10,000
  • Population Density: ~4,000/sq mi
  • Median Age: ~35 years (younger, due to rental properties)
  • Households: ~4,000
  • Average Household Size: 2.2 persons

Source: U.S. Census Bureau

Household Income

  • Median Household Income: ~$75,000 (2020-2024 ACS)
  • Per Capita Income: ~$42,000
  • Renters: High percentage (many apartments)
  • Young Professionals: Significant portion of population

Economic Profile: Above Maryland average, diverse employment base

Education Levels

  • High School Graduates: 88%+
  • Bachelor’s Degree or Higher: 35%+
  • Renters: High percentage of young adults

Housing Market

Current Market Overview

Gambrills housing is primarily rental apartments and townhomes at mid-range prices.

Median Home Prices (2025):

  • Single Family Homes: $450,000 - $650,000
  • Townhomes: $350,000 - $500,000
  • Apartments/Rentals: $1,300 - $2,200/month (rent)

Price per Square Foot:

  • Single Family: $250-$350/sq ft
  • Townhomes: $220-$300/sq ft

Living in Gambrills

Quality of Life

Pros:

  • Excellent highway access (I-95)
  • Extensive shopping and dining options
  • Convenient to Annapolis and Baltimore
  • Hotel corridor with services
  • Young professional atmosphere
  • Good public transit connections
  • Apartment living options

Considerations:

  • Heavy traffic on Annapolis Road
  • Less residential character
  • Limited parks and recreation
  • Commercial feel dominates
  • Can be noisy due to traffic
  • Less community feel than residential areas

Community Vibe

Gambrills attracts:

  • Young professionals wanting convenience
  • Commuters to Baltimore and DC
  • Those prioritizing shopping and dining access
  • Apartment renters
  • Business travelers (hotels)
  • Those wanting I-95 access

Local Amenities

  • Shopping: Extensive retail corridor

  • Hotels: Numerous accommodations
  • Healthcare: Primary care on-site
  • Recreation: Limited locally, extensive nearby
