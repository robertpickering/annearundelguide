BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport
#

Complete Guide to Baltimore-Washington International Airport
#

BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport serves Anne Arundel County and the greater Baltimore-Washington metropolitan area. This comprehensive guide covers everything travelers need to know about BWI, from terminals and airlines to parking, dining, and ground transportation.

Airport Overview
#

History & Namesake
#

Named After:

  • Thurgood Marshall: First African American Supreme Court Justice
  • Service: 1967-1991 on the Supreme Court
  • Baltimore Native: Born and raised in Baltimore
  • Legacy: Civil rights icon, legal pioneer

Airport Name Changes:

  • Original: Baltimore-Washington International Airport
  • 1997: Renamed BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport
  • Recognition: Honoring Justice Marshall’s legacy

Parking & Transportation
#

Parking Options
#

Short-Term Parking:

  • Location: Terminal A/B (adjacent to terminals)
  • Cost: $4/hour, $28/day maximum
  • Best For: Pickups, drop-offs, quick trips
  • Distance: 0-5 minutes walk to terminal

Terminal Parking (Long-Term):

  • Location: Adjacent to terminals
  • Cost: $32/day
  • Best For: 2-7 day trips
  • Distance: 0-5 minutes walk to terminal

Economy Parking:

  • Location: 1 mile from terminals
  • Cost: $20/day
  • Best For: Extended trips
  • Shuttle: Free 24/7 shuttle service (5-10 minutes)
  • Distance: 10-15 minutes to terminal

Valet Parking:

  • Location: Terminal A/B
  • Cost: $45-60/day
  • Best For: Convenience, business travelers
  • Service: Door-to-door service

Reserved Parking:

  • Online Booking: Reserve spaces in advance
  • Cost: Similar to economy rates
  • Guarantee: Guaranteed space available
  • Website: bwiairport.com/parking

Parking Reservations:

  • Online: Book in advance
  • Phone: (410) 859-7254
  • Cancellation: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before
  • Rainy Days: Covered parking available (premium)

Security & TSA
#

TSA Checkpoints
#

Security Locations:

  • Terminal A: 2 checkpoints
  • Terminal B: 2 checkpoints
  • Terminal C: 3 checkpoints
  • Terminal D: 1 checkpoint

TSA Checkpoint Hours:

  • Terminal A: 4 AM - 12 AM
  • Terminal B: 4 AM - 1 AM
  • Terminal C: 4 AM - 12 AM
  • Terminal D: Varies by flight schedule

Security Wait Times:

  • Peak Hours: 6-9 AM, 4-7 PM (20-40 minutes)
  • Off-Peak: 10 AM - 3 PM (10-20 minutes)
  • Weekends: Generally lighter
  • Holidays: Expect longer waits

TSA Guidelines
#

What to Bring:

  • ID: Government-issued photo ID required
  • Boarding Pass: Digital or printed
  • Liquids: 3-1-1 rule (3.4 oz bottles in 1 quart bag)
  • Electronics: Large electronics out of bags
  • Shoes: Remove shoes at checkpoint
  • Jackets: Remove and place in bin

TSA PreCheck:

  • Benefits: Dedicated lane, keep shoes/laptop on
  • Cost: $78 for 5 years
  • Application: Online, in-person appointment
  • Enrollment Centers: Available at BWI
  • Website: tsa.gov/precheck

Global Entry:

  • Benefits: Expedited customs for international travel
  • Cost: $100 for 5 years
  • Includes: TSA PreCheck
  • Application: Online, interview required
  • Website: globalentry.gov

Frequently Asked Questions
#

Common BWI Airport Questions
#

Q: How far is BWI from Annapolis? A: BWI is about 25 miles from Annapolis, approximately 35-45 minutes by car.

Q: What’s the best parking option? A: Economy parking ($20/day) for extended trips. Terminal parking for shorter trips.

Q: Can I get TSA PreCheck at BWI? A: Yes, TSA PreCheck kiosks and enrollment on-site at BWI.

Q: Is WiFi free at BWI? A: Yes, free high-speed WiFi throughout all terminals.

Q: What time should I arrive? A: 2 hours before domestic flights, 3 hours before international.

Q: Are there lounges at BWI? A: Yes, Admirals Club, United Club, Amex Centurion, and Serenity Lounge.

Q: How do I get to BWI from Annapolis? A: By car (35-45 min), MTA bus Route 95, or rideshare ($60-100).

Q: Can I park long-term at BWI? A: Yes, economy parking at $20/day, terminal parking at $32/day.
