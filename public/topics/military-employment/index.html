Job Search Tips

  1. Start Early - Don’t wait until separation
  2. Network Aggressively - Connections matter
  3. Translate Skills - Make military experience understandable
  4. Use Veterans Resources - Free services available
  5. Target Industries - Defense, government, security
  6. Consider Relocation - Some areas have more opportunities
  7. Update Resume - Tailor for civilian audience
  8. Get References - Professional references help
  9. Prepare for Interviews - Practice civilian interview style
  10. Be Flexible - Open to various opportunities
