Sunnydale

Sunnydale is a small residential community in eastern Anne Arundel County, known for its quiet suburban feel and proximity to Chesapeake Bay beaches.

Estimated reading time: 5 minutes

Your First Look at Sunnydale

Sunnydale represents what Southern Anne Arundel County does best: peaceful, rural-style living with waterfront access and rolling countryside. While you’re still in Anne Arundel County with all the county services and schools you’d expect, Sunnydale offers a different pace than the more developed northern and central county areas.

This is the kind of place where you might see farm animals grazing on rolling hills, discover a local vineyard on your drive home, and spend weekends on the water rather than in a mall parking lot. It’s a community that caters to people who want county amenities without the suburban density.

The population is smaller and more spread out than areas like Arnold or Millersville, giving it that countryside feel even though you’re only a short drive from major retail and employment centers.

Where Sunnydale Fits in Southern Anne Arundel County

Sunnydale is located in the southern portion of Anne Arundel County, part of what’s sometimes called “South County.” This area includes several small communities and unincorporated neighborhoods:

  • Nearby communities: Deale, Crofton, Davidsonville, Galesville, Harwood, Shady Side, Tracys Landing, and Friendship
  • South: Chesapeake Bay shoreline
  • North: More developed Anne Arundel areas (Crofton, Annapolis)
  • West: Rural countryside and farm land
  • East: Chesapeake Bay and waterways
  • To Annapolis: 20-30 minutes via Route 2 or Route 3
  • To Baltimore: 40-50 minutes via Route 2 or I-97
  • To DC: 70-90 minutes depending on traffic
  • To BWI Airport: 25-35 minutes

The key access route is Route 2 (Ritchie Highway) which runs north-south through the area, connecting you to Annapolis and beyond.

The Southern Anne Arundel Lifestyle

This is what makes Sunnydale different from other Anne Arundel communities:

Rural atmosphere:

  • Larger lots and more spacing between homes
  • Farm properties and horse farms in the area
  • Less commercial development
  • Slower pace of life

Waterfront access:

  • Proximity to Chesapeake Bay and tributaries
  • Boat ramps and private docks
  • Fishing and water recreation
  • Waterfront views from many properties

Vineyards and local agriculture:

  • Several wineries in Southern Anne Arundel (Robin Hill Farm, The Vineyards at Dodon, Boyd Cru Wines, etc.)
  • Farm-to-table dining options
  • Local produce stands
  • Agricultural heritage

Community events:

  • Farm festivals and harvest celebrations
  • Waterfront events and regattas
  • Local markets and community gatherings
  • More small-town feel than suburban

Schools: AACPS with Rural School Options

Sunnydale residents are served by Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS), but the southern county has some distinctive school options.

Schools serving Southern Anne Arundel:

  • Elementary: Southern Anne Arundel Elementary or nearby options
  • Middle: Southern Anne Arundel Middle School or Crofton area options
  • High: Southern High School (located in the southern county area)

Additional options:

  • Anne Arundel Evening High School (for adults)
  • Phoenix Academy (grade 12 only)
  • Marley Glen School, Ruth Parker Eason School, Central Special School (PK-12 for special needs)

School characteristics:

  • Smaller school sizes than northern county schools
  • Strong community focus
  • More rural/suburban blend of student demographics
  • AACPS Blue Ribbon recognition across the district

Enrollment: Follows standard AACPS enrollment procedures. Southern county families use the county’s school assignment system.

Housing: What to Expect

Sunnydale’s housing reflects the rural Southern Anne Arundel character.

Price ranges:

  • Typical range: Varies from affordable rural homes to luxury waterfront properties
  • Average home values: Generally lower than waterfront Annapolis, but waterfront properties command premium prices
  • Lot sizes: Larger than central county, typical of rural/suburban blend

Property types:

  • Single-family homes: Traditional suburban homes on larger lots
  • Waterfront properties: Homes with boat docks and bay access (premium pricing)
  • Rural estates: Larger properties with acreage, some horse-friendly
  • Farm properties: Working farms or equestrian properties

Neighborhood character:

  • More spread out than suburban developments
  • Mix of year-round residents and seasonal waterfront homeowners
  • Some properties on cul-de-sacs or quiet country roads
  • Waterfront communities with private or public boat access

The housing market here attracts people who want:

  • More space than suburban counties
  • Waterfront or water access
  • Rural feel with county services
  • Privacy and quiet

Shopping and Daily Needs

Southern Anne Arundel has less commercial development than central county, which means you drive for most shopping.

Local shopping:

  • Deale area: Small shopping centers, grocery stores, restaurants
  • Crofton: Crofton Town Center and surrounding retail (10-15 minutes)
  • Annapolis: Specialty shopping, fine dining (20-30 minutes)

Major retail:

  • Glen Burnie: 25-30 minutes west (Target, Costco, big-box retailers)
  • Annapolis: 20-30 minutes east (City Dock specialty shopping)
  • Davidsonville: Small local shopping areas

Restaurants:

  • Local seafood restaurants (waterfront dining)
  • Family-friendly chains in Crofton/Deale
  • Upscale dining in Annapolis (30-minute drive)

The trade-off is that you drive more for shopping, but you get quieter neighborhoods and less traffic in return.

Healthcare Access

Nearby healthcare:

  • Anne Arundel Medical Center (Annapolis): Full-service hospital, 20-30 minutes
  • Urgent care: Options in Crofton and Deale
  • Primary care: Various physician practices throughout Southern Anne Arundel
  • Specialized care: Annapolis and Baltimore for advanced medical services

Healthcare access is good but requires more driving than central county areas. Most routine care is local, with Annapolis as the referral center.

Employment and Economy

Sunnydale residents typically commute to employment centers:

Local employment:

  • Agriculture and viticulture: Vineyards, farms, related businesses
  • Maritime industries: Boat work, marine services, waterfront businesses
  • Small local businesses: Retail, services, restaurants
  • Tourism and hospitality: Seasonal waterfront tourism

Commute destinations:

  • Annapolis: 20-30 minutes (government, naval academy, tourism)
  • Fort Meade: 20-30 minutes (military, federal agencies)
  • Baltimore: 40-50 minutes (metropolitan employment)
  • DC: 70-90 minutes (federal, corporate employment)

The area has fewer major employers than central county, so many residents commute. However, the waterfront and agricultural sectors provide local jobs.

Commuting and Transportation

Driving: Route 2 (Ritchie Highway) is the main north-south corridor. It connects you to Annapolis, Fort Meade, and Baltimore.

  • To Annapolis: 20-30 minutes via Route 2
  • To Baltimore: 40-50 minutes via Route 2 or I-97
  • To Fort Meade: 20-30 minutes via Route 2
  • To BWI Airport: 25-35 minutes via Route 2 or I-97
  • To DC: 70-90 minutes via I-95

Traffic: Route 2 can get congested during peak times, especially the section between Annapolis and Baltimore. Weekends are generally lighter.

Public transit: Limited MTA Maryland bus routes serve the area, with connections to Annapolis and Baltimore. Not as robust as central county transit.

Boating: Many residents have waterfront access and use boats for recreation and sometimes commuting (seasonal/weather dependent).

Outdoor Recreation

This is where Southern Anne Arundel really shines.

Water activities:

  • Chesapeake Bay: Boating, fishing, kayaking, sailing
  • Private docks: Many properties have waterfront access
  • Public boat ramps: County-provided access points
  • Swimming: Private beaches, some public access

Parks and trails:

  • Jones Point Park (nearby): Boat launch, trails, fishing, picnic areas
  • Quiet Waters Park (Crofton): 340 acres, trails, playgrounds, swimming
  • Fort Smallwood Park (Chesapeake Beach): Beach, marina, trails, fishing
  • Sandy Point State Park (Arnold): Beach, fishing, camping, events

Vineyard visits:

  • Robin Hill Farm & Vineyards: Estate wines, tastings, events
  • The Vineyards at Dodon: Davidsonville, tours and tastings
  • Boyd Cru Wines: Local winery with tastings
  • Great Frogs: Winery and restaurant
  • Clyopatra Winery & Vineyard: Tasting room and events
  • Love Point Vineyards and Winery: Waterfront winery
  • Mark Cascia Vineyards: Local production

Farming and agriculture:

  • Working farms in the area
  • Farm stands and pick-your-own operations
  • Agricultural heritage sites
  • Horse farms and equestrian facilities

The Sunnydale Vibe

Sunnydale works well if you want:

  • Rural countryside living with county services
  • Waterfront access and water activities
  • Slower pace of life
  • Larger lots and more privacy
  • Proximity to Annapolis without waterfront pricing (except for actual waterfront)
  • Vineyard and agricultural culture

It might not be ideal if you need:

  • Walkable town centers (you’ll drive for most things)
  • Extensive local shopping (Crofton is 15 minutes away)
  • Robust public transit
  • Quick access to major employment centers (you’ll commute)
  • Urban amenities or nightlife (Annapolis is 30 minutes away)

What Makes Sunnydale Different

vs. Crofton: Crofton is more suburban with town center development. Sunnydale is more rural with waterfront emphasis.

vs. Deale: Deale is more compact with more local shopping. Sunnydale feels more spread out and countryside.

vs. Severna Park: Severna Park is more traditional suburban with strong town center. Sunnydale is more rural and water-focused.

vs. Arnold: Arnold has waterfront but also more suburban development. Sunnydale is more rural/countryside.

Practical Details

County services: All Anne Arundel County services apply—trash, recycling, police, fire, parks, libraries. Response times may be slightly longer due to rural layout, but still solid.

Taxes: County-level property taxes only. No additional municipal taxes since Sunnydale is unincorporated.

Emergency services: Anne Arundel County Fire/Rescue and Police Department provide coverage.

Libraries: Anne Arundel County Public Library serves Southern county. The Crofton Branch or Severna Park Branch are likely the nearest options.

Internet/utility coverage: Standard county infrastructure in developed areas. Rural properties may have well/septic and satellite or microwave internet.

Demographics and Community

Population: Smaller and more spread out than central county communities. Exact figures vary by definition of “Sunnydale” area.

Age distribution: Mix of families, retirees, and professionals who want rural living. More retirees and empty-nesters drawn to waterfront properties.

Lifestyle:

  • Waterfront and boating-oriented
  • Agricultural and rural appreciation
  • Slower pace, more privacy-focused
  • Community gatherings at local venues (vineyards, farms, waterfront)

Community feel: Small-town, neighborly, with stronger connections to immediate neighbors due to spread-out layout. More private and less focused on town-center activities.

Vineyard and Agricultural Culture

Southern Anne Arundel has developed a notable wine country reputation:

Vineyard scene:

  • Multiple wineries within easy driving distance
  • Estate-grown wines from local grapes
  • Tasting rooms and wine clubs
  • Farm-to-table restaurants serving wine-pairing dinners
  • Vineyard weddings and events
  • Wine festivals and harvest celebrations

Agricultural heritage:

  • Working farms still operating in the area
  • Farm stands and local produce
  • Equestrian facilities and horse properties
  • Agricultural tourism (farm visits, petting zoos, etc.)

This culture adds a distinctive character to Sunnydale that you won’t find in other Anne Arundel communities.

Future Development

Southern Anne Arundel is relatively protected from rapid development due to:

  • Rural zoning in many areas
  • Waterfront conservation efforts
  • Agricultural preservation programs
  • Smaller lot requirements in some zones

However, as Annapolis and Baltimore expand, pressure for development increases. The area maintains its rural character through careful planning and zoning.

Bottom Line

Sunnydale is a solid choice for people who want rural living with county services and waterfront access. You get larger lots, slower pace, water activities, and a distinctive agricultural/wine culture that sets it apart from suburban Anne Arundel.

The trade-offs are driving more for shopping and services, commuting for work, and accepting that you’ll be farther from major employment centers. But if you value privacy, waterfront access, and countryside living with county amenities, Sunnydale delivers on those fundamentals.
