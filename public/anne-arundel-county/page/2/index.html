Odenton

Family-Friendly Community Near Fort Meade Odenton is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Known for its proximity to Fort Meade and Joint Base Andrews, excellent schools, and convenient MARC train access, Odenton is a popular choice for military families, government employees, and commuters to Washington, D.C. Parole

Estimated reading time: 5 minutes Your First Look at Parole Parole is what happens when suburban development meets proximity to a historic capital city. Located right at the western edge of Annapolis, this Anne Arundel County community blends residential neighborhoods with major shopping centers, healthcare facilities, and easy access to both the city and the broader metropolitan region. The population sits around 15,000-16,000 residents (based on 2010 census data at 15,922, with growth since then). Pasadena

Chesapeake Bay Waterfront Living Pasadena is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Located on the western shore of the Chesapeake Bay, Pasadena is renowned for its waterfront properties, boating access, and close-knit community atmosphere. The area offers a unique blend of suburban living with exceptional water access. Sunnydale

While you’re still in Anne Arundel County with all the county services and schools you’d expect, Sunnydale offers a different pace than the more developed northern and central county areas. This is the kind of place where you might see farm animals grazing on rolling hills, discover a local vineyard on your drive home, and spend weekends on the water rather than in a mall parking lot.
