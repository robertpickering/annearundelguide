Rental Market

Rental options in Anne Arundel County reflect strong demand and diverse price points.

<div class="content-grid">
  <div class="info-box">
    <h3>🏢 Rental Costs</h3>
    <p><strong>Median Rent:</strong> $1,990 per month</p>
    <p><strong>Fair Market Rent (2-Bedroom):</strong> $1,965 (14.5% higher than Maryland average)</p>
    <p><strong>Units Under $2,000:</strong> Only 33 rental units listed (as of October 2025)</p>
