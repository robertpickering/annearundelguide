Attractions in Anne Arundel County

Discover the best attractions, parks, trails, museums, and historic sites in Anne Arundel County, Maryland.

Attractions in Anne Arundel County#

Anne Arundel County offers something for every visitor — from state parks and waterfront trails to historic sites and museums. Explore our attraction guides to plan your next adventure.

North Point State Park

Address: 4904 Rock Point Road, Parkville, MD 21234 Overview North Point State Park is a 565-acre recreational area that offers visitors diverse outdoor experiences along the Patapsco River while simultaneously preserving the historically significant location where British ships attempted to pass Fort McHenry in September 1814 in an event that directly inspired Francis Scott Key to write America’s national anthem. This park features beautiful wooded areas, waterfront access, and excellent facilities for fishing, boating, and picnicking that make it suitable for visitors seeking outdoor recreation while also providing meaningful historical context that connects local Maryland to broader American history. You will discover that this park offers something genuinely special because it combines the recreational enjoyment of Chesapeake Bay activities with the historical significance of a site that helped define American identity and national unity during the War of 1812. The combination of natural beauty, recreational variety, and historical depth makes it an ideal destination for families, history enthusiasts, and outdoor lovers who want an experience that engages multiple interests simultaneously. ...

Patapsco Valley State Park Areas

Address: Multiple access points throughout Ellicott City and surrounding areas, Anne Arundel County, MD Overview The Patapsco Valley State Park includes multiple access points and areas that offer visitors beautiful natural settings for outdoor recreation while spanning over 16,000 acres of protected Maryland landscape that combines dramatic gorge scenery, peaceful riverside paths, and challenging mountain terrain that appeals to hikers, cyclists, and nature lovers of all skill levels and interests. These park areas feature extensive trail systems, water features including waterfalls and streams, and diverse natural landscapes that provide excellent opportunities for hiking, fishing, mountain biking, road cycling, and nature appreciation in ways that accommodate beginners and experienced outdoor enthusiasts simultaneously. You will discover that this park system offers something genuinely special because the variety of locations and activities makes it easy to find the perfect spot for your interests and skill level while also providing enough depth to justify multiple visits as you explore different areas and try different recreational activities. The combination of geological drama from the deep gorge, ecological diversity from varied habitats, and recreational variety from multiple activity options creates a destination that satisfies diverse outdoor interests while maintaining the natural beauty and peaceful atmosphere that make outdoor recreation genuinely restorative and enjoyable. ...

Patuxent State Park

Address: 4401 Branchview Road, Davidsonville, MD 21035 Overview Patuxent State Park is a 6,700-acre recreational area offering visitors diverse outdoor experiences along the Patuxent River while protecting the upper 12 miles of the Patuxent River’s watershed that provides critical habitat and water quality benefits for the entire region while ensuring the natural area remains intact for future generations to enjoy. This expansive park features camping facilities, extensive hiking trails, water sports access, and abundant wildlife viewing opportunities in a beautiful natural setting that combines rolling woodlands, meadows, and riverside scenery in ways that create multiple ecosystems within the park boundaries. You will discover that this park offers something genuinely special because the conservation focus that protects the watershed creates natural character that enhances every recreational activity while the central Maryland location makes it accessible to residents and visitors throughout the region without requiring extensive travel time. The combination of ecological protection, recreational variety, and scenic beauty makes it an ideal destination for families, outdoor enthusiasts, and nature lovers who want authentic Maryland experiences that connect them to the natural heritage of the Chesapeake Bay watershed. ...

Quiet Waters Park

Address: 1500 Forest Drive, Annapolis, MD 21403 Overview Quiet Waters Park is a 300-acre urban oasis in Annapolis that offers residents and visitors a peaceful retreat from the busier parts of the city while maintaining easy access to all the amenities that make this region so appealing. The park features a stunning lake, extensive walking trails, beautiful gardens, and recreational facilities that make it a perfect destination for outdoor enthusiasts of all ages and fitness levels. You will discover that this park provides exactly what its name suggests - a tranquil space where the stresses of daily life melt away while you reconnect with nature and enjoy peaceful recreation. The well-maintained grounds and thoughtful design make it an ideal destination for families, couples, and individuals seeking a genuine escape from urban intensity without having to travel far from home. ...

Sandy Point State Park

Address: 1100 E College Parkway, Annapolis, MD 21409 Overview Sandy Point State Park is a 786-acre recreational paradise stretching along the northwestern shore of the Chesapeake Bay. This beloved destination offers visitors an incredible array of activities while providing stunning views of the iconic Chesapeake Bay Bridge that connects the region. You will find yourself drawn to this special place where Maryland’s natural beauty combines with accessible recreational opportunities for people of all ages. The park serves as a gateway to Chesapeake Bay experiences that many locals consider essential to understanding why this region remains so beloved by visitors year after year. ...
