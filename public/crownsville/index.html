Crownsville, Maryland

Rural South County Waterfront Community

Crownsville is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Located on the western shore of the Chesapeake Bay, Crownsville offers a unique blend of rural charm and waterfront living with historic character, waterfront properties, and a close-knit community atmosphere.

About Crownsville

Community Overview

Crownsville offers:

  • Chesapeake Bay waterfront access
  • 15 minutes to Annapolis
  • 40-50 minutes to Baltimore
  • 55-65 minutes to Washington, D.C.
  • 25-30 minutes to BWI Airport
  • Rural character with historic charm

The small community is known for its waterfront homes, historic downtown, and strong sense of community.

Google Maps: View Crownsville Location

History

Crownsville developed as a small waterfront community in the early 20th century. The town was named after Crownsville, the former capital of Maryland (now Annapolis). The community maintained its rural character while developing waterfront properties and small-scale residential areas. The historic downtown area retains character from earlier decades.

Waterfront Community

Crownsville’s defining feature is its Chesapeake Bay location:

  • Waterfront homes with private docks
  • Public boat launches for water access
  • Fishing piers and shore fishing
  • Small-scale development preserving rural feel

The water is central to Crownsville’s identity and lifestyle.

Living in Crownsville

Quality of Life

Pros:

  • Exceptional waterfront access and boating lifestyle
  • Small-town, rural character
  • Close proximity to Annapolis

  • Beautiful bay views and sunsets
  • Lower density than other communities

Considerations:

  • Longer commutes to major job centers

Waterfront Living Tips

For New Waterfront Residents

Essential Considerations:

  1. Flood Zone: Check FEMA flood maps for your property
  2. Dock Rights: Verify water rights and dock permissions
  3. Insurance: Get flood and windstorm insurance quotes
  4. Boat Access: Confirm depth and navigation to your dock
  5. Maintenance: Budget for seasonal dock and seawall upkeep
  6. Noise: Consider proximity to boat traffic and marinas
