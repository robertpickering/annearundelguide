Commuting & Transportation in Anne Arundel County

Complete guide to commuting, public transit, ferry services, parking, and transportation options in Anne Arundel County, MD

Commuting & Transportation in Anne Arundel County

As someone who has commuted from Anne Arundel County to both Baltimore and DC for over a decade, every transportation option has been tried. This guide shares what works, what doesn’t, and how to choose the right commute for your life.

Understanding Your Commute Options

Major Highways

Primary Routes Through Anne Arundel County:

MD-2 (Ritchie Highway)

  • Route: Annapolis to Baltimore
  • Usage: Heavy commuter traffic, especially mornings (6-9 AM) and evenings (4-7 PM)
  • Key Intersections: I-95, MD-174, US-50
  • Traffic Patterns:
    • Morning rush: 6:00-9:30 AM
    • Evening rush: 3:30-7:00 PM
    • Avoid if possible during peak hours

MD-3 (Annapolis Boulevard)

  • Route: Connects Annapolis to Glen Burnie
  • Usage: Major arterial road
  • Traffic: Consistent heavy traffic during rush hours

MD-174 (Severn Road)

  • Route: Severn to Glen Burnie
  • Usage: Key route to I-95
  • Alternatives: When MD-2 is congested

US-50 (East West Highway)

  • Route: Baltimore to Annapolis (Chesapeake Bay Bridge)
  • Usage: Scenic but congested route
  • Bridge Toll: $4.00 one-way (cash or Tag)

I-95 Access:

  • Via MD-3: From southern Anne Arundel
  • Via MD-174: From central Anne Arundel
  • Baltimore Beltway (I-295): Accessible from eastern county

I-295 (Baltimore Beltway):

  • Access: Via MD-2, MD-3, or MD-70
  • Usage: Circumferential route around Baltimore
  • Congestion: Heavy during rush hours

Traffic Patterns & Peak Hours

Morning Rush:

  • Primary: 6:00-9:30 AM
  • Secondary: 9:30-10:00 AM (extended rush)
  • Routes to DC: US-50, MD-175, I-95
  • Routes to Baltimore: MD-2, MD-3, I-295

Evening Rush:

  • Primary: 3:30-7:00 PM
  • Secondary: 7:00-8:00 PM
  • Avoid: Leaving work/school during peak times if possible

Weekend Traffic:

  • Generally lighter
  • Chesapeake Bay Bridge congested on summer weekends
  • Holiday travel patterns differ

Congestion Tools:

  • Google Maps (real-time traffic)
  • Waze (crowdsourced traffic)
  • Maryland Transportation Authority website

Public Transportation

Maryland Transit Administration (MTA) Bus Service

MTA Bus Routes Serving Anne Arundel County:

Route 75/76 (Annapolis to BWI Airport):

  • Route: Annapolis - Glen Burnie - BWI Airport
  • Frequency: Every 30-60 minutes
  • Travel Time: 45-60 minutes from Annapolis to BWI
  • Hours: 5:30 AM - 11:30 PM
  • Fare: $2.00 cash, $1.50 with CharmCard
  • Use Case: Airport travel, connecting to Amtrak

Route 30 (Baltimore to Annapolis):

  • Route: Baltimore Downtown - Annapolis
  • Frequency: Hourly
  • Travel Time: 90-120 minutes
  • Hours: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Fare: $4.00 cash, $3.00 with CharmCard
  • Use Case: Commuting to downtown Baltimore

Route 200/201 (County Routes):

  • Coverage: Glen Burnie, Severn, Odenton, Crofton
  • Frequency: Every 30-60 minutes during peak hours
  • Use Case: Local county commuting
  • Fare: $2.00 cash, $1.50 with CharmCard

Route 50 (To BWI and Beyond):

  • Route: Various locations to BWI Airport
  • Frequency: Every 30-60 minutes
  • Fare: $2.00 cash

CharmCard:

  • What it is: Reloadable transit card
  • Where to buy: MTA vending machines, online
  • Cost: $5 for card + load amount
  • Benefits: Lower fares, easier payment
  • Website: www.charmcard.com

MTA Resources:

  • Website: www.mta.maryland.gov
  • Phone: 410-768-7000
  • Trip Planner: www.mta.maryland.gov/planning-trip
  • Real-time Bus Tracking: MTA mobile app

MARC Train Service

MARC Train Lines:

Camden Line (Baltimore to DC):

  • Stations in Anne Arundel: None direct
  • Nearest Station: Baltimore Union Station
  • Travel Time: Baltimore to DC ~45-60 minutes
  • Fares: $5.75-$12.00 one-way
  • Use Case: Commuting to DC from Baltimore

Penn Line (Baltimore to DC):

  • Stations in Anne Arundel: None
  • Nearest Access: Baltimore Union Station
  • Travel Time: Similar to Camden Line
  • Alternative: MARC parking at Baltimore stations

Accessing MARC from Anne Arundel:

  1. Drive to Baltimore: 30-60 minutes depending on location
  2. MTA Bus: Route 30 to Baltimore
  3. Park and Ride: Several MARC stations have parking
  • Union Station (Baltimore): $10-15/day
  • Baltimore Penn Station: $10-15/day

MARC Tickets:

  • Purchase: MARC ticket machines, online, mobile app
  • Fare Types: One-way, round-trip, 10-trip, monthly
  • Website: www.marctrains.com
  • Phone: 410-768-7000

Ferry Services

Chesapeake Bay Ferry:

Annapolis to Baltimore Ferry:

  • Route: Annapolis City Dock to Baltimore Inner Harbor
  • Schedule: Seasonal (typically March - November)
  • Frequency: Every 2-4 hours
  • Travel Time: 45 minutes
  • Fares: $5-8 one-way (pedestrian), vehicle ferry separate
  • Use Case: Scenic commute, weekend travel
  • Website: www.chesapeakebayferry.com

Seasonal Service:

  • Operating Months: March through November
  • Summer: More frequent service
  • Winter: Limited or no service
  • Weather Dependent: Service may cancel in poor conditions

Ferry Tips:

  • Check schedule before planning commute
  • Weather can delay or cancel service
  • Pedestrian-only during summer
  • Consider vehicle ferry for regular commuting

BWI Marshall Airport

BWI Airport Information:

  • Full Name: Baltimore-Washington International Thurgood Marshall Airport
  • Address: 7000 Friendship Road, Glen Burnie, MD 21240
  • Phone: 410-859-7100
  • Website: www.bwiairports.com

Distance from Anne Arundel:

  • Glen Burnie: 10-15 minutes
  • Severn/Odenton: 15-20 minutes
  • Crofton: 20-25 minutes
  • Annapolis: 25-35 minutes

Getting to BWI:

Personal Vehicle:

  • Directions: Follow MD-2/MD-174 to I-95 South
  • Parking Options:
    • Daily Parking: $12-20/day
    • Hourly Parking: $4-6/hour
    • Valet Parking: $25-30/day
    • Economy Parking: $10-12/day (shuttle service)
    • Off-site Parking: $8-15/day (private lots)

MTA Bus Route 75/76:

  • From Annapolis: Direct to BWI
  • Cost: $2.00
  • Travel Time: 45-60 minutes
  • Schedule: 5:30 AM - 11:30 PM
  • No Parking Required

Rideshare/Taxi:

  • Uber/Lyft: $40-80 from most Anne Arundel locations
  • Local Taxi Companies: Available, call ahead
  • Airport Shuttles: Shared ride options available

Amtrak/MARC Connection:

  • BWI Rail Station: Connects to Amtrak and MARC
  • From BWI: Take MTA bus or drive to BWI Rail Station
  • Amtrak to DC: ~30 minutes
  • Amtrak to Baltimore: ~30 minutes

BWI Parking Tips:

  • Book parking in advance: Often cheaper online
  • Consider hotel packages: Hotel + parking discounts
  • Compare off-site lots: Often cheaper than official parking
  • Carpool: Split parking costs

Parking & Transportation Costs

Parking Permits & Fees

Annapolis Downtown Parking:

Daily Parking:

  • Public Garages: $2-4/hour, $15-25/day maximum
  • Street Parking: $1.50-3.00/hour
  • Hours: Typically 9 AM - 9 PM

Annual Parking Permits:

  • Resident Permits: $200-400/year
  • Business Permits: $500-1,000+/year
  • Requirements: Proof of residence/business location

University of Maryland Annapolis:

  • Student Parking: Limited, permit required
  • Faculty/Staff: Permit parking available
  • Visitor: Guest parking available

County Government Parking:

  • 1 County Grounds (Annapolis): Employee parking permits
  • Visitor parking: Available, limited
  • Permits: Required for employees

Toll Roads & Costs

Chesapeake Bay Bridge (US-50):

  • Toll: $4.00 one-way (cash or Tag)
  • Tag (ExpressPass): $4.00 (no cash discount)
  • Direction: Annapolis to Baltimore = eastbound
  • Payment: Tag or cash at toll booth
  • Website: www.chesapeakebaybridge.com

I-95 Express Lanes:

  • Location: Baltimore area (accessible from Anne Arundel)
  • Toll: Variable based on congestion
  • Payment: Tag required
  • Cost: $1-15+ depending on traffic

MD-175 Toll Road:

  • Location: Frederick County (not in AAC)
  • Toll: None currently
  • Note: Some Maryland roads under toll discussions

Toll Tags:

  • EZPass: Accepted on all Maryland toll roads
  • Tag types: Transponder, Tag & Go, Plate Link
  • Where to buy: AAA, participating retailers, online
  • Website: www.ezpassmd.com

Transportation Cost Comparisons

Monthly Cost Estimates (Single Commuter):

Personal Vehicle:

  • Gas: $150-250/month (depends on distance)
  • Parking: $100-300/month (if work provides no free parking)
  • Maintenance: $50-100/month (average)
  • Insurance: $100-200/month
  • Total: $400-850/month

Public Transit:

  • MTA Bus Pass: $75-100/month (unlimited rides)
  • MARC Train: $150-250/month (monthly pass)
  • Total: $75-250/month

Rideshare:

  • Uber/Lyft to work: $3-5/trip each way
  • Daily: $6-10, $120-200/month
  • Not recommended for daily commuting

Carpool:

  • Gas split: $50-100/month
  • Parking: May need permit
  • Total: $50-150/month

Transportation Alternatives

Carpooling Programs

County Carpool Program:

  • Anne Arundel County Commuter Services: Offers carpool matching
  • Website: www.aacounty.org/transportation
  • Benefits: Save money, reduce traffic, carpool lanes access
  • How to Join: Register online, get matched with nearby commuters

Workplace Carpool:

  • Ask coworkers about existing carpools
  • Create your own carpool group
  • Use workplace bulletin boards or intranet
  • Consider neighborhoods for matching

Carpool Benefits:

  • Cost savings: Split gas and parking
  • HOV access: Some roads allow carpool-only lanes
  • Stress reduction: Someone else can drive
  • Environmental: Fewer cars on road

Ride-Sharing

Uber:

  • App: Uber app (iOS, Android)
  • Services: UberX, Uber Black, Uber Pool
  • Cost: $3-5 base + per mile/km
  • Best for: Occasional trips, not daily commute

Lyft:

  • App: Lyft app (iOS, Android)
  • Services: Lyft Standard, Lyft XL, Shared
  • Cost: Similar to Uber
  • Local drivers: Many Anne Arundel drivers

Ride-Share Tips:

  • Compare apps: Different apps have different prices
  • Shared options: Cheaper but longer travel time
  • Schedule rides: Book in advance for reliability
  • Regular routes: Some drivers offer regular commute rates

Bicycle & Walking

Bike Infrastructure:

Bike Lanes:

  • Major roads have bike lanes
  • Ritchie Highway (MD-2): Bike lanes in sections
  • Annapolis Boulevard (MD-3): Some bike lanes
  • US-50: Limited bike lanes

Chesapeake Bay Trail:

  • **Route:**沿着 the Chesapeake Bay
  • Use: Recreational cycling, some commuting
  • Surface: Paved in most sections
  • Connections: Links multiple Anne Arundel communities

Bike-Friendly Areas:

  • Annapolis: Very bike-friendly, flat terrain
  • Severna Park: Some bike lanes, flat areas
  • Crofton: Emerging bike infrastructure
  • Glen Burnie: Limited bike infrastructure

Walking:

  • Annapolis: Very walkable downtown
  • Residential areas: Sidewalks available
  • Safety: Varies by neighborhood
  • Weather: Maryland weather limits year-round walking

Bike Commuting Tips:

  1. Check weather: Maryland summers are hot/humid
  2. Pack change of clothes: Shower at work if possible
  3. Bike storage: Ensure secure storage at work/home
  4. Route planning: Use bike-friendly routes
  5. Safety gear: Helmet, lights, reflective clothing
  6. Maintenance: Regular tune-ups
  7. Bike share: Some areas have bike share programs

Final Thoughts

Choosing the right transportation option for your Anne Arundel County commute depends on your destination, budget, schedule, and personal preferences. Most residents drive, but public transit, carpooling, and other alternatives can save money and reduce stress.

My recommendation: Track your commute costs for a month, try alternatives when possible, and be flexible with your schedule to avoid peak traffic times.

The best commute is the one that works for your life, not the fastest route.
