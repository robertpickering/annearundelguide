Odenton

Family-Friendly Community Near Fort Meade Odenton is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. Known for its proximity to Fort Meade and Joint Base Andrews, excellent schools, and convenient MARC train access, Odenton is a popular choice for military families, government employees, and commuters to Washington, D.C. Parole

Estimated reading time: 5 minutes Your First Look at Parole Parole is what happens when suburban development meets proximity to a historic capital city. Located right at the western edge of Annapolis, this Anne Arundel County community blends residential neighborhoods with major shopping centers, healthcare facilities, and easy access to both the city and the broader metropolitan region. The population sits around 15,000-16,000 residents (based on 2010 census data at 15,922, with growth since then).
