Major Cities & Communities

Anne Arundel County encompasses 625 square miles with vibrant communities, each offering unique character, amenities, and lifestyles.

🏛️

Annapolis

Maryland's historic capital, home to the U.S. Naval Academy, charming waterfront downtown, and upscale neighborhoods. Perfect for those seeking history, culture, and coastal living.

  • • Population: ~41,000
  • • Median Home: $650K+
  • • Known for: Historic sites, waterfront
Learn more →
⚓

Odenton

Family-friendly community near Fort Meade and MARC train access. Excellent schools, convenient shopping, and easy highway access make this a top choice for military families.

  • • Population: ~25,000
  • • Median Home: $450K+
  • • Known for: Military proximity, schools
Learn more →
🌉

Glen Burnie

Anne Arundel's most populous town, offering diverse neighborhoods, excellent shopping at Annapolis Road, and direct access to I-95 and the Baltimore-Washington Parkway.

  • • Population: ~72,000
  • • Median Home: $380K+
  • • Known for: Shopping, highways
Learn more →
🎭

Pasadena

Waterfront community with beautiful Chesapeake Bay views, marina access, and a mix of historic charm and modern development. Great for boating enthusiasts and professionals.

  • • Population: ~27,000
  • • Median Home: $550K+
  • • Known for: Waterfront, marinas
Learn more →
🌲

Severn

Rural charm meets suburban convenience. Known for large properties, equestrian facilities, and proximity to BWI Airport. Ideal for families seeking space and tranquility.

  • • Population: ~28,000
  • • Median Home: $520K+
  • • Known for: Rural living, airport access
Learn more →
🏆

Severna Park

Consistently ranked among Maryland's best communities. Award-winning schools, small-town charm, and strong community spirit make this a top choice for families.

  • • Population: ~20,000
  • • Median Home: $600K+
  • • Known for: Schools, community
Learn more →
🏖️

Crofton

South County's largest community, offering diverse housing, excellent parks, and waterfront access to Chesapeake Bay. Strong sense of community and convenient amenities.

  • • Population: ~33,000
  • • Median Home: $480K+
  • • Known for: Parks, waterfront
Learn more →
