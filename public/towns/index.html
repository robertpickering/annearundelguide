Arnold

Estimated reading time: 5 minutes Your First Look at Arnold Moving to Arnold means waking up to water views and ending your day with sunset over the Chesapeake Bay. This Anne Arundel County community sits on the Broadneck Peninsula, where the Magothy River meets the Severn River, giving residents boat access to the bay without leaving the neighborhood. When I first toured Arnold, what struck me most wasn’t the waterfront homes or the proximity to Annapolis—it was how the community actually functions. You’ve got the Baltimore & Annapolis Trail running right through, which means you can bike to schools, shops, or even Annapolis itself without touching a highway. It’s one of those rare places where the commute might be a bike ride instead of a 40-minute drive. ...
