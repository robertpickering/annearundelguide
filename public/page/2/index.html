Welcome to Anne Arundel County#

Anne Arundel County is a vibrant region on the Chesapeake Bay with over 1.5 million residents, diverse communities, and a mix of urban, suburban, and rural living. From the historic capital of Annapolis to the family-friendly neighborhoods of Severna Park and Crofton, our county offers something for everyone.

Things to Do#

Anne Arundel County offers 15+ major attractions including state parks, trails, historic sites, and museums:

  • Sandy Point State Park - 6,500 acres on the Chesapeake Bay with beaches, camping, and kayaking
  • BWI Trail - 5.7-mile paved recreational trail from Glen Burnie to Arundel Mills
  • Baltimore & Annapolis Trail - 14-mile rail trail connecting Annapolis to Baltimore
  • Maryland State House - Oldest capitol building in continuous legislative use
  • Quiet Waters Park - Zoo, nature center, playground, and tennis courts in Annapolis
  • Historic London Town & Gardens - Restored 18th-century colonial buildings and gardens
  • North Point State Park - Historic site where the Star-Spangled Banner was inspired
  • Patuxent River State Park - 6,700 acres with camping, hiking, and water sports
