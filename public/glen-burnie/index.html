BWI Trail

Address: Multiple access points throughout Glen Burnie to Odenton, MD Overview The BWI Trail is a 21.5-mile multi-use rail trail that runs from Glen Burnie to Odenton, offering a smooth, paved path perfect for walking, running, cycling, and countless other recreational activities. This exceptional trail provides a safe, car-free corridor through Anne Arundel County, connecting neighborhoods, parks, and transit stations in a way that makes outdoor recreation accessible to everyone. You will discover that this trail represents one of the region’s most valuable recreational resources because it combines convenience with natural beauty and unique attractions. The well-maintained surface and strategic routing make it an ideal destination for fitness enthusiasts, families, and casual visitors seeking a peaceful outdoor experience. ...

Love Point State Park

Address: 2701 Love Point Road, Stevensville, MD 21666 Overview Love Point State Park is a picturesque waterfront park that offers visitors beautiful views of the Chesapeake Bay and excellent recreational opportunities in a setting that feels both intimate and authentically Maryland. This serene destination provides a perfect setting for fishing, boating, picnicking, and enjoying nature’s beauty along Maryland’s scenic coastline while avoiding the crowds that often diminish the experience at larger, more famous destinations. You will discover that this park punches above its weight despite its smaller size because it combines quality facilities, natural beauty, and recreational variety in ways that satisfy visitors of all ages and interests.
