# VA Benefits for Veterans in Anne Arundel County

Veterans living in Anne Arundel County have access to extensive VA benefits. From healthcare to education to housing assistance, this guide covers what's available near Fort Meade and Joint Base Andrews.

## VA Healthcare System

### VA Medical Facilities Near Anne Arundel

**Baltimore VA Medical Center** - Primary facility for Anne Arundel veterans  
- Address: 2901 W. Lanham Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21215  
- Phone: 410-455-5000  
- Full medical services  
- About 30-40 minutes from most of Anne Arundel  
- **Official VA Facilities**: [va.gov/health-care/find-locations](https://www.va.gov/find-locations/)

**Fort Meade VA Clinic** - Primary care on post  
- Location: On Fort Meade installation  
- Primary care services  
- Specialty clinics  
- For service members and veterans with base access  
- **VA Clinic Locator**: [va.gov/health-care/find-locations](https://www.va.gov/find-locations/)

### VA Enrollment

**Who Can Enroll:**
- Most veterans with any military service
- Income-based priority groups
- Service-connected disabilities get higher priority
- Certain exposures (Agent Orange, radiation) qualify

**How to Enroll:**
- Online at [va.gov/health-care/apply](https://www.va.gov/health-care/apply)
- Mail application at [va.gov/forms/va/VA10-10EZ.asp](https://www.va.gov/forms/va/VA10-10EZ.asp)
- In person at VA facility
- Through accredited representative

**Required Documents:**
- DD-214 (discharge papers)
- Social Security number
- Income information (if applicable)
- Other military service documentation

### Priority Groups

**Priority 1:** Service-connected disability 50%+  
**Priority 2:** Service-connected disability 30-40%  
**Priority 3:** Service-connected disability 10-20%  
**Priority 4:** Disabled veterans, Purple Heart recipients  
**Priority 5:** Income-based eligibility  
**Priority 6-8:** Lower income veterans, various categories

### Healthcare Benefits

**Included Services:**
- Primary care visits
- Hospital care
- Emergency care
- Prescriptions
- Mental health services
- Rehabilitation services
- Preventive care
- Dental (limited)
- Vision (limited)

**Prescription Costs:**
- Varies by priority group
- Income-based copays
- Annual thresholds
- Some veterans pay no copay

### Mental Health Services

**Services Available:**
- Individual therapy
- Group therapy
- PTSD treatment
- Substance abuse treatment
- Suicide prevention
- Crisis intervention

**Special Programs:**
- PTSD residential treatment
- Military sexual trauma treatment
- Suicide prevention crisis line: 988 (press 1)

### Emergency Care

**VA Emergency Coverage:**
- VA pays for emergency care at non-VA facilities
- Must be eligible for VA care
- Notify VA within specific timeframe
- Authorization requirements vary

**When VA Covers Emergency:**
- Emergency condition
- No VA facility nearby
- Care necessary to stabilize
- Notify VA within 72 hours if possible

## Disability Compensation

### Service-Connected Disability

**What It Is:**
- Monthly payment for disabilities from military service
- Percentage rating (0-100%)
- Higher rating = higher payment

**How to Apply:**
- File claim through VA
- Provide medical evidence
- VA determines rating
- May need medical exam

**Current Rates (2026):**
- 0%: No payment (service-connected but no compensation)
- 10%: ~$165/month
- 30%: ~$485/month
- 50%: ~$1,000/month
- 70%: ~$1,600/month
- 100%: ~$3,700+/month

**Note:** Rates change annually with cost of living adjustments

### Dependent Benefits

**Additional Compensation:**
- Spouse, children, dependent parents
- Additional payments for dependents
- Increases with more dependents
- Apply through VA

### Special Monthly Compensation

**Higher Payments For:**
- Need of aid and attendance
- Housebound status
- Specific severe disabilities
- Multiple disabilities

## VA Education Benefits

### GI Bill Options

**Post-9/11 GI Bill:**
- 36 months of benefits
- Tuition and fees covered at public schools
- Housing allowance
- Book stipend
- Transferable to family members

**Montgomery GI Bill:**
- 36 months of benefits
- Fixed monthly payment
- Can use at various schools
- Some benefits less generous than Post-9/11

**vocational Rehabilitation (Chapter 31):**
- For service-connected disabilities
- Career counseling
- Training and education
- Employment assistance

### Using GI Bill in Maryland

**Public Universities:**
- Maryland public schools - covered tuition
- Anne Arundel Community College - covered
- Maryland State College - covered

**Private Schools:**
- Maximum tuition coverage
- May not cover full private school cost
- Compare benefits carefully

### VA Education Counseling

**Fort Meade VRA:**
- Veterans Readjustment Advisors
- On-post counseling
- Help with benefit claims
- Education planning

**VA Education Hotline:** 1-888-442-4551

## VA Home Loans

### VA Loan Benefits

**No Down Payment:**
- Many veterans buy with zero down
- No private mortgage insurance
- Competitive interest rates
- Limited closing costs

**Eligibility:**
- Minimum service requirements
- DD-214 requirements
- Certificate of Eligibility (COE) available

**How to Get COE:**
- Online through VA website
- Through lender
- Mail application

### Using VA Loan in Anne Arundel

**Market Conditions:**
- Competitive market
- VA loans very popular
- Some lenders specialize in VA
- VA appraisal requirements

**VA Appraisal:**
- Property must meet standards
- Safety and livability requirements
- Different from conventional appraisal

### Funding Fee

**Upfront Fee:**
- Percentage of loan amount
- Varies by service type
- Varies by first use vs subsequent
- Can be financed into loan

**Exemptions:**
- Service-connected disability
- Purple Heart recipients
- Surviving spouses

## VA Life Insurance

### SGLI (Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance)

**Active Duty:**
- Automatic coverage
- $400,000 maximum
- Low cost

### VGLI (Veterans' Group Life Insurance)

**After Separation:**
- Convert SGLI to VGLI
- Renewable coverage
- Term life insurance
- Available for life

### Other VA Life Insurance

**SOCI:** Service-Disabled Veterans Life Insurance  
**SCVS:** Service-Disabled Veterans Insurance

## Tips for Veterans

1. **Keep DD-214 safe** - Essential for all benefits
2. **Enroll in VA healthcare** - Even if not using immediately
3. **Apply for disability** - If service-connected
4. **Use VA education** - GI Bill benefits
5. **Consider VA home loan** - No down payment option
6. **Register for Selective Service** - Required for some benefits
7. **Keep records organized** - All military documentation
8. **Use accredited reps** - Free help with claims
9. **Stay informed** - Benefits change, stay updated
10. **Connect with other vets** - Support networks helpful
