# Tricare Health Insurance for Military Families

If you're military or a military family member living in Anne Arundel County, Tricare provides your health insurance. This guide covers your options near Fort Meade, Naval Academy, and Joint Base Andrews.

## Tricare Options

### Tricare Prime

**Best For:** Active duty service members and families

**Features:**
- No monthly premium (for service members)
- $0-200 per person annual enrollment fee (for families)
- Choose primary care manager (PCM)
- Referrals needed for specialists
- Low copays for care
- Comprehensive coverage

**Enrollment:**
- Automatic for active duty
- Enroll through milConnect website
- Choose PCM from network providers
- Can change PCM once per year

### Tricare Select

**Best For:** Reserve members, National Guard, some retirees

**Features:**
- No monthly premium
- Deductible and coinsurance
- No referral needed for specialists
- More flexibility in providers
- Higher out-of-pocket costs than Prime

**Enrollment:**
- Must enroll online
- Pay enrollment fee if applicable
- Choose providers from network
- Can see any Tricare-authorized provider

### Tricare for Life

**Best For:** Military retirees age 65+

**Features:**
- Supplemental to Medicare
- No additional premium
- Medicare pays first, Tricare pays second
- No network restrictions
- Minimal out-of-pocket costs

**Eligibility:**
- Must have Medicare Part A and B
- Must be enrolled in Tricare
- Automatic enrollment for some

### Tricare Reserve Select

**Best For:** Selected Reserve members

**Features:**
- Monthly premium
- Similar to Tricare Select
- No enrollment fee
- Coverage when on active duty orders

### Tricare Young Adult

**Best For:** Adult children ages 23-26

**Features:**
- Monthly premium
- Extended coverage beyond age 21
- Similar benefits to Tricare Select

## Using Tricare in Anne Arundel

### Routine Care

**Primary Care:**
- Choose PCM from network
- Annual physical included
- Preventive care covered
- Routine labs and tests

**Specialist Care:**
- Prime needs referral from PCM
- Select doesn't need referral
- Authorization may be required for some services
- Ask about prior authorization

### Emergency Care

**24/7 Coverage:**
- Go to nearest emergency room
- AAMC handles most military emergencies
- Call Tricare if stable to discuss facility

**Authorization:**
- Emergency services don't need pre-authorization
- Follow-up care may need authorization
- Contact Tricare within timeframe if required

### Prescriptions

**Pharmacy Benefits:**
- Military pharmacy (Tricare Pharmacy) - lowest cost
- Retail pharmacy network - moderate cost
- Mail-order available
- Generic drugs preferred

**Copays:**
- Varies by plan and pharmacy type
- Generic vs brand name pricing
- 90-day supply discounts

### Mental Health Services

**Covered Services:**
- Individual counseling
- Family therapy
- Psychiatric services
- Substance abuse treatment

**Access:**
- Tricare network mental health providers
- Some require referral
- Crisis services covered

## Costs & Out-of-Pocket

### Prime Costs

**Active Duty Members:**
- No premium
- Enrollment fee waived
- Low or no copays

**Family Members:**
- Annual enrollment fee ($200 individual, $400 family)
- Copays for visits ($0-30 typically)
- Out-of-pocket maximum applies

### Select Costs

**Deductibles:**
- Individual deductible varies
- Family deductible higher
- Reset annually

**Coinsurance:**
- After deductible, pay percentage
- Tricare pays rest
- Out-of-pocket max protects you

### Prescription Costs

**Network Pharmacy:**
- Generic: $5-15
- Preferred brand: $15-35
- Non-preferred: $35-55

**Mail Order:**
- 90-day supply discounts
- Same tiers as retail
- Convenient for maintenance meds

## Tips for Tricare Users

1. **Choose PCM carefully** - This doctor coordinates your care
2. **Know your plan** - Understand what's covered and costs
3. **Use network providers** - Saves money significantly
4. **Get referrals when needed** - Prime requires them for specialists
5. **Keep documentation** - Save all records and receipts
6. **Update contact info** - Keep Tricare informed of changes
7. **Ask about prior authorization** - Some services need approval first
8. **Use mail order** - Often cheaper for maintenance medications

## Common Questions

### Can I see any doctor?

**Prime:** Must use network, except emergencies  
**Select:** Can see any Tricare-authorized provider  
**For Life:** Any provider accepting Medicare

### What if I move?

**Update address immediately** - Tricare has regional offices  
**New PCM required** - Choose from new area network  
**Coverage continues** - No gap in coverage

### How do I change plans?

**Open enrollment periods** - Annual window  
**Qualifying life events** - Marriage, birth, move, etc.  
**Request through milConnect**
