## School System Overview

### System Structure

AACPS operates a comprehensive system from pre-kindergarten through high school:

**Educational Levels:**
- **Pre-K:** 3- and 4-year-old programs
- **Elementary:** Kindergarten - 5th grade
- **Middle:** 6th - 8th grade
- **High School:** 9th - 12th grade
- **Alternative:** Special education, adult education

**School Zones:**
- Schools are zoned by residential address
- Some students attend non-zoned schools (magnet, specialty)
- Boundary changes announced annually

## Enrollment & Registration

### New Student Enrollment

**Required Documents:**
1. **Proof of Residence:**
   - Current lease agreement
   - Deed or mortgage statement
   - Utility bill (within 30 days)
2. **Birth Certificate:** Certified copy
3. **Immunization Records:** State-mandated vaccines
4. **Previous School Records:** Transcripts, report cards
5. **Medical Records:** Health screening forms
6. **Photo ID:** Parent/guardian identification
7. **Social Security Numbers:** For all students

**Enrollment Process:**
1. **Online Pre-Registration:** Start at [aacounty.org/aacps/enrollment](https://www.aacps.org/enrollment)
2. **Schedule Appointment:** Book enrollment appointment
3. **In-Person Registration:** Bring all documents to school
4. **Placement Testing:** May be required for certain grades
5. **Welcome:** Attend orientation meeting

**Timeline:** Complete enrollment within first 2 weeks of residence

### School Zoning

**Determining Your School:**
- **Zoning by Address:** Each residential address has assigned schools
- **Online Zoning Tool:** [aacounty.org/school-zoning](https://www.aacps.org/schools)
- **Verify Zoning:** Contact school directly for confirmation

**Zoning Changes:**
- **Annual Review:** Boundaries reviewed yearly
- **Notification:** Families notified of boundary changes
- **Grandfathering:** Some students may remain in original school

**How to Check Zoning:**
1. Visit [aacounty.org/school-zoning](https://www.aacps.org/schools)
2. Enter your address
3. View assigned schools by grade level
4. Print confirmation for records

### Registration Timeline

**For Starting School:**
- **Fall Enrollment:** February - May for next school year
- **Mid-Year Enrollment:** Any time during school year
- **Summer Enrollment:** April - August for fall start

**For Transfers:**
- **Spring Registration:** February - March for next school year
- **Summer Processing:** June - August
- **Fall Ready:** First day of school

## Academic Programs

### Advanced Placement (AP)

**AP Course Availability:**
- **High Schools:** All county high schools offer AP courses
- **Number of AP Courses:** 15-25 per high school
- **AP Classes Available:**
  - Mathematics: Calculus AB/BC, Statistics, Pre-AP Algebra
  - Science: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Science
  - English: AP Literature, AP Language
  - History: AP US History, AP World History, AP Government
  - Foreign Languages: French, Spanish, German, Latin
  - Arts: AP Art History, AP Music Theory

**AP Benefits:**
- **College Credit:** Earn college credit in high school
- **College Admissions:** Demonstrates academic rigor

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Common Enrollment Questions

**Q: What if I just moved to the county?**
A: Register at your zoned school with proof of residence. Enrollment can happen any time during the school year.

**Q: How do I know which school my child attends?**
A: Use the school zoning tool on the AACPS website or call the school directly.

**Q: Can my child transfer to a different school?**
A: Transfer requests are considered based on capacity and district policies. Contact your zoned school.

**Q: What if my child is new to English?**
A: Enroll in the ELL program for English language support.

**Q: How do I apply for free or reduced-price lunch?**
A: Complete the annual application on the AACPS website.

### Common Academic Questions

**Q: What if my child is struggling academically?**
A: Contact the school counselor for support services and intervention plans.

**Q: How do I get my child into advanced classes?**
A: Gifted identification and teacher recommendations determine placement.

**Q: What support is available for special needs students?**
A: Special education services are available through IEPs and other supports.

**Q: How can I get involved in my child's school?**
A: Join the PTA/PTO, attend meetings, and volunteer for events.
