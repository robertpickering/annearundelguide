## Frequently Asked Questions

### Common Cost of Living Questions

**Q: Is Annapolis expensive?**
A: Yes, Annapolis has the highest housing costs in the county, with median home prices around $750,000.

**Q: What's the cheapest area in Anne Arundel County?**
A: Glen Burnie and Gambrills tend to have the most affordable housing options.

**Q: Is rent or buy better in Anne Arundel?**
A: Depends on your timeline. Buying builds equity; renting is more flexible. Calculate based on how long you'll stay.

**Q: What are the property taxes like?**
A: Maryland has relatively low property taxes at 0.95%, among the lowest in the state.

**Q: How much should I budget for groceries?**
A: $300-500/month for singles, $800-1,200/month for families of 4.

**Q: Are there property tax exemptions?**
A: Yes, Maryland offers homestead exemptions and other property tax relief programs.
