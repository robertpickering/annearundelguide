# Veterans & Military Resources in Anne Arundel County

**Estimated reading time: 6 minutes**

Anne Arundel County has a significant military presence, with Fort Meade (NSA headquarters), Joint Base Andrews, and Naval Academy nearby. If you're a veteran or military family, there are plenty of resources available - but you need to know where to look and what's actually available to you.

## Military Installations Nearby

### Fort Meade

Home to the National Security Agency and major military commands. This is the largest military installation in the county.

**What it offers:**
- Base Exchange (BX) with military discounts
- Commissaries for grocery shopping
- Medical facilities for active duty and some retirees
- Morale, welfare, and recreation programs
- Child and youth services

**Access:** Some areas require valid military ID for entry. Check base access requirements before visiting.

### Joint Base Andrews

Home to the 113th Wing of the DC Air National Guard and other units. Close to DC and offers good access to the national capital area.

**Services:**
- Air National Guard programs
- Family support services
- Morale and recreation activities

**Access:** Base ID required for most facilities.

### Naval Academy (Annapolis)

While not an active military installation in the traditional sense, the Naval Academy has extensive support programs for military families and veterans.

**Resources:**
- Library and educational resources
- Community events and programs
- Support services for families

## VA Benefits

### Health Care

**VA Medical Center:** The VA Medical Center in Baltimore serves Anne Arundel County residents. It offers primary care, mental health services, and specialty care.

**What's covered:**
- Primary care visits
- Mental health services
- Prescription medications (with copays)
- Specialty care (with referral)
- Emergency care (with authorization)

**Enrollment:** You need to enroll in VA health care to access benefits. Apply online at VA.gov or visit the Baltimore VA Medical Center.

### TRICARE (for Active Duty and Retirees)

**TRICARE Prime:** Full coverage with a small copay. Requires enrollment and a primary care physician.

**TRICARE Select:** PPO-style coverage with higher out-of-pocket costs but more flexibility.

**What's covered:**
- Routine checkups
- Specialist visits (with referral for Prime)
- Mental health services
- Prescription medications
- Emergency care

**Local providers:** Many healthcare providers in Anne Arundel County accept TRICARE. Check the TRICARE website for a list of local participating providers.

### GI Bill

The GI Bill provides education benefits for veterans and their families.

**Types:**
- Post-9/11 GI Bill: Covers tuition, housing, and books for eligible veterans
- Montgomery GI Bill: Monthly payments for education and training
- Vocational Rehabilitation: For veterans with service-connected disabilities

**Anne Arundel Community College (AACC):** Located in Odenton, AACC accepts GI Bill benefits and has programs for veterans.

**Local universities:** University of Maryland Eastern Shore, University of Maryland Baltimore County, and others in the region accept GI Bill benefits.

## Local Support Programs

### Veterans Service Organizations

**Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW):** Multiple posts in the county offer support and community for veterans.

**American Legion:** Active posts throughout Anne Arundel County provide veteran services and community events.

**Disabled American Veterans (DAV):** Offers assistance with VA claims and benefits.

**Fisher House Foundation:** Provides temporary housing for families of veterans receiving medical treatment.

### County Resources

**Anne Arundel County Veterans Service Office:**
- Location: 1699 West Street, Annapolis
- Services: Help with VA claims, benefits applications, and local resources
- Phone: 410-222-3318
- Website: aacounty.org/veterans

**What they help with:**
- VA claim applications
- Benefits eligibility questions
- Local resource connections
- Employment assistance

### Mental Health and Counseling

**Veterans Crisis Line:** Call 988 and press 1, or text 838255. Available 24/7, confidential, and free.

**Maryland VA Counseling:** Free counseling services for veterans and their families.

**Local providers:** Many private therapists in the county specialize in military-related mental health issues.

### Employment and Career

**Veterans Employment Resource Center:** Located in Annapolis, provides job placement assistance and career counseling for veterans.

**Job fairs:** The county regularly hosts military and veteran job fairs. Check the county website for upcoming events.

**Training programs:** Various organizations offer job training and certification programs specifically for veterans.

## Housing and Financial Support

### VA Home Loans

**What it covers:**
- Purchase loans with no down payment
- Refinance options
- Construction loans
- No private mortgage insurance (PMI) required

**Benefits:**
- Competitive interest rates
- No down payment required
- Lower closing costs than conventional loans
- No PMI means lower monthly payments

**Local lenders:** Several banks and mortgage companies in Anne Arundel County specialize in VA loans.

### Financial Assistance Programs

**Emergency Financial Assistance:** Available for veterans facing financial hardship. Contact the Veterans Service Office for details.

**Tuition Assistance:** Some organizations offer scholarships and grants for veterans pursuing education.

### Transportation

**Military parking:** Free parking at many county facilities for military vehicles with valid ID.

**Discounts:** Some businesses offer military discounts on fuel, food, and other services.

## Schools and Family Support

### Military Family Programs

**Child Development Centers:** Available at Fort Meade and Joint Base Andrews for active duty families.

**Youth programs:** After-school programs, sports, and activities for military children.

**Relocation assistance:** Programs to help military families moving to or from the area.

### Education Support

**School liaison officers:** Help military children transition between schools and enroll in AACPS.

**Educational programs:** Some organizations offer tutoring and academic support for military children.

## My Perspective

Having lived near Fort Meade and worked with military families in the county, I've seen firsthand how valuable these resources are. But here's the reality: many veterans don't know they're eligible for benefits, and even fewer use them.

The biggest mistake I see veterans make is not asking for help. There are plenty of people in the county who want to support you - Veterans Service Officers, local nonprofits, VA representatives, and community organizations.

Don't be proud enough to do everything alone. These resources exist specifically to support you and your family.
