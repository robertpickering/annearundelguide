## Emergency Services

### When to Use Emergency Room

**Use 911 or go to ER for:**
- Chest pain or difficulty breathing
- Severe injuries
- Stroke symptoms
- Severe bleeding
- Loss of consciousness
- Poisoning or overdose
- Serious burns
- Broken bones with deformity

**Use Urgent Care for:**
- Minor injuries
- Fever and illness
- Minor cuts requiring stitches
- Urinary tract infections
- Minor fractures
- Rashes
- Eye infections

### Emergency Room Wait Times

**Vary by time and day:**
- Weekday evenings typically busiest
- Weekends can have longer waits
- Major trauma cases priority
- Ask about expected wait when arriving

### All Three Anne Arundel Hospitals

**AAMC:** Annapolis - main county hospital  
**Holy Cross:** Glen Burnie - full service  
**MedStar:** Multiple clinic locations - primary and specialty care

## Primary Care Physicians

### Finding a Doctor

**Insurance First:**
- Check your insurance provider network
- Use insurer's "find a doctor" tool
- Verify doctor accepts your plan
- Call office to confirm accepting new patients

**Questions to Ask:**
- Do you accept my insurance?
- Are you accepting new patients?
- How long until first appointment?
- Where is office located?
- Office hours that work for you?
- How to reach doctor after hours?

### Pediatric Care

**Children's Healthcare Providers:**
- AAC Pediatric Associates
- Children's National Hospital affiliated clinics
- Individual pediatric practices
- AACPS school-based health centers

**Finding Pediatrician:**

- Visit office before choosing

- Confirm hospital privileges

## Health Records

### Getting Medical Records

**From Doctor Offices:**
- Request in writing
- May have fee for copies
- Can request electronic copies
- Usually within 30 days

**From Hospitals:**
- Medical records department
- More extensive records
- May take longer to process
- Photo ID required

### Sharing Records

**When Moving:**
- Request transfer to new doctor
- Provide new doctor's info
- Some offices handle transfer
- Keep copies for yourself

## Tips for Healthcare in Anne Arundel

1. **Know your insurance** - Understand coverage before needing care
2. **Keep records** - Maintain personal health file
3. **Find a PCP** - Primary care doctor for routine care
4. **Know emergency vs urgent** - Don't ER for minor issues
5. **Ask about costs** - Get estimates for procedures
6. **Use preventive care** - Stay up to date on screenings
7. **Keep pharmacy consistent** - Helps with drug interaction monitoring
8. **Update insurance info** - Keep current with changes
