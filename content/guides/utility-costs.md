# Anne Arundel County Utility Costs

Understanding utility costs in Anne Arundel County is essential for budgeting. This guide covers electric, water, gas, internet, and other utility expenses throughout the county.

## Electric Service

### AEP Maryland

**Service Area:**
- Most of Anne Arundel County
- Primary electric provider
- Generation and distribution

**Contact:**
- Phone: 410-486-1234
- Website: www.aepmaryland.com
- Outages: 410-486-1234

### Electric Rates

**Rate Structure:**
- Base rate per kilowatt-hour
- Seasonal variations
- Time-of-use pricing available
- Different tiers based on usage

**Average Costs:**
- Summer (high usage): $120-200/month
- Winter (heating): $100-180/month
- Spring/Fall (moderate): $80-140/month
- Annual average: $100-160/month

### Heating Costs

**Electric Heating:**
- Higher winter costs
- Heat pumps efficient
- Baseboard heating expensive
- Thermostat management helps

**Average Winter Bill:**
- Smaller home: $100-140
- Medium home: $140-200
- Large home: $200-300+

### Cooling Costs

**Air Conditioning:**
- Summer usage varies
- Heat waves increase costs
- Programmable thermostats help
- Energy-efficient units cost less

**Average Summer Bill:**
- Moderate AC use: $120-160
- Heavy AC use: $160-240
- Minimal AC: $80-120

### Energy Efficiency Tips

**Reduce Costs:**
- Programmable thermostat
- Energy-efficient appliances
- LED lighting
- Weatherization
- Regular maintenance
- Seal drafts

**Save Money:**
- Use off-peak hours
- Lower heat in winter, higher in summer
- Use ceiling fans
- Unplug electronics
- Energy audit available

## Natural Gas

### Availability

**Service Area:**
- Most of county covered
- PG&W (Potomac Gas) provides service
- Some areas no gas service
- Check availability for specific address

**Provider:**
- Potomac Gas Utility Company
- Natural gas distribution
- Billing and service

### Gas Rates

**Rate Structure:**
- Per therm or CCF
- Seasonal pricing
- Customer charge
- Usage-based pricing

**Average Costs:**

**Winter (heating):**
- Small home: $80-120/month
- Medium home: $120-180/month
- Large home: $180-300+/month

**Summer (cooking, water heater):**
- Basic usage: $30-50/month
- Heavy usage: $50-80/month

### Annual Gas Costs

**Typical Year:**
- Heating season (6 months): $600-1,500
- Non-heating season: $200-400
- Total annual: $800-1,900

**Factors Affecting Cost:**
- Home size
- Insulation
- Age of heating system
- Thermostat settings
- Weather severity

## Water & Sewer

### Anne Arundel County Water

**Provider:**
- County Department of Public Works
- Water distribution
- Sewer services

**Contact:**
- Phone: 410-222-3258
- Email: dpw@aacounty.org
- Website: www.aacounty.org/departments/public-works

### Water Rates

**Residential Rates:**
- Base charge
- Per 1,000 gallons
- Tiered pricing
- Minimum charge

**Average Monthly Costs:**

**Small household (1-2 people):**
- Water: $40-60/month
- Sewer: $40-60/month
- Total: $80-120/month

**Medium household (3-4 people):**
- Water: $60-90/month
- Sewer: $60-90/month
- Total: $120-180/month

**Large household (5+ people):**
- Water: $90-150+/month
- Sewer: $90-150+/month
- Total: $180-300+/month

### Water Quality

**Testing:**
- County provides annual water quality report
- Regular testing
- Public reports available
- Private testing available

**Issues:**
- Generally good quality
- Older homes may have plumbing issues
- Well water different requirements

### Sewer Services

**Sewer Coverage:**
- County sewer in most areas
- Septic in some rural areas
- Different rates for each

**Septic Systems:**
- Homeowner responsibility
- Regular pumping required
- Inspection needed for sale
- Maintenance costs extra

## Internet Service

### Providers in Anne Arundel

**Comcast Xfinity:**
- Most widespread coverage
- Cable internet
- Speeds up to 1,200 Mbps
- Bundle options available

**Verizon Fios:**
- Fiber optic
- Available in select areas
- High speeds
- Reliable service

**Google Fiber:**
- Expanding service area
- Check availability
- Competitive pricing
- Fast speeds

**CenturyLink:**
- DSL and fiber options
- Available in some areas
- Moderate speeds
- Bundle options

**Satellite:**
- HughesNet
- Viasat
- Rural areas
- Higher latency

### Internet Costs

**Basic DSL (25-50 Mbps):**
- $40-60/month
- Adequate for browsing
- Limited for streaming

**Cable (100-400 Mbps):**
- $50-80/month
- Good for most households
- Streaming, gaming ok

**Fiber (300-1,000 Mbps):**
- $60-100/month
- Fast speeds
- Good for heavy use
- Price varies by speed

**Promotions:**
- Often first-year discounts
- Bundle discounts
- Check current promotions
- Read fine print

### Installation Costs

**Setup Fees:**
- $50-100 typically
- Often waived with promotions
- Equipment fees may apply
- Check current fees

**Equipment:**
- Modem rental: $10-15/month
- Router rental: $10-15/month
- Buy own equipment to save
- Sometimes included in promotion

## Total Utility Costs

### Monthly Estimates by Home Type

**Apartment (1 bedroom):**
- Electric: $60-100
- Water: $30-50 (often included)
- Gas: $30-60 (if included, less)
- Internet: $50-80
- Total: $140-290/month

**Apartment (2 bedroom):**
- Electric: $80-120
- Water: $40-60 (often included)
- Gas: $40-80 (if included, less)
- Internet: $50-80
- Total: $170-340/month

**Small House (2-3 bedroom):**
- Electric: $100-160
- Water: $60-100
- Gas: $50-150 (seasonal)
- Internet: $50-80
- Total: $260-490/month

**Medium House (3-4 bedroom):**
- Electric: $120-180
- Water: $80-120
- Gas: $70-200 (seasonal)
- Internet: $50-80
- Total: $320-580/month

**Large House (5+ bedroom):**
- Electric: $160-250
- Water: $100-150
- Gas: $100-300+ (seasonal)
- Internet: $50-100
- Total: $410-700+/month

### Seasonal Variations

**Summer:**
- Higher electric (AC)
- Water usage may increase
- Pool costs if applicable
- Overall: +$50-150/month

**Winter:**
- Higher electric or gas (heating)
- Water usage similar
- Possible freeze concerns
- Overall: +$50-200/month

**Spring/Fall:**
- Lowest utility costs
- Moderate electric usage
- No heating or heavy cooling
- Overall: baseline costs

## Setting Up Utilities

### Move-In Checklist

**Contact Providers:**
- Electric: 3-5 days before move
- Gas: 2-3 days before move

- Internet: 1-2 weeks before (installation)
- Trash: Confirm service

**Required Information:**
- Social Security number
- Driver's license
- Proof of address
- Employment info
- Credit check

**Deposits:**
- Electric: $100-300
- Gas: May require deposit
- Internet: Equipment fees
- Check current requirements
