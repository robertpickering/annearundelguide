# Anne Arundel County Housing Calculator

Understanding housing costs in Anne Arundel County is crucial for budgeting. This guide helps you estimate mortgage payments, rent, and total housing affordability.

## Housing Market Overview

### Anne Arundel County Housing

**Market Type:**
- Competitive suburban market
- Near Baltimore and DC
- Strong demand
- Prices higher than national average

**Price Ranges:**

**Condos/Apartments:**
- 1 bedroom: $180,000-300,000
- 2 bedroom: $250,000-400,000
- 3 bedroom: $350,000-500,000+

**Single Family Homes:**
- 2-3 bedroom: $350,000-550,000
- 4 bedroom: $500,000-800,000+
- Luxury/waterfront: $800,000-2,000,000+

**Rental Ranges:**
- 1 bedroom apartment: $1,400-2,000/month
- 2 bedroom apartment: $1,800-2,800/month
- 3 bedroom house: $2,500-4,000+/month

- No PMI

- Most common

**5% Down:**

**0% Down (VA):**

- Funding fee applies
- No PMI

## Property Taxes

### Anne Arundel County Property Tax

**Rate:**
- Approximately 1.08-1.15% of assessed value
- Rate changes annually
- Assessed value typically 10% of market value
- Effective rate lower than nominal

**Calculation:**
- Assessment = Market value × 10%
- Tax = Assessment × Tax rate
- Effective tax = ~1.08-1.15% of market value

**Examples:**

**$300,000 Home:**
- Assessment: $30,000
- Annual tax: ~$3,300
- Monthly tax: ~$275

**$400,000 Home:**
- Assessment: $40,000
- Annual tax: ~$4,400
- Monthly tax: ~$367

**$500,000 Home:**
- Assessment: $50,000
- Annual tax: ~$5,500
- Monthly tax: ~$458

**$600,000 Home:**
- Assessment: $60,000
- Annual tax: ~$6,600
- Monthly tax: ~$550

**$700,000 Home:**
- Assessment: $70,000
- Annual tax: ~$7,700
- Monthly tax: ~$642

### Tax Reductions

**Homestead Credit:**
- Limits tax increases
- Apply annually
- Primary residence only
- Significant savings over time

**Other Credits:**
- Disabled veterans
- Senior citizens
- Specific programs
- Check eligibility

## Homeowners Insurance

### Insurance Costs

**Factors Affecting Cost:**
- Home value
- Location
- Coverage amount
- Deductible
- Claims history
- Age of home

**Average Annual Costs:**

**$300,000 Home:** $800-1,200/year  
**$400,000 Home:** $1,000-1,500/year  
**$500,000 Home:** $1,200-1,800/year  
**$600,000 Home:** $1,400-2,100/year  
**$700,000 Home:** $1,600-2,400/year  

**Monthly Costs:**

**$300,000 Home:** $70-100/month  
**$400,000 Home:** $85-125/month  
**$500,000 Home:** $100-150/month  
**$600,000 Home:** $115-175/month  
**$700,000 Home:** $130-200/month  

### Insurance Coverage

**Dwelling Coverage:**
- Should equal rebuilding cost
- Not same as market value
- Consider construction costs

**Personal Property:**
- Typically 50-70% of dwelling
- Covers belongings
- Special limits for items

**Liability Coverage:**
- $100,000-300,000 standard
- Consider higher coverage
- Umbrella policy option

### Mortgage Insurance

**PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance):**
- Required if less than 20% down
- 0.5-1.5% of loan annually
- Can be cancelled at 20% equity
- Monthly cost

**FHA Mortgage Insurance:**
- Upfront premium
- Monthly premium
- May last entire loan term
- Different rules than PMI

## Affordability Guidelines

- Housing expenses ≤ 28% of gross income
- Total debt ≤ 36% of gross income

## Tips for Budgeting

1. **Calculate total cost** - Not just mortgage
2. **Factor in utilities** - Often overlooked
3. **Budget for maintenance** - 1% rule
4. **Include insurance** - Required and important
5. **Consider taxes** - Annual expense monthly
6. **Add closing costs** - Upfront expense
7. **Account for HOA** - If applicable
8. **Plan for appreciation** - May affect refinancing
9. **Consider future needs** - Family growth, job changes
10. **Get pre-approved** - Know what you can afford
