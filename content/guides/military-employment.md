# Military Employment & Career Resources

Military families in Anne Arundel County have unique employment challenges and opportunities. From military spouse employment to veteran hiring preferences to transition support, this guide covers career resources.

## Employment Near Fort Meade

### Fort Meade Employers

**NSA (National Security Agency):**
- Major employer at Fort Meade
- Federal positions available
- Clearance preferred but not always required
- Competitive federal benefits

**Military Installations:**
- Civilian positions at military bases
- Federal employment benefits
- Security clearance may be required
- Apply through USAJobs

**Defense Contractors:**
- Many companies serving military
- Various clearance levels needed
- Competitive salaries
- Range from large to small companies

### Federal Employment

**USAJobs.gov:**
- Federal job listings
- Filter by location (Anne Arundel County)
- Create profile and resume
- Apply directly online

**Federal Benefits:**
- Strong benefits package
- Retirement plans
- Health insurance
- Paid leave
- Job security

**Security Clearance:**
- Some positions require clearance
- Sponsorship available for eligible positions
- Processing takes time
- Clearance valuable for private sector

## Job Search Tips

4. **Use Veterans Resources** - Free services available

7. **Update Resume** - Tailor for civilian audience
8. **Get References** - Professional references help
9. **Prepare for Interviews** - Practice civilian interview style
