# BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport

## Airport Overview

### History & Namesake

**Named After:**
- **Thurgood Marshall:** First African American Supreme Court Justice
- **Service:** 1967-1991 on the Supreme Court
- **Baltimore Native:** Born and raised in Baltimore
- **Legacy:** Civil rights icon, legal pioneer

**Airport Name Changes:**
- **Original:** Baltimore-Washington International Airport
- **1997:** Renamed BWI Thurgood Marshall Airport
- **Recognition:** Honoring Justice Marshall's legacy

### Airport Facilities

**Terminals:**
- **Terminal A:** American Airlines, Delta Air Lines
- **Terminal B:** Southwest Airlines, JetBlue Airways
- **Terminal C:** United Airlines, Alaska Airlines, Spirit Airlines
- **Terminal D:** International flights, charter airlines

**Amenities:**
- **WiFi:** Free throughout airport
- **Charging Stations:** Throughout all terminals
- **Lounges:** Multiple airline lounges
- **Shopping:** 100+ retail stores
- **Dining:** 50+ restaurants and bars
- **Art:** Extensive art collection throughout terminals

### Airport Services

**Traveler Services:**

- **Medical:** First aid stations in terminals
- **ATMs:** Throughout all terminals
- **Currency Exchange:** International terminal
- **Pet Relief Areas:** Designated areas in each terminal

## Security & TSA

### TSA Checkpoints

**Security Locations:**
- **Terminal A:** 2 checkpoints
- **Terminal B:** 2 checkpoints
- **Terminal C:** 3 checkpoints
- **Terminal D:** 1 checkpoint

**TSA Checkpoint Hours:**
- **Terminal A:** 4 AM - 12 AM
- **Terminal B:** 4 AM - 1 AM
- **Terminal C:** 4 AM - 12 AM
- **Terminal D:** Varies by flight schedule

**Security Wait Times:**
- **Peak Hours:** 6-9 AM, 4-7 PM (20-40 minutes)
- **Off-Peak:** 10 AM - 3 PM (10-20 minutes)
- **Weekends:** Generally lighter
- **Holidays:** Expect longer waits

### TSA Guidelines

**What to Bring:**
- **ID:** Government-issued photo ID required
- **Boarding Pass:** Digital or printed
- **Liquids:** 3-1-1 rule (3.4 oz bottles in 1 quart bag)
- **Electronics:** Large electronics out of bags
- **Shoes:** Remove shoes at checkpoint
- **Jackets:** Remove and place in bin

**TSA PreCheck:**
- **Benefits:** Dedicated lane, keep shoes/laptop on
- **Cost:** $78 for 5 years
- **Application:** Online, in-person appointment
- **Enrollment Centers:** Available at BWI
- **Website:** [tsa.gov/precheck](https://www.tsa.gov/precheck)

**Global Entry:**
- **Benefits:** Expedited customs for international travel
- **Cost:** $100 for 5 years
- **Includes:** TSA PreCheck
- **Application:** Online, interview required
- **Website:** [globalentry.gov](https://www.globalentry.gov/)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Common BWI Airport Questions

**Q: How far is BWI from Annapolis?**
A: BWI is about 25 miles from Annapolis, approximately 35-45 minutes by car.

**Q: What's the best parking option?**
A: Economy parking ($20/day) for extended trips. Terminal parking for shorter trips.

**Q: Can I get TSA PreCheck at BWI?**
A: Yes, TSA PreCheck kiosks and enrollment on-site at BWI.

**Q: Is WiFi free at BWI?**
A: Yes, free high-speed WiFi throughout all terminals.

**Q: What time should I arrive?**
A: 2 hours before domestic flights, 3 hours before international.

**Q: Are there lounges at BWI?**
A: Yes, Admirals Club, United Club, Amex Centurion, and Serenity Lounge.

**Q: How do I get to BWI from Annapolis?**
A: By car (35-45 min), MTA bus Route 95, or rideshare ($60-100).

**Q: Can I park long-term at BWI?**
A: Yes, economy parking at $20/day, terminal parking at $32/day.
