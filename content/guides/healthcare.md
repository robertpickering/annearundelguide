### Emergency Care

**When to Use Emergency Services:**
- **Life-Threatening:** Chest pain, difficulty breathing
- **Severe Injuries:** Major trauma, deep wounds
- **Stroke Symptoms:** Facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty
- **Heart Attack Signs:** Chest pain, shortness of breath
- **Severe Allergic Reactions:** Anaphylaxis

- **Mental Health Crisis:** Suicidal thoughts, self-harm

**Emergency Departments:**

- **Prevention:** Early detection of health issues
- **Screening:** Age-appropriate cancer screenings
- **Vaccinations:** Stay up-to-date on immunizations
- **Health Planning:** Discuss lifestyle and health goals
- **Relationship:** Build ongoing relationship with doctor

**Wellness Exam Components:**
- **Physical Exam:** Vital signs, general health assessment
- **Medical History:** Review of health conditions and medications
- **Screening Tests:** Blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes
- **Cancer Screening:** Mammograms, colonoscopy, PSA, Pap smears
- **Lifestyle Counseling:** Diet, exercise, smoking cessation
- **Vaccinations:** Flu shots, boosters

**Recommended Screening Schedule:**
- **Blood Pressure:** Every 2 years (or annually if elevated)
- **Cholesterol:** Every 4-6 years (or as recommended)
- **Diabetes:** Every 3 years (or as recommended)
- **Mammogram:** Every 1-2 years (age 40+)
- **Colonoscopy:** Every 10 years (age 50+)
- **Pap Smear:** Every 3-5 years (age 21+)
- **Prostate Screening:** Discuss with doctor (age 50+)

### Understanding Insurance

**Key Insurance Terms:**
- **Premium:** Monthly insurance payment
- **Deductible:** Amount you pay before insurance kicks in

- **Coinsurance:** Percentage you pay (20% typical)

- **Network:** In-network vs. out-of-network providers
- **Prior Authorization:** Approval needed for certain services

**Finding In-Network Providers:**
- **Insurance Website:** Provider directory
- **Call Insurance:** Verify network status
- **Ask Provider:** Confirm they accept your insurance

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Common Healthcare Questions

**Q: How do I choose a primary care physician?**
A: Consider location, insurance coverage, availability, and patient reviews. Schedule a meet-and-greet appointment to evaluate the doctor.

**Q: What if I can't afford medical care?**
A: CVPH offers sliding fee scales based on income. Also explore Medicaid eligibility and hospital financial assistance programs.

**Q: Can I see a specialist without a referral?**
A: Some insurance plans require referrals. Check your plan requirements and contact your primary care provider.

**Q: What should I bring to my first doctor's visit?**
A: Insurance card, photo ID, list of medications, medical history, and any relevant test results.

**Q: How do I get my medical records?**
A: Request from your provider's office using their medical records release form. There may be a copying fee.

**Q: What if I have a medical emergency at night?**
A: Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department (AAMC in Annapolis).

**Q: Is telehealth covered by insurance?**
A: Most insurance plans cover telehealth visits. Check with your specific plan for coverage details.

**Q: How do I find an in-network specialist?**
A: Use your insurance provider's directory online or call customer service for assistance.
