# Settling In: Your First 30 Days in Anne Arundel County

Welcome to Anne Arundel County! Whether you're relocating for military service at Naval Academy, Fort Meade, or Joint Base Andrews, working in the DC/Baltimore corridor, or simply seeking Maryland's coastal lifestyle, this guide will help you navigate your first month in our beautiful county.


---

## Week 1 Priorities: The Essentials

### Registering Your Vehicle with Maryland MVA

Getting your out-of-state vehicle registered in Maryland is one of your first legal requirements.

**Maryland Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA) Requirements:**

- **Address:** Multiple MVA branch locations throughout Anne Arundel County
  - **Glen Burnie MVA:** 1001 Crain Highway, Glen Burnie, MD 21061 (Phone: 410-922-3000)
  - **Annapolis MVA:** 200 Calvert Street, Annapolis, MD 21401 (Phone: 410-263-2450)
  - **Severna Park MVA:** 750 Ritchie Highway, Severna Park, MD 21146
- **Website:** [www.marylandmva.com](https://mva.maryland.gov)
- **Hours:** Monday-Friday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (some locations open Saturdays)

**Documents Required:**
- Out-of-state title and registration
- Valid driver's license
- Maryland proof of insurance
- Vehicle inspection (may be required)
- Social Security Number
- Payment for registration fees ($20-$100+ depending on vehicle)

**Key Facts:**
- Maryland requires vehicle registration within **60 days** of becoming a resident
- You must obtain Maryland plates within this period
- Vehicle inspection required before MD plates issued
- Use tax applies (6% of vehicle value) unless credits from previous state

### Setting Up Utilities

Getting utilities connected is critical before or immediately upon moving in.

**Electric Service - AEP Maryland:**
- **Service Area:** Most of Anne Arundel County
- **Phone:** 410-486-1234
- **Website:** [www.aepmaryland.com](https://www.aepmaryland.com)
- **Hours:** Monday-Friday 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Outage Reporting:** Call 410-486-1234 or report online
- **New Service:** Call at least 3 days before move-in date
- **Initial Deposit:** $100-$300 (may be waived with good credit)

**Water & Sewer - Anne Arundel County Department of Public Works:**
- **Phone:** 410-222-3258
- **Email:** dpw@aacounty.org
- **Website:** [www.aacounty.org/departments/public-works](https://www.aacounty.org/departments/public-works)
- **Billing Questions:** 410-222-3258
- **Emergency:** 24/7 for water main breaks

**Internet & Cable Providers:**
- **Comcast Xfinity:** Most locations covered (Website: [comcast.com](https://www.comcast.com))
- **Verizon Fios:** Available in select areas
- **Google Fiber:** Expanding service area (check availability)
- **CenturyLink:** DSL and fiber options
- **Satellite:** HughesNet, Viasat (rural areas)

**Average Internet Costs in AAC:**
- Basic DSL: $40-60/month
- Cable (50-200 Mbps): $50-80/month
- Fiber (300-1000 Mbps): $60-100/month

## Week 2-4 Priorities: Building Your Life

### Getting Your Maryland Driver's License

**Maryland MVA Requirements:**

**When Required:**
- You MUST obtain MD license within **60 days** of becoming resident
- Required for vehicle registration

**Documents Needed:**
- Current out-of-state license
- Proof of Maryland address (lease, utility bill, bank statement)
- Social Security Card
- Birth certificate or passport
- Proof of residency (2 documents)

**Testing:**
- Written knowledge test (may be waived with valid out-of-state license)
- Vision screening required
- Road test usually waived if valid out-of-state license

**Fees:**
- Non-commercial license: $20 (4-year) or $17 (1-year)
- Real ID available (recommended for air travel)

### Registering to Vote

**Anne Arundel County Board of Elections:**
- Address: 1140 Crain Highway, Glen Burnie, MD 21061
- Phone: 410-222-3100
- Website: [www.electionboard.org](https://www.electionboard.org)
- Hours: Monday-Friday 8:30 AM - 4:30 PM

**Voter Registration:**
- Can register online at [VoteMaryland.MD.gov](https://vote.maryland.gov)
- Can register by mail
- Can register in-person at MVA, Board of Elections
- Must register **21 days** before election
- Maryland same-day registration available during early voting period

**Polling Places:**
- Assigned based on address
- Check online for location
- Early voting locations available

### Registering Children in School

**Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS):**
- Website: [www.aacps.org](https://www.aacps.org)
- Phone: 410-222-4500
- Enrollment Office: 1 County Grounds, Annapolis, MD 21401

**Enrollment Requirements:**
- Proof of residency (lease, deed, utility bill)
- Child's birth certificate
- Immunization records
- Previous school records (transcripts, report cards)
- Individualized Education Program (IEP) if applicable
- Parent/guardian photo ID

**Enrollment Process:**
1. Determine your school zone based on address
2. Contact school or district office
3. Submit documents and complete forms
4. Schedule health exam if needed
5. Attend orientation (varies by school)

**School Options:**
- zoned public schools
- Magnet programs (application required)
- Private schools
- Homeschooling (registration required)

**School Performance Information:**
- GreatSchools.org provides ratings
- Niche.com has school rankings
- State report cards available through Maryland State Department of Education
