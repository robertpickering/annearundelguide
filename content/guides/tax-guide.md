# Anne Arundel County Tax Guide

Understanding taxes in Anne Arundel County is essential for budgeting and financial planning. This guide covers property taxes, income taxes, sales taxes, and other tax obligations.

## Property Taxes

### Anne Arundel County Property Tax

**County Tax Rate:**
- Based on assessed value of property
- Rates change annually
- Published each fiscal year
- Different rates for residential and commercial

**Assessment Process:**
- Property assessed annually
- Assessment based on market value
- Assessment notices sent annually
- Appeal process available

**Payment Schedule:**
- Two installments per year
- First installment due July 1
- Second installment due December 1
- Penalties for late payment

### Property Tax Rates

**Current Rates:**
- County portion: Varies annually
- State portion: Included in county rate
- School portion: Included in rate
- Total rate published annually

**How to Find Current Rate:**
- County website
- Assessment office
- Tax bill notice
- Annual publications

### Homestead Tax Credit

**What It Is:**
- Limits property tax increase on primary residence
- Protects homeowners from assessment increases
- Must apply annually
- Must be primary residence

**Benefits:**
- Tax savings
- Budget predictability
- Protection from rapid increases
- Apply each year

**Eligibility:**
- Must be primary residence
- Must own and occupy
- Application required annually
- Deadline for application

### Tax Appeals

**When to Appeal:**
- Assessment seems too high
- Property value decreased
- Assessment errors
- Compared to similar properties

**Process:**
- Receive assessment notice
- File appeal within timeframe
- Provide supporting evidence
- Hearings scheduled

**Supporting Evidence:**
- Recent appraisals
- Comparable sales
- Property condition documentation
- Errors in assessment

### Tax Payment Options

**Payment Methods:**
- Online payment
- Mail payment
- In-person payment
- Auto-pay available

**Online Payment:**
- County website
- Credit card or check
- Immediate confirmation
- Secure processing

### Tax Records

**Assessment Records:**
- Public records available
- Online access
- Property details
- Tax history

**Tax Bills:**
- Sent annually
- Payment instructions included
- Due dates clearly marked
- Records maintained

## Maryland Income Tax

### Maryland State Income Tax

**Tax Structure:**
- Progressive tax system
- Multiple tax brackets
- Filed annually
- Withholding from paychecks

**Tax Brackets:**
- State portion: 2-5.75%
- Local portion: 1.75-3.2%
- Combined rate varies by income
- Baltimore City has higher rates

**Filing Requirements:**
- File if income above threshold
- All Maryland income must be reported
- Resident vs non-resident rules
- Federal return required

### Maryland Tax Credits

**Available Credits:**
- Earned income credit
- Child tax credit
- Education credits
- Senior citizen credits
- Various other credits

**Eligibility:**
- Income requirements
- Filing status requirements
- Documentation required
- Application process

### Withholding

**Maryland Withholding:**
- State tax withheld from paychecks
- W-4 form for state
- Adjust based on situation
- Review annually

**Adjusting Withholding:**
- More withholding = refund
- Less withholding = more take-home pay
- Balance for cash flow
- Avoid underpayment penalties

## Sales Tax

### Maryland Sales Tax

**State Rate:**
- 6% state sales tax
- Applies to most goods and services
- Some exemptions exist

**Local Additions:**
- County may add local tax
- Anne Arundel: Additional tax
- Total rate varies by location
- Check current rate

**Total Anne Arundel Rate:**
- State: 6%
- County additional
- Total typically around 7-8%

### Sales Tax Exemptions

**Non-Taxable Items:**
- Groceries (food items)
- Prescription medications
- Medical devices
- Clothing (sometimes)
- Educational materials

**Taxable Items:**
- Most prepared food
- Clothing above threshold
- Electronics
- Services (varies)
- Luxury items

### Use Tax

**When Applicable:**
- Purchases from out-of-state
- No sales tax paid
- Use tax may apply
- Self-reported on income tax

**Examples:**
- Online purchases
- Out-of-state purchases
- Items brought into state
- Tax was not collected

## Other Taxes

### Inheritance Tax

**Maryland Inheritance Tax:**
- Tax on inherited assets
- Rates vary by relationship
- Exemptions for spouses
- File if required

**Exemptions:**
- Spouses exempt
- Lineal descendants (some exemption)
- Charitable bequests
- Specific exemptions

### Gift Tax

**Maryland Gift Tax:**
- Coordinated with federal
- Federal exemption applies
- Maryland follows federal
- Rarely affects most taxpayers

### Estate Tax

**Maryland Estate Tax:**
- Tax on estate value
- Exemption amount applies
- Filing required if over threshold
- Rate varies by estate size

**Exemption:**
- $5+ million exemption (recent changes)
- Amount adjusted periodically
- File if over threshold
- Federal coordination

## Tax Planning Tips

### Property Tax Planning

1. **Watch assessments** - Monitor annually
2. **Consider homestead** - Apply for credit
3. **Appeal if needed** - Don't accept errors
4. **Budget payments** - Plan for two installments
5. **Track improvements** - Affects assessment

### Income Tax Planning

1. **Withhold correctly** - Avoid penalties or over-withholding
2. **Track deductions** - Itemized vs standard
3. **Use credits** - Reduce tax liability
4. **Keep records** - Documentation essential
5. **Plan estimated payments** - If self-employed

### Sales Tax Planning

1. **Know exemptions** - Save on groceries, meds
2. **Compare purchases** - Out-of-state may have lower rates
3. **Track use tax** - Don't owe unexpectedly
4. **Business purchases** - Resale certificates
