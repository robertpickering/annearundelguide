**Anne Arundel County's Most Populous Community**

Glen Burnie is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. As the county's most populous area with approximately 72,000 residents, Glen Burnie serves as a major commercial and residential hub with excellent highway access, diverse neighborhoods, and convenient amenities.

## About Glen Burnie

### Community Overview

Glen Burnie is centrally located in Anne Arundel County with:
- **Direct I-95 access** via MD-295 and local roads
- **15-20 minutes** to Baltimore
- **30-40 minutes** to Washington, D.C.
- **10-15 minutes** to BWI Airport

The area features extensive shopping, dining, residential neighborhoods, and commercial development along Annapolis Road (MD-174).

**Google Maps:** [View Glen Burnie Location](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Glen+Burnie,+MD)

### History

Glen Burnie developed around the early 20th century, with significant growth post-World War II. The community was named after the Glen Burnie estate, and it has evolved from a rural area to one of Maryland's largest CDPs.

### Transportation Hub

Glen Burnie's location makes it a transportation hub:
- **I-95:** Major north-south corridor
- **MD-295 (Baltimore-Washington Parkway):** East-west connector
- **US-50:** Routes to Eastern Shore
- **BWI Airport:** 10-15 minutes away

This accessibility supports both residential living and commercial development.
