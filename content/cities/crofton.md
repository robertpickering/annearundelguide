**South Anne Arundel's Largest Community**

Crofton is a census-designated place (CDP) in Anne Arundel County, Maryland. As one of the largest and most populous communities in South County, Crofton serves as a major residential and commercial hub with diverse neighborhoods, excellent amenities, and a strong sense of community.

## About Crofton

### Community Overview

Crofton offers:
- **South County's largest commercial center**
- **20 minutes** to Annapolis
- **40-50 minutes** to Baltimore
- **50-60 minutes** to Washington, D.C.
- **20-25 minutes** to BWI Airport
- **Water access** to Chesapeake Bay (nearby)

The community is known for its master-planned neighborhoods, extensive shopping, and strong community amenities.

**Google Maps:** [View Crofton Location](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Crofton,+MD)

### History

Crofton developed as a planned community in the mid-20th century, with significant growth occurring from the 1960s through the 1990s. The area was named after the Crofton Estate, and it was developed with an emphasis on family-friendly neighborhoods, green spaces, and community facilities. The community grew organically into South County's primary residential and commercial hub.

### Master-Planned Community

Crofton features several distinct residential communities:
- **Crofton Village:** Original planned community center
- **Fox Hill:** Established neighborhoods
- **Glen Burnie/Crofton border:** Mixed residential/commercial
- **Waterfront areas:** Some properties with bay access
