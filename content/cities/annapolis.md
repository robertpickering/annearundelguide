**Maryland's Historic Capital & America's Sailing Capital**

Annapolis serves as the capital city of Maryland and is one of America's most historically significant communities. Founded as Maryland's capital in 1695, it served as the first capital of the United States from 1783 to 1784, where the Treaty of Paris was ratified and George Washington resigned his commission as Commander-in-Chief. With a population of 40,812 (2020 Census), Annapolis combines colonial heritage, maritime culture, and world-class dining in a walkable downtown setting.

## About Annapolis

### History & Heritage

Annapolis is one of the oldest planned cities in the United States. The city was designated Maryland's capital in 1695, and the Maryland State House—the oldest state capitol building in continuous legislative use—was completed in 1772.

From November 1783 to August 1784, Annapolis served as the temporary national capital under the Articles of Confederation. During this time, the Continental Congress ratified the Treaty of Paris (ending the Revolutionary War) and George Washington resigned his commission as Commander-in-Chief of the Continental Army.

The city's educational heritage dates to 1696 with the founding of King William's School, which became St. John's College in 1784—making it the third-oldest institution of higher education in the United States, after Harvard and William & Mary.

### Geography & Location

Annapolis sits on the Chesapeake Bay at the mouth of the Severn River, approximately:
- **25 miles south of Baltimore**
- **30 miles east of Washington, D.C.**
- **Part of the Baltimore-Washington metropolitan area**

This strategic waterfront location has made Annapolis a center for maritime trade, naval education, and water sports for over 300 years. The city is widely known as "America's Sailing Capital."

## Top Attractions

### U.S. Naval Academy

**Address:** 150 Blake Rd, Annapolis, MD 21402

Founded in 1845, the U.S. Naval Academy is America's premier military service academy, training future leaders for the U.S. Navy and Marine Corps. The 544-acre campus features historic buildings, museums, and waterfront locations along the Severn River.

- **Founded:** 1845
- **Admission:** Free for public tours (advance registration required)
- **Tours:** Daily guided tours available
- **Website:** [usna.edu](https://www.usna.edu/)

**Google Maps:** [U.S. Naval Academy](https://www.google.com/maps/place/U.S.+Naval+Academy,+150+Blake+Rd,+Annapolis,+MD+21402)

### Maryland State House

**Address:** 100 State Circle, Annapolis, MD 21401

The Maryland State House is the oldest state capitol building in continuous legislative use in the United States. Built in 1772, it houses the Maryland General Assembly and features the largest dome of any state capitol building, crafted from Virginia wood.

- **Built:** 1772
- **Tours:** Free self-guided and guided tours
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Website:** [mgaleg.maryland.gov](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/)

**Google Maps:** [Maryland State House](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Maryland+State+House,+100+State+Cir,+Annapolis,+MD+21401)

### Historic Downtown & Maryland Avenue

Maryland Avenue is the main commercial corridor of downtown Annapolis, featuring:
- Boutique shopping and antique stores
- Upscale restaurants and seafood houses
- Art galleries and historic sites
- Walkable colonial and Federal-era architecture

- **Parking:** Multiple parking garages and street parking available
- **Best Time to Visit:** Weekdays for a quieter experience

**Google Maps:** [Maryland Avenue, Annapolis](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Maryland+Avenue,+Annapolis,+MD)

### Annapolis Waterfront & Harbor

Known as "America's Sailing Capital," the Annapolis waterfront features:
- Marinas and yacht clubs
- Seafood restaurants with harbor views
- Harbor cruises and boat rentals
- Maritime museums and sailing schools
- Annual events: Annapolis to Bermuda Race, boat shows

- **Best Season:** May through October for water activities
- **Annual Events:** Sailing races, boat shows, festivals

**Google Maps:** [Annapolis Waterfront](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Annapolis+Waterfront,+Annapolis,+MD)

### St. John's College

**Address:** 400 Saint John's Lane, Annapolis, MD 21401

Founded in 1696 as King William's School and chartered in 1784, St. John's College is the third-oldest institution of higher education in the United States. Known for its unique Great Books curriculum, the college maintains a distinctive classical education approach.

- **Founded:** 1696 (chartered 1784)
- **Website:** [stjohns.edu](https://www.stjohns.edu/)

**Google Maps:** [St. John's College](https://www.google.com/maps/place/St.+John's+College,+400+Saint+John's+Ln,+Annapolis,+MD+21401)

### Annapolis Maritime History Center & Museum

Located at the historic City Dock, the maritime museum features exhibits on Annapolis' naval heritage, model ships, and interactive displays about Chesapeake Bay maritime culture.

- **Location:** City Dock, Annapolis, MD 21401
- **Website:** [visitannapolis.com](https://www.visitannapolis.com/)

**Google Maps:** [Annapolis Maritime History Center](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Annapolis+Maritime+History+Center,+Annapolis,+MD)

## Education

### Public Schools (AACPS)

Annapolis residents attend **Anne Arundel County Public Schools (AACPS)**, one of Maryland's largest school systems serving over 78,000 students.

**Annapolis Area Schools:**
- **Elementary:** Multiple AACPS elementary schools serve Annapolis neighborhoods
- **Middle School:** Severn School or George S. Smith Middle School (depending on zone)
- **High School:** Annapolis High School (approximately 450 students per graduating class)

**School assignments may change.** For current information, visit [AACPS official website](https://www.aacps.org) or contact the district directly.

### Higher Education

- **St. John's College:** Founded 1696, unique Great Books liberal arts curriculum
- **U.S. Naval Academy:** Federal service academy, undergraduate education for naval officers

### Private Schools

- **St. John's College:** Classical liberal arts education
- **Archbishop Spalding High School:** Catholic high school in nearby Severn
- **Annapolis Area Christian School:** Private Christian education

## Cost of Living

### Overall Assessment

Annapolis has a higher cost of living compared to national averages, driven primarily by housing costs. As Maryland's capital and a desirable waterfront city, real estate prices reflect the city's historical significance, maritime location, and proximity to Baltimore and Washington, D.C.

**Cost Breakdown:**
- **Housing:** Significantly above national average
- **Utilities:** Near national average
- **Transportation:** Below national average
- **Groceries:** Above average
- **Healthcare:** Above average

### Tax Information

- **State Income Tax:** Maryland (2% – 6.25% progressive)
- **Property Tax:** ~0.95% (among the state's lowest)
- **Sales Tax:** 6.45% (state + county combined)
